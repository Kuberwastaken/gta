GTA VI unlocks at **midnight local store time on Thursday, November 19, 2026**, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. These guides turn our sourced research into things you can act on: what to do this week, what to buy, how much space to clear, and what to know before you take your first car in [[world/vice-city|Vice City]].

Every guide follows the same rule as the rest of GTA VI Wire: claims are graded, and anything not from Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft carries a tag. Our [[about/how-we-grade|grading system]] explains the labels.

## What should I do before GTA 6 launches?

Four things: **buy it before Nov 20** (that keeps the Vintage Vice City Pack), **pre-load it** (open since Nov 10), **free about 250 GB** (Rockstar says up to 212 GB), and **let update 1.002 install**. The [[guides/gta-6-day-one-checklist|day-one checklist]] has the full list with dates.

## Do I need to know anything before I play?

Yes. GTA VI changes some GTA V habits. Previews report that police only respond to witnessed crimes, cash on hand is lost when you die, loot needs a fence, and you carry only two long guns. Our [[guides/gta-6-tips-before-you-start|tips before you start]] covers 25 of these, graded. Gameplay systems in depth live in the [[gameplay/index|gameplay section]].
