GTA VI unlocks at midnight local store time on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The whole US store runs on Eastern time, so Pacific players start at 9 PM on Nov 18. These guides cover what to do in the four days before that, and what changes from GTA V once you are driving around [[world/vice-city|Vice City]].

Claims that don't come from Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft carry a grade tag. Most gameplay detail here comes from previews (Edge, Red Bull, Engadget, VICE) and is graded reported. The labels are explained on [[about/how-we-grade|how we grade]].

## What should I do before GTA 6 launches?

Buy it before Nov 20 so you keep the Vintage Vice City Pack. Pre-load it: the download has been open since Nov 10. Free about 250 GB, against Rockstar's "up to 212 GB". Then let update 1.002.000 install (about 1.07 GB on PS5, 2.2 GB on Xbox). The [[guides/gta-6-day-one-checklist|day-one checklist]] puts these in order with the exact times.

## What changes from GTA V?

Police only respond to a witness, an alarm or a stolen-car tracker. Cash in your pocket is gone when you are Wasted or Busted, so bank it through the BuckMe app. Duffel-bag loot is worth nothing until a fence buys it. The weapon wheel is replaced by two handguns and two long guns, with the rest in your car. All of this is reported from previews.
