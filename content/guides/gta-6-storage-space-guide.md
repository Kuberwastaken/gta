**GTA VI needs up to 212 GB of free space**, Rockstar says, on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Series S alike. Rockstar gives no per-console split. Our advice is to **clear about 250 GB**, which covers the download, the install, update 1.002 and some headroom for whatever lands on Nov 19.

This page explains the numbers you will see on screen. For pre-load dates and how to start the download, see [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|pre-load and file size]]. More launch prep in the [[guides/index|guides hub]].

## How big is GTA 6 on each console?

| Console | Download shown | Notes | Grade |

| All consoles (Rockstar) | Up to 212 GB free space | "Depending on device" | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> |
| PS5 / PS5 Pro | ~209.8 GB (209.75 GB on first pre-load screens) | ~212 GB installed | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |

The PS5 figure comes from RockstarINTEL (credited to @MrDalekJD) via Timesaver. The Xbox figures come from Microsoft's display-catalog data, also via Timesaver.

## Why does Xbox say 225 GB?

Because there are two kinds of gigabyte. The Xbox package is **225,336,918,016 bytes**.

- Divide by 1,000,000,000 (decimal GB, which stores use) and you get **225.34 GB**.
- Divide by 1,073,741,824 (binary GiB, which consoles use) and you get about **209.86 GB**.

It is not a bigger download on Xbox. It is the same number of bytes, counted two ways. This is the most common confusion we see about GTA VI's size.

## What about the 230 GB German box?

Photos of the German PS5 box reportedly print **"230 GB"** as the minimum free space <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Rockstar has not explained the difference from its 212 GB figure. It may be a cautious print figure set before final builds, but that is our guess <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>. Either way, clearing 250 GB covers it.

## Will GTA 6 fit on a base PS5 or Series S?

Rockstar lists the same "up to 212 GB" for the base PS5 and the Series S, and both run the game natively. Whether it fits depends on what else you have installed, so check your free space in system settings now, not on launch night. If you need to delete games, the biggest ones go first; you can always re-download them.

Two things eat into your space after the first download:

- **Patches.** Update 1.002.000 started rolling out Nov 13: about **1.07 GB on PS5** and **2.18-2.2 GB on Xbox** <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Consoles often need temporary space to apply patches. See [[release/gta-6-day-one-patch|day-one patch]].
- **Captures.** If you plan to record clips of [[world/vice-city|Vice City]] for social media, those files add up fast.

Earlier guesses of a 15-50 GB day-one patch were never confirmed <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>.

## Do I need an SSD expansion?

Only if you can't free about 250 GB by deleting other games. Rockstar has not said whether GTA VI can be stored on or played from external USB drives, so we won't guess. If you do expand, use the console's approved internal-speed storage.

## Does the physical copy save space?

No. The box holds a **download code**, not a disc, so you download the full game either way. See [[release/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc|physical copy, no disc]].

## Does cloud gaming avoid the download?

On Xbox, yes. **Xbox Cloud Gaming** streams GTA VI at Series S settings from 1 AM ET on Nov 19, on Xbox One and Series X|S, with nothing to install. You still have to buy the game, and it is not on Game Pass. See [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|Series S and cloud gaming]]. PlayStation has no equivalent at launch, though Remote Play from your own console is supported <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## Quick checklist

- Check free space today.
- Clear about 250 GB.
- Start the pre-load (open since Nov 10) and leave the console in Rest Mode or Sleep with auto-downloads on.
- Confirm update 1.002 has installed by Nov 18.
