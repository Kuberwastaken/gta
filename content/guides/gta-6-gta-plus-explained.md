GTA+ is Rockstar's paid membership for GTA Online, and digital GTA VI pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox come with one month of it free. Rockstar now advertises optional benefits for Jason and Lucia in GTA VI. Single-player does not mean subscription extras are absent. Sony confirms that claiming the PS5 month starts a subscription that continues at the regular monthly price unless canceled. Check the price and next payment date on your platform rather than treating a rounded US price as universal. Other buying guides are in the [[guides/index|guides hub]].

## What GTA+ gives you

Rockstar's GTA+ page lists the membership on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> The perks, as reported: <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

| Perk | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly GTA$ deposit | Reportedly GTA$500,000 into GTA Online |
| Shark Card bonus | +15% GTA$ on Shark Card purchases |
| Game catalogue | Older Rockstar games, playable while subscribed |
| GTA Online access | Recently added on consoles |

The table above covers GTA Online and the games catalogue. It is not the complete membership benefit list now that GTA VI story-mode extras are advertised.

### Does it include GTA Online on PC?

Sources disagree. GameSpot says GTA Online access through GTA+ is console-only, while Rockstar's page lists PC benefits. PC members may get the perks without the online-access part, but we could not confirm that wording on Rockstar's page. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> GTA VI has no PC version, so this matters only for GTA V players.

## NBA 2K27 Vice City crossover: what GTA+ includes

**November 27 source check:** 2K's [Season 3 Courtside Report](https://nba.2k.com/2k27/courtside-report/season-3/) and [November 16 announcement](https://newsroom.2k.com/news/vice-city-comes-to-nbar-2k27-in-season-3) set out a limited GTA+ offer alongside NBA 2K27's Vice City-themed Season 3. This is content and access for **NBA 2K27**, not a GTA VI basketball mode, new GTA VI mission or announcement of GTA VI multiplayer. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (publisher announcement)

| Offer | Announced window | What it includes and excludes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GTA+ play access | November 27, 2026 to January 15, 2027, 11 AM ET | NBA 2K27 **Standard Edition**, including access to Season 3, for GTA+ members on **PS5 and Xbox Series X\|S**, at no additional cost. This is temporary membership access, not permanent ownership. |
| GTA+ purchase discount | The same November 27-January 15 window | **20% off Standard Edition** on the platform linked to the GTA+ membership. 2K says it cannot be combined with other offers or discounts. It is not a discount promise for every edition. |
| GTA+ Bonus Pack | December 17, 2026 to March 31, 2027, noon ET | A Vice City-styled blue Boat Hat and Oversized T-Shirt, 30 Skill Boosts (5 Games), and 15 Gatorade Boosts (5 Games). Requires NBA 2K27 access and an NBA 2K Account to redeem and use. It does not begin with the November play-access window. |
| Separately sold Vice City Edition | November 27, 2026 to January 15, 2027, 11 AM ET | A different digital edition with the Season 3 Pro Pass, Vice City T-Shirt and specified boosts. The GTA+ Standard access offer does not include this edition or its Pro Pass. |

For readers in India, **January 15 at 11 AM ET is January 15 at 9:30 PM IST**. The separate **March 31 noon ET** bonus redemption cutoff is also **9:30 PM IST on March 31**, because the US daylight-saving offset differs. 2K gives November 27 as the start date but does not specify an exact activation hour in these announcements. We have not tested entitlement on an account, so a local calendar date alone does not establish that a download button has appeared.

The Vice City Edition is announced for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC. **That broader sales-platform list does not expand the GTA+ access offer to PC or Switch 2.** 2K also says the Vice City Edition is unavailable in Belgium, Brazil and South Korea. Do not apply that edition-specific exclusion as a complete country list for every membership offer.

The two official pages distinguish the themed Season 3 environment and rewards from the paid Pro Pass and Hall of Fame Pass. Premium passes are separate purchases requiring the base game, internet and an NBA 2K account; their extra rewards are earned through gameplay. No claim here promises every themed reward immediately or without a paid pass. Some detailed premium reward levels differ between the two publisher pages, so this guide does not reproduce a combined level-by-level list.

**GTA+ still renews monthly.** At no additional cost within a paid membership is not an unconditional free copy. Check your platform's entitlement, price and next renewal date before claiming or buying. The temporary play window ends before the bonus-pack redemption deadline; a later pack deadline does not extend game access. No purchase or subscription change is needed to read this guide.

## How do you claim it?

On Xbox, open the Store, open your profile, choose Offers & credits and redeem the GTA+ offer. You have 180 days after placing your pre-order, it works once per platform account, and restricted child accounts can't use it. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Xbox Store)

[Sony's published instructions](https://www.playstation.com/support/games/gta-vi-offer-terms) confirm a **March 31, 2027** claim deadline. Buy digital Standard or Ultimate on PS Store on or before November 19, then follow the confirmation email and the GTA+ product-page offer using the **same account**. Complete the on-screen no-extra-cost claim, checking the next payment date before finishing. New, lapsed and existing subscribers qualify; existing subscribers can stack this promotional month. One claim per account, age and country restrictions apply, and the Ultimate upgrade alone is excluded. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Sony lists excluded PlayStation Store countries in the linked terms. Buying a boxed code is not the same as buying the digital edition directly from PS Store; do not assume the box qualifies.

## Does it auto-renew?

**On PS5, yes.** Sony says the membership continues monthly at the regular price after the promotional month until canceled. The claim screen gives the next payment date; use **Subscriptions Management** in account settings to cancel before that date if you do not want a paid month. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Xbox's store gives **180 days after placing the pre-order** to redeem its offer. Read the renewal terms displayed during the Xbox claim rather than assuming Sony's redemption deadline or a reported US monthly price applies to every platform and country. The free month is optional; buying GTA VI and claiming GTA+ are separate steps.

## What does GTA+ add to GTA VI story mode?

**November 19 correction:** our earlier statement that GTA+ did nothing in GTA VI was wrong. [Rockstar's live GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) now explicitly advertises optional in-game benefits for Jason and Lucia on console. That establishes that story-mode benefits exist. The detailed list below comes from [GameSpot's launch report](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-gta-subscription-includes-a-free-supercar-discounts-and-more/) and [GTA BOOM's hands-on guide](https://www.gtaboom.com/how-to-unlock-every-gta-benefit-in-gta-6-9a2d), not a fully recovered first-party terms page. See the [[about/corrections|corrections log]].

| Reported benefit | What the guides describe |
| --- | --- |
| Vehicle and garage | A '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy and a single-car garage in Crosstown, with a weapon locker and direct fencing for stolen goods. Progression-gated, not necessarily available at the beginning. |
| Transport | Premium Scooter Bros and RydeMe services, free scooter/bike rentals and free public transit. GameSpot also lists Seashark rentals. |
| Store discounts | 10% off at eligible businesses, including weapon and clothing shops, salons, tattooists and vehicle mod shops. |
| Vehicle sales | 10% extra on eligible vehicle sales. |

<span class="tag-reported">reported</span> GTA BOOM says its tested route is **Settings > Account > GTA+ Boosts**, where the boosts can be enabled. Check that the membership is active and that you are using the platform account holding it. These are optional extras, distinct from the [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|Ultimate Edition rewards]] and [[release/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses|Vintage Vice City Pack]]. There is no need to subscribe merely to play the purchased story.

### What happens when membership ends?

**Do not assume all rewards are permanent.** GameSpot says the benefits stop being available when membership expires. GTA BOOM instead says an already claimed car and garage remain, while ongoing discounts and services stop. The two guides also differ on whether the vehicle claim begins during or after its relevant chapter. We have not recovered readable first-party terms settling those points: the membership page returned only navigation in our direct check. We therefore do not promise permanent ownership, a precise chapter trigger, or a recovery method for an unclaimed reward. Read the actual in-game benefit and cancellation terms before paying on that assumption.

Canceling renewal and reaching the end of paid membership are different moments. Keep the platform's displayed next-payment date in view; the existing claim and cancellation instructions above still apply. No subscription is activated by reading this guide.

### Does this announce GTA VI multiplayer?

No. Story-mode membership benefits do not announce an online mode, a release date, character transfers or shared GTA$ balances. Those remain separate questions on [[release/gta-6-gta-online-status|GTA Online status]].

GTA Online on GTA V is not winding down. Take-Two shipped the Kortz Center Heist Summer Update on July 14, 2026, and says it will keep supporting GTA Online after GTA VI launches. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>
