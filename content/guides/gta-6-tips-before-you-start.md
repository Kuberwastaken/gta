## Save before you stop playing

**Do not assume every free-roam errand has been saved.** [Kotaku's November 19 testing](https://kotaku.com/be-careful-with-auto-saving-in-gta-6-it-doesnt-happen-as-often-as-you-think-2000758487) found autosaves after main story missions and larger side missions, but not after every smaller activity. Its guide reports no autosave during a mission. [TechRadar's launch-day advice](https://www.techradar.com/live/news/gta-6-launch-day-november-19) also warns against relying only on sporadic autosaves. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> These are the outlets' released-game observations, not our own tests or an exhaustive list of every autosave trigger.

Before quitting:

1. Finish the current mission and get safely into free roam. Kotaku reports that missions, combat, police pursuit and some movement states prevent manual saving.
2. Open **pause menu > Game > Save**, as described by Kotaku. Check the save slot and the displayed save details before choosing what to overwrite.
3. Confirm that the new save is present before closing the game. Keep an earlier manual save if you want a way back; do not overwrite your only fallback merely to test a save-loss report.

### How many save slots are available?

[GTA BOOM's own menu check](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-has-a-new-save-system-and-will-not-let-you-replay-story-missions-fa48) reports **15 manual slots and one overwriting autosave slot**. TechRadar independently lists 15 manual slots and says you cannot save during missions. Rotating manual slots gives you earlier points to return to, within that limit. It is not the same as an unlimited history or a backup against every crash or storage problem. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

### Can you replay a completed mission?

GTA BOOM reports **no mission-replay menu in the launch game**. Its practical alternative is loading a manual save made before the mission. Keep that limitation in mind before starting a long section, rather than assuming a GTA V-style replay list will let you revisit it later. The report is not an announcement of future replay support or New Game+.

### Is lost progress always an autosave mistake?

No. Kotaku separates its own autosave tests from online allegations that progress was lost even after a manual save. Those allegations do not establish how widespread a bug is, a single cause, or a verified recovery method. Manual saving reduces dependence on autosave; it is **not a guarantee against all reported save problems**. No fix or patch is promised here.

A suspended console session is not a completed save, and it should not be the only copy of progress you care about. Separately, published cheat guides report that activating a cheat disables saving for that session. Save before experimenting, not after; the plain [cheat-code table](/cheats) lists inputs without changing that restriction.

## Before playing: comfort and accessibility settings

[TechRadar's November 18 hands-on review](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/gta-6-review), based on a PS5 Pro playthrough, describes these options. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> This is a settings checklist, not a claim that we tested each option on every console or that every menu label is identical across builds.

- **Reading:** check subtitle and UI scale/readability before you begin.
- **Camera comfort:** review shake, bob, camera and control-mapping options. Change one thing at a time so you can tell what helps.
- **Visual clarity:** the reviewer describes high-contrast highlighting, reduced light brightness and a color option for the focus trail. These are reported options, not a guarantee of medical suitability or removal of all flashing content.
- **Audio comfort:** look for the reported tinnitus-reduction setting. It does not replace a comfortable listening level.
- **Input demands:** the review describes simplified controls, lower damage, auto driving and simpler mini games. Inspect the actual menu for the activity you need rather than assuming one toggle changes everything.

These are hands-on review findings, unlike the preview-sourced gameplay tips below. If frame pacing or responsiveness is your concern, settings cannot be assumed to create a 60 fps mode: see the [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|measured performance guide]].

## How does the wanted system work?

**November 20 correction:** we previously described Pay 'n' Spray as clearing the entire wanted level. [Red Bull's preview](https://www.redbull.com/us-en/gta-6-gameplay-details) describes the vehicle's wanted status, while [Engadget's launch guide](https://www.engadget.com/2320983/gta-6s-cops-will-remember-you-thanks-to-the-heat-system/) separates that clue from the others and from regional heat. We have narrowed the claim here; see the [[about/corrections|corrections log]].


It tracks what police know about you, not only what you did. The full system is on [[gameplay/gta-6-wanted-system-and-heat|wanted system and heat]].

1. Check for witnesses. A crime only draws police if there is a witness, an alarm or a stolen-car tracker.
2. Expect six stars, up from GTA V's five, per the Extended Look. A fan site's claim that GTA VI drops stars entirely is <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> by that same footage.
3. Read the star style before you run:

| Star style | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Solid | Police have visual contact |
| Flashing | They lost contact |
| Hollow / outlined | They don't know who the suspect is |
| Red | You are inside the search zone |

4. Shake your description. Heat tracks CCTV, your clothes and appearance, how many suspects there were, weapons and the vehicle. Split Jason and Lucia up, change clothes, get a barber haircut, wear a mask in front of CCTV, or swap or torch the car. A Pay 'n' Spray repairs the car and addresses its vehicle clue; other identifying details and regional heat can remain.
5. Ride the bus. Rockstar's Phil Hooker says police won't search one unless they saw you board it. Don't return to a shop you just robbed: police may hold a perimeter there. Helicopters carry searchlights, and police dogs appear.
6. Watch your record. Each character has a Criminal Profile running from Professional to Irredeemable, with angel and devil icons, and it feeds into the story. How much it changes is unknown; see [[gameplay/gta-6-criminal-profile|Criminal Profile]].

## How does money work?

There are three kinds: bank, cash and duffel-bag loot. Details on [[gameplay/gta-6-money-banks-and-fences|money, banks and fences]].

7. **Bank cash at ATMs before every job.** Cash is lost on Wasted or Busted; the BuckMe bank balance is safe.
8. Don't count loot as money. It has to be sold to a fence, and fences specialise and unlock through the story, so early hauls may sit unsold.
9. Rob almost anything, armored vans included. Safe codes can be found or bought. Blowing the safe is faster but can damage what's inside.
10. Keep a buffer. Some story beats require a minimum amount of cash.
11. Don't look for a Shark Card. Rockstar North co-head Rob Nelson says single-player has no real-money purchases. See [[gameplay/gta-6-gameplay-features-confirmed|confirmed gameplay features]].

## How do weapons work?

12. **Pick your loadout at the car.** You carry two handguns and two long guns, with one long gun visible on your back. The rest ride in the trunk, like RDR2's horse. More on [[guides/gta-6-controls-and-weapon-slots|controls and weapon slots]] and [[gameplay/gta-6-weapons|weapons]].
13. Holster in public. A visible gun changes NPC reactions without automatically drawing police, but a witness who sees you draw it can call it in.
14. Turn on auto-aim in settings if you want it; previews say it is off by default, and recoil is heavier than GTA V.
15. Use Focus, the slow-motion mode that highlights weak points. Vitals targeting disarms without killing. The Extended Look only showed Jason using Focus.
16. Loot police cars for gear.

## What changes with cars?

17. **Scan before you steal.** Cars have security tiers, and theft can mean hot-wiring, a key cloner, lock picks, a quick-time hijack, or smashing a window, which trips the alarm. Trackers alert police. The Waink app shows a car's security, tracker, registration cost and resale value. See [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|vehicles]].
18. Register what you keep. Stolen cars can be registered for a fee, and each character can keep several personal cars in garages.
19. Refuel. Hold the pump button for about 10 seconds. Engine health is tracked too.
20. Order a car through Scooter Bros for a fee when you are stranded. E-scooters are also in.

## What's on the phone?

21. Learn the five apps:

| App | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| BuckMe | Banking |
| Waink | Car security, tracker and value scan |
| Snapmatic | Social feed: follow accounts, watch NPC events and livestreams |
| RydeMe | Ride-hailing; passengers chat |
| Scooter Bros | Vehicle delivery |

Jason and Lucia can't post publicly on Snapmatic because they are fugitives, but they can DM photos to each other. An optional livestream feed opens with R3. LifeInvader and Bleeter are <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. See [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]].

22. Play radio on foot. Rockstar says radio and podcasts now play through the phone outside vehicles, a series first. Station list: [[music/gta-6-radio-stations|radio stations]].

## What about Jason, Lucia and the world?

23. **Don't plan on switching freely.** Rob Nelson told Famitsu switching is "restricted depending on the progression of the story". Red Bull said "at any time"; we go with Nelson. See [[gameplay/gta-6-character-switching|character switching]].
24. Spend time together. Partner activities build closeness, and Nelson says the romance can fade while the pair stay partners in crime. Whether it changes the ending is unconfirmed. See [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|Jason and Lucia's relationship]].
25. Eat, sleep and train. Gyms change physique, stamina and focus, and diet and sleep change weight. The map opens gradually. Rockstar confirmed tropical storms and seasonal hurricanes to Game Informer, plus over 170 animal species to log, from [[world/grassrivers|Grassrivers]] gators to flamingos. Some owners won't let you pet their dog.

Rockstar hasn't published a control layout, the full penalty for dying beyond lost cash, how fast heat fades, or how many fences exist. Short answers on the rest are in the [[guides/gta-6-faq|GTA 6 FAQ]].
