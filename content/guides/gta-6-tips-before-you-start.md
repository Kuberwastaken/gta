Five GTA V habits will get you killed or broke in GTA VI. Police need a witness before they come. Cash in your pocket is gone when you are Wasted or Busted. Stolen loot is worth nothing until a fence buys it. The weapon wheel is gone. Switching between Jason and Lucia is locked in parts of the story. Nearly every tip below comes from hands-on previews (Edge, Red Bull, Engadget, VICE) and the August Extended Look, so treat the details as <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> until the game unlocks on Nov 19.

Not set up yet? The [[guides/gta-6-day-one-checklist|day-one checklist]] comes first. Other launch guides are in the [[guides/index|guides hub]].

## How does the wanted system work?

It tracks what police know about you, not only what you did. The full system is on [[gameplay/gta-6-wanted-system-and-heat|wanted system and heat]].

1. Check for witnesses. A crime only draws police if there is a witness, an alarm or a stolen-car tracker.
2. Expect six stars, up from GTA V's five, per the Extended Look. A fan site's claim that GTA VI drops stars entirely is <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> by that same footage.
3. Read the star style before you run:

| Star style | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Solid | Police have visual contact |
| Flashing | They lost contact |
| Hollow / outlined | They don't know who the suspect is |
| Red | You are inside the search zone |

4. Shake your description. Heat tracks CCTV, your clothes and appearance, how many suspects there were, weapons and the vehicle. Split Jason and Lucia up, change clothes, get a barber haircut, wear a mask in front of CCTV, or swap or torch the car. A Pay 'n' Spray repairs the car and clears the wanted level.
5. Ride the bus. Rockstar's Phil Hooker says police won't search one unless they saw you board it. Don't return to a shop you just robbed: police may hold a perimeter there. Helicopters carry searchlights, and police dogs appear.
6. Watch your record. Each character has a Criminal Profile running from Professional to Irredeemable, with angel and devil icons, and it feeds into the story. How much it changes is unknown; see [[gameplay/gta-6-criminal-profile|Criminal Profile]].

## How does money work?

There are three kinds: bank, cash and duffel-bag loot. Details on [[gameplay/gta-6-money-banks-and-fences|money, banks and fences]].

7. **Bank cash at ATMs before every job.** Cash is lost on Wasted or Busted; the BuckMe bank balance is safe.
8. Don't count loot as money. It has to be sold to a fence, and fences specialise and unlock through the story, so early hauls may sit unsold.
9. Rob almost anything, armored vans included. Safe codes can be found or bought. Blowing the safe is faster but can damage what's inside.
10. Keep a buffer. Some story beats require a minimum amount of cash.
11. Don't look for a Shark Card. Rockstar North co-head Rob Nelson says single-player has no real-money purchases. See [[gameplay/gta-6-gameplay-features-confirmed|confirmed gameplay features]].

## How do weapons work?

12. **Pick your loadout at the car.** You carry two handguns and two long guns, with one long gun visible on your back. The rest ride in the trunk, like RDR2's horse. More on [[guides/gta-6-controls-and-weapon-slots|controls and weapon slots]] and [[gameplay/gta-6-weapons|weapons]].
13. Holster in public. A visible gun changes NPC reactions without automatically drawing police, but a witness who sees you draw it can call it in.
14. Turn on auto-aim in settings if you want it; previews say it is off by default, and recoil is heavier than GTA V.
15. Use Focus, the slow-motion mode that highlights weak points. Vitals targeting disarms without killing. The Extended Look only showed Jason using Focus.
16. Loot police cars for gear.

## What changes with cars?

17. **Scan before you steal.** Cars have security tiers, and theft can mean hot-wiring, a key cloner, lock picks, a quick-time hijack, or smashing a window, which trips the alarm. Trackers alert police. The Waink app shows a car's security, tracker, registration cost and resale value. See [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|vehicles]].
18. Register what you keep. Stolen cars can be registered for a fee, and each character can keep several personal cars in garages.
19. Refuel. Hold the pump button for about 10 seconds. Engine health is tracked too.
20. Order a car through Scooter Bros for a fee when you are stranded. E-scooters are also in.

## What's on the phone?

21. Learn the five apps:

| App | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| BuckMe | Banking |
| Waink | Car security, tracker and value scan |
| Snapmatic | Social feed: follow accounts, watch NPC events and livestreams |
| RydeMe | Ride-hailing; passengers chat |
| Scooter Bros | Vehicle delivery |

Jason and Lucia can't post publicly on Snapmatic because they are fugitives, but they can DM photos to each other. An optional livestream feed opens with R3. LifeInvader and Bleeter are <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. See [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]].

22. Play radio on foot. Rockstar says radio and podcasts now play through the phone outside vehicles, a series first. Station list: [[music/gta-6-radio-stations|radio stations]].

## What about Jason, Lucia and the world?

23. **Don't plan on switching freely.** Rob Nelson told Famitsu switching is "restricted depending on the progression of the story". Red Bull said "at any time"; we go with Nelson. See [[gameplay/gta-6-character-switching|character switching]].
24. Spend time together. Partner activities build closeness, and Nelson says the romance can fade while the pair stay partners in crime. Whether it changes the ending is unconfirmed. See [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|Jason and Lucia's relationship]].
25. Eat, sleep and train. Gyms change physique, stamina and focus, and diet and sleep change weight. The map opens gradually. Rockstar confirmed tropical storms and seasonal hurricanes to Game Informer, plus over 170 animal species to log, from [[world/grassrivers|Grassrivers]] gators to flamingos. Some owners won't let you pet their dog.

Rockstar hasn't published a control layout, the full penalty for dying beyond lost cash, how fast heat fades, or how many fences exist. Short answers on the rest are in the [[guides/gta-6-faq|GTA 6 FAQ]].
