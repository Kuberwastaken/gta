## Release date and unlock time

### When does GTA 6 come out?

**November 19, 2026.** Rockstar set the date on Nov 6, 2025, after delaying from May 26, 2026, and Take-Two re-confirmed it on Nov 9, 2026, saying the launch is "ten days away". History on [[release/gta-6-release-date|release date]] and [[timeline/gta-6-delays|delays]].

### What time does GTA 6 unlock?

Rockstar says **midnight local time on Nov 19 in all regions**. "Local" means your store region's midnight. The whole US store opens at **midnight ET**, so Central players start at 11 PM, Mountain at 10 PM and Pacific at **9 PM on Nov 18** <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Every region: [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|release time by country]].

### What time does GTA 6 unlock in the UK?

**Midnight GMT on Nov 19** (00:00 UTC) <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

### What time does GTA 6 unlock in India and Australia?

India unlocks at **midnight IST on Nov 19**, which is 18:30 UTC Nov 18. Sydney unlocks at **midnight AEDT**, 13:00 UTC Nov 18. Perth follows the Sydney timestamp, so it opens at **9 PM on Nov 18** per KeenGamer <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

### Who gets GTA 6 first?

**New Zealand**, at midnight NZDT (11:00 UTC on Nov 18). That is about 18 hours before the US East Coast.

### Can I play early with a New Zealand account or a VPN?

A VPN or a changed console clock alone does not work. On PS5, early access reportedly needs a New Zealand account and a New Zealand payment method, and it may break store terms <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Sources disagree about Xbox. We don't recommend it.

### Is there a GTA 6 review embargo?

Reportedly **Wednesday, Nov 18**, per NateTheHate and Insider Gaming <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. A "6-7 AM EST" time is <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Rockstar has not confirmed either. See [[release/gta-6-reviews-and-review-embargo|reviews and embargo]].

## Pre-load, size and patches

### When does GTA 6 pre-load start?

**It already has: Nov 10.** PS5 opened at midnight local time; Xbox at 12 a.m. GMT worldwide (7 PM ET Nov 9). Rockstar originally said Nov 12, so older articles are out of date. See [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|pre-load and file size]].

### How big is GTA 6?

Rockstar says **up to 212 GB** free space. PS5 downloads about 209.8 GB; Xbox about 209.86 GB <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Clear about 250 GB to be safe. See the [[guides/gta-6-storage-space-guide|storage space guide]].

### Why does the Xbox store say 225 GB?

It is the same file in different units. The package is 225,336,918,016 bytes: **225.34 GB** in decimal, about **209.86 GB** in the binary units consoles use <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

### Is there a day-one patch?

**Update 1.002.000** began rolling out on Nov 13: about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.2 GB on Xbox <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar has published no notes and hasn't called it a day-one patch. Earlier 15-50 GB patch claims were <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. See [[release/gta-6-day-one-patch|day-one patch]].

## Price and editions

### How much does GTA 6 cost?

**$79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate** in the US. In the UK it is £69.99 / £89.99, and €79.99 / €99.99 in the eurozone <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The $100 base-price prediction was <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>. Every region: [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]].

### Is the Ultimate Edition worth it?

It adds 16 items, including the '95 Grotti Cheetah and five businesses, unlocked across the story <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. You can upgrade later for the price gap. See [[guides/gta-6-which-edition-should-you-buy|which edition should you buy]].

### What is the pre-order bonus?

The **Vintage Vice City Pack**: a two-tone '55 Vapid Stanier, the Shore Court Garage, 1980s outfits and hairstyles, and a tropical weapon skin. It goes to all purchases before Nov 20, so launch-day buyers still get it. See [[release/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses|pre-order bonuses]].

### Is there a GTA 6 disc?

**No.** The boxed copy is a download code. Older "disc confirmed" stories were a misreading. Take-Two's Strauss Zelnick says discs "don't really make sense for the consumer" but hasn't ruled one out. See [[release/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc|physical copy, no disc]].

### My box code says it is already used. What now?

Some buyers report used or missing codes <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar has announced no specific remedy. Contact the retailer first, and redeem early rather than at midnight.

### Does the Collector's Box include the game?

**No.** "The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection" is $399.99 of merchandise, game sold separately, Rockstar Store only. See [[release/gta-6-collectors-box|Collector's Box]].

### Is there a GTA 6 PS5 bundle?

No bundle has been announced. Sony has two limited GTA VI DualSense controllers ($84.99) and a Japan-only Midnight Black PS5 with no game.

## Platforms and performance

### Does GTA 6 run at 60 fps?

**No.** Rockstar targets **30 fps** on every console, PS5 Pro included, with no performance toggle. Fan-site 60 fps tables are wrong. See [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|PS5 Pro performance]].

### Is GTA 6 better on PS5 Pro?

Yes, a little. Pro adds PSSR upscaling and **ray-traced shadows**, which Rockstar says are Pro-only. The frame rate is still 30 fps.

### Does GTA 6 work on Xbox Series S?

Yes, natively at 30 fps. Its resolution is undisclosed, and figures like "720p-900p" are guesses <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. See [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|Series S and cloud]].

### Can I play GTA 6 on Xbox One or PS4?

**Xbox One: only through Xbox Cloud Gaming**, from 1 AM ET Nov 19, if you buy the game. **PS4: no.**

### Is GTA 6 on Game Pass?

**No.** You must buy it. Cloud playtime can be bought without Game Pass in supported markets.

### When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not announced. GamesBeat reported a **Q1 2027** target from four unnamed sources <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. There is no PC streaming via Xbox Cloud at launch either. See [[release/gta-6-pc-release-date|PC release date]] and the [[leaks/gta-6-pc-at-launch-rumour|PC-at-launch rumour]].

### Is GTA 6 on Switch 2?

Not announced. Nintendo told people to ask Rockstar. NateTheHate said Rockstar ran tests, but "tests don't always equal release" <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. See [[release/gta-6-switch-2|Switch 2]].

## Online, GTA+ and money

### Does GTA 6 have online multiplayer?

**Not at launch.** Rockstar calls it "a single player experience", and the ESRB rating has no "Users Interact" label. No online mode, name or date has been announced. See [[release/gta-6-gta-online-status|GTA Online status]].

### Will GTA Online shut down?

No. Take-Two says it will keep supporting GTA Online on GTA V after GTA VI launches.

### Does GTA 6 have microtransactions?

Rob Nelson of Rockstar North says single-player has **no real-money purchases** <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The ESRB "In-Game Purchases" label most likely reflects the Ultimate upgrade and GTA+ <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>.

### What is the free GTA+ month?

Digital pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox include one month of GTA+. It reportedly auto-renews at about $8 a month unless you cancel <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[guides/gta-6-gta-plus-explained|GTA+ explained]].

## Story, map and rating

### How long is GTA 6?

Rockstar has given no official length. Rob Nelson's own playthrough reportedly took **about 80 hours**, but outlets disagree on whether that includes side content <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[guides/gta-6-how-long-to-beat|how long to beat]].

### How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar told Edge the landmass is "nearly as large as" GTA IV, GTA V and RDR2 combined. Rockstar has published no square-kilometre figure. See [[world/gta-6-map-size|map size]].

### Who are the main characters?

**[[characters/jason-duval|Jason Duval]]** and **[[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia Caminos]]**, a criminal couple in the state of Leonida. A four-protagonist idea was considered and dropped.

### Is there a GTA 6 map I can print?

Rockstar is putting a **free printable Leonida map** on rockstargames.com on Nov 19, with a $0.99 physical version to follow <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The box has no paper map. See [[guides/gta-6-printable-map|printable map]].

### What is GTA 6 rated?

**ESRB M (Mature 17+)** and **PEGI 18**. It is R18+ in Australia, CERO Z in Japan and 19+ in South Korea. See [[release/gta-6-age-rating|age rating]].
