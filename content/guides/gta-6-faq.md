## Release date and unlock time

### When does GTA 6 come out?

November 19, 2026. Rockstar set that date on Nov 6, 2025, moving it from May 26, 2026, and Take-Two's Nov 9, 2026 earnings release says the launch is "ten days away". History: [[release/gta-6-release-date|release date]] and [[timeline/gta-6-delays|delays]].

### What time does GTA 6 unlock?

Rockstar says "midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions", meaning your store region's midnight. The US store runs on Eastern time, so Central starts at 11 PM, Mountain at 10 PM and Pacific at 9 PM on Nov 18 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Every region: [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|release time by country]].

### What time does it unlock in the UK, India and Australia?

UK: 00:00 GMT on Nov 19. India: midnight IST, which is 18:30 UTC Nov 18. Sydney: midnight AEDT, 13:00 UTC Nov 18; KeenGamer lists Perth at 9 PM Nov 18 because it shares the Sydney timestamp <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

### Who gets GTA 6 first?

New Zealand, at 11:00 UTC on Nov 18, about 18 hours before the US East Coast at 05:00 UTC Nov 19.

### Can I play early with a New Zealand account or a VPN?

A VPN or a changed console clock does nothing on its own. On PS5 it reportedly takes a New Zealand account plus a New Zealand payment method, may breach store terms, and sources disagree on whether Xbox works at all <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

### When is the review embargo?

Wednesday, Nov 18, per NateTheHate and Insider Gaming <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>; the "6-7 AM EST" time is <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Rockstar has confirmed neither. See [[release/gta-6-reviews-and-review-embargo|reviews and embargo]].

## Pre-load, size and patches

### When does pre-load start?

It opened Nov 10: midnight local on PS5, 12 a.m. GMT worldwide on Xbox (7 PM ET Nov 9). Articles saying Nov 12 predate Rockstar's Nov 3 change. See [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|pre-load and file size]].

### How big is GTA 6?

Rockstar asks for up to 212 GB free. The PS5 download is about 209.8 GB and the Xbox one about 209.86 GB <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>; clear 250 GB to leave patch room. See the [[guides/gta-6-storage-space-guide|storage space guide]].

### Why does the Xbox store say 225 GB?

Units. The package is 225,336,918,016 bytes, which is 225.34 GB in decimal and about 209.86 GB in the binary units the console displays <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

### Is there a day-one patch?

Update 1.002.000 started rolling out Nov 13 at about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.2 GB on Xbox, with no patch notes <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The 15-50 GB patch claims from earlier were <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. See [[release/gta-6-day-one-patch|day-one patch]].

## Price and editions

### How much does GTA 6 cost?

$79.99 Standard and $99.99 Ultimate in the US; £69.99 / £89.99 in the UK and €79.99 / €99.99 in the eurozone <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The $100 base-price prediction was <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>. All regions: [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]].

### Is the Ultimate Edition worth it?

It adds 16 items, including the '95 Grotti Cheetah and five businesses, unlocked across the story's chapters rather than on day one <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. You can upgrade later for the price gap ($20 on Xbox). See [[guides/gta-6-which-edition-should-you-buy|which edition should you buy]].

### What is the pre-order bonus?

The Vintage Vice City Pack: a two-tone '55 Vapid Stanier, the Shore Court Garage, 1980s outfits and hairstyles, and a tropical weapon skin. Any purchase before Nov 20 qualifies, launch day included. See [[release/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses|pre-order bonuses]].

### Is there a GTA 6 disc?

No. The box holds a download code; older "disc confirmed" stories were a misreading. Strauss Zelnick says discs "don't really make sense for the consumer" but hasn't ruled one out. See [[release/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc|physical copy, no disc]].

### My box code says it's already used. What now?

Go back to the retailer. Some Best Buy and Rockstar Store buyers report used or missing codes <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>, and Rockstar has announced no remedy, so redeem days before launch, not at midnight.

### Does the Collector's Box include the game?

No. "The Goodtime State, Vice City Collection" is $399.99 of merchandise, sold only on the Rockstar Store, one per account. See [[release/gta-6-collectors-box|Collector's Box]].

### Is there a GTA 6 PS5 bundle?

None has been announced. Sony sells two limited GTA VI DualSense controllers at $84.99 and a Japan-only Midnight Black PS5 that ships without the game.

## Platforms and performance

### Does GTA 6 run at 60 fps?

No. Rockstar targets 30 fps on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S, with no performance toggle; fan-site 60 fps tables are wrong. See [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|PS5 Pro performance]].

### Is it better on PS5 Pro?

Slightly. Pro adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows, which Rockstar says are Pro-only, at the same 30 fps.

### Does it work on Xbox Series S?

Yes, natively at 30 fps. Resolution is undisclosed, and "720p-900p" figures are guesses <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. See [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|Series S and cloud]].

### Can I play on Xbox One or PS4?

Xbox One only through Xbox Cloud Gaming, from 1 AM ET Nov 19, and you still have to buy the game. PS4 has no version.

### Is GTA 6 on Game Pass?

No. Since Nov 11, cloud playtime can be bought without Game Pass in supported markets, but the game itself is a purchase.

### When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Unannounced. GamesBeat reported a Q1 2027 target from four unnamed sources <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>, and Xbox Cloud won't stream it to PC at launch. See [[release/gta-6-pc-release-date|PC release date]] and the [[leaks/gta-6-pc-at-launch-rumour|PC-at-launch rumour]].

### Is GTA 6 on Switch 2?

Not announced. Nintendo of America's Devon Pritchard told people to ask Rockstar; NateTheHate says Rockstar ran tests, but "tests don't always equal release" <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. See [[release/gta-6-switch-2|Switch 2]].

## Online, GTA+ and money

### Does GTA 6 have online multiplayer?

Not at launch. Rockstar calls it "a single player experience", and the ESRB listing carries no "Users Interact" label. See [[release/gta-6-gta-online-status|GTA Online status]].

### Will GTA Online shut down?

No. Take-Two says it will keep supporting GTA Online on GTA V after GTA VI launches; the Kortz Center Heist update shipped July 14, 2026.

### Does GTA 6 have microtransactions?

Rob Nelson of Rockstar North told Kinda Funny single-player has no real-money purchases <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The ESRB "In-Game Purchases" label most likely covers the Ultimate upgrade and GTA+ <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>.

### What is the free GTA+ month?

Digital pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox include one month, which reportedly renews at about $8 a month unless cancelled <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[guides/gta-6-gta-plus-explained|GTA+ explained]].

## Story, map and rating

### How long is GTA 6?

No official length. Rob Nelson's own run reportedly took about 80 hours with optional content <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[guides/gta-6-how-long-to-beat|how long to beat]].

### How big is the map?

Rockstar told Edge the landmass is "nearly as large as" GTA IV, GTA V and RDR2 combined, and has given no square-kilometre figure. See [[world/gta-6-map-size|map size]].

### Who are the main characters?

[[characters/jason-duval|Jason Duval]] and [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia Caminos]], a criminal couple in the state of Leonida. Rockstar considered four protagonists and dropped the idea.

### Is there a map I can print?

The box has none. Rockstar's [official media page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media) now offers the printable Leonida map and cover-art poster <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. The map reveals locations; it is linked rather than embedded in our guide. See [[guides/gta-6-printable-map|printable map]].

### What is GTA 6 rated?

ESRB M (Mature 17+) and PEGI 18; R18+ in Australia, CERO Z in Japan and 19+ in South Korea. See [[release/gta-6-age-rating|age rating]].

## Safety

### Are GTA 6 PC downloads real?

No. There is no PC version, and fake "GTA 6 PC download" files and crypto wallet drainers are circulating <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

Mute keywords from 11:00 UTC on Nov 18, when New Zealand unlocks and reviews reportedly drop. The CyberLeek footage from August and October contains major story spoilers; our [[leaks/index|leaks section]] covers it without the plot.
