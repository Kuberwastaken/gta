Rockstar has not confirmed that Tommy Vercetti appears in GTA 6, so a return or cameo is <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. What is <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> is that Rockstar keeps name-checking him in edition and pre-order copy. References are not an appearance, and that gap is the whole story here.

## Where the rumour came from

Fans have expected a nod to the hero of 2002's GTA Vice City since 2022, when Bloomberg reported a fictional Miami setting. GTA 6 is set in a modern [[world/vice-city|Vice City]], and Trailer 1 confirmed it in December 2023. Rockstar's own marketing then gave the theory something to hold on to in 2026.

## Evidence for: the confirmed references

The [GTA VI site](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) describes the Ultimate Edition's Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers as "sourced from the Vercetti Estate". The Vintage Vice City Pack includes a tropical weapon pattern "inspired by Tommy Vercetti's iconic palm tree button-up" and "Vice City stylings sourced from the Vercetti Estate". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> Famitsu noted that Tommy's name appears in the pack description, per [GTAVice.net](https://www.gtavice.net/news/gta-6-famitsus-full-rob-nelson-interview-translated-into-english). Trailer 1 also shows the Ocean View Hotel, Tommy's base in the original, and names Shore Drive, [Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/games/the-gta-6-trailers-best-easter-eggs-references-and-hidden-details/) spotted. The pack and edition contents are on [[release/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses|pre-order bonuses]] and [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]].

## Evidence against an appearance

No official video has shown him: not Trailer 1, Trailer 2, the 27-minute Extended Look or the October 15 Launch Trailer (all on [[media/index|trailers and media]]). Rockstar's character page lists eight bio entries and none is Tommy.

Rockstar's framing points to Easter eggs. Rob Nelson told Famitsu, "The universes are separate, but we like looking back at previous titles in slightly clever ways." Our reading is that this describes callbacks, not a returning lead. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> The word "Estate" is also loaded, since an estate is often what someone leaves behind. It could hint that Tommy is gone in-universe, or it could be a brand name on items. That reading is ours, not Rockstar's. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> [whengta6.com](https://www.whengta6.com/guides/will-tommy-vercetti-be-in-gta-6) lands on the same verdict: referenced, not confirmed.

## Other returning characters

None are confirmed. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Fans nicknamed an Ammu-Nation owner in Trailer 2 "Phil", but [GTA Intel](https://gtaintel.com/news/gta-6-confirmed-characters-encyclopedia) flags the Phil Cassidy link as unsourced to Rockstar. Cameos by Michael, Franklin, Trevor or Lamar are fan-site claims. "Val" as Valentina from GTA Online is a fan ID that one site overstates as confirmed.

The confirmed callbacks are smaller. The Extended Look references Blaine County and Los Santos, borrows GTA IV-style stranger encounters and a San Andreas bench-press minigame, and Trailer 2 has Jason in a V-Rock T-shirt.
