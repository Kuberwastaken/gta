No. GTA 6 does not launch on PC. It releases on **November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only**, and Rockstar has announced no PC version, store page or system requirements. The claim that it would launch on PC is <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>.

What is live is a separate claim: a PC version in **Q1 2027**. That is <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> by GamesBeat from anonymous sources, and it is still open. Our evergreen [[release/gta-6-pc-release-date|PC release date page]] tracks the date; this page grades the rumour.

## The claim

Two versions of the PC rumour have circulated:

1. **"GTA 6 will be on PC at launch."** This was fan hope for years, plus a steady stream of fake "PC download" sites.
2. **"GTA 6 is coming to PC in Q1 2027."** GamesBeat reported this on Oct 13, 2026, citing four anonymous sources.

## Where did it come from?

Rockstar's history is the main driver. GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both reached PC after their console launches, so fans expected either a day-one PC version this time or a short gap.

The Q1 2027 report came from GamesBeat, as relayed by [PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/3260660/we-guessed-late-2027-for-gta-6-on-pc-a-report-says-q1-2027.html) on Oct 14, 2026. PCWorld had itself guessed late 2027. Rockstar did not respond to the report.

## Evidence against PC at launch

- **Trailer 1 (Dec 2023)** listed PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no PC, [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/gta-6-trailer-now-available-leak-forced-rockstar-games-to-release/z36508) reported. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>
- **The June 2026 price reveal** named the same two platforms at $79.99 and $99.99. See [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]]. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>
- **Rockstar's GTA VI site** lists PS5 and Xbox Series X|S and says "Plays Best On PS5". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>
- **Xbox Wire, Nov 10, 2026:** Xbox Cloud Gaming will run GTA 6 on Xbox One and Series X|S consoles from 1 AM ET on Nov 19. PC and browser streaming are not offered at launch, [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-confirms-xbox-cloud-gaming-support-but-pc-players-face-a-catch-3427015/) reported. More on [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|Series S and cloud gaming]].

## Evidence for a later PC version

- **Take-Two keeps talking about PC.** On the Aug 7, 2026 earnings call, Strauss Zelnick said PC can now be "40% or 50%" of sales for multiplatform titles, per [The Motley Fool's transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/14/take-two-ttwo-q1-2027-earnings-call-transcript/). He did not announce a PC version. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (quote)
- **Zelnick has deferred to Rockstar.** He said any disc or PC news would come from Rockstar, [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-disc-and-pc-version-update-come-rockstar-take-two/) reported. That is not a denial.
- **The GamesBeat report** is specific (a quarter) and multi-sourced (four people). <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## Is the GTA 6 PC download real?

No. There is no PC build to download. Fake installers and crypto wallet drainers are spreading around the launch, so treat any "GTA 6 PC" file as malware. Our [[guides/gta-6-faq|GTA 6 FAQ]] covers other launch scams.

## What would change this grade?

- **Upgrade to confirmed:** a Rockstar Newswire post, a Steam or Rockstar Games Launcher store page, or Take-Two naming a PC date on an earnings call. Take-Two's next scheduled results come in early 2027.
- **Downgrade the Q1 2027 report:** a Rockstar or Take-Two statement giving a different window, or Q1 2027 passing with no PC version.
