GTA 6 does not launch on PC. It releases on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and the idea that it would arrive on PC the same day is <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>. A separate claim, a PC version in Q1 2027, is <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> by GamesBeat and still open. This page grades the rumour; the [[release/gta-6-pc-release-date|PC release date page]] tracks the date itself.

## Where the PC rumour came from

The day-one version was fan hope plus precedent working backwards. GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both reached PC after their console launches, and every year a fresh batch of fake "PC download" sites kept the idea in search results.

The Q1 2027 version has a real origin. GamesBeat reported it on October 13, 2026, citing four anonymous sources, and [PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/3260660/we-guessed-late-2027-for-gta-6-on-pc-a-report-says-q1-2027.html) relayed it the next day. PCWorld had itself guessed late 2027. Rockstar did not respond.

## Evidence against PC at launch

No first-party platform list has ever included PC. Trailer 1 in December 2023 named PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/gta-6-trailer-now-available-leak-forced-rockstar-games-to-release/z36508) reported. The June 24, 2026 price reveal named the same two platforms at $79.99 and $99.99 (see [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]]). Rockstar's [GTA VI site](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) still lists only consoles and carries a "Plays Best On PS5" badge. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Cloud streaming does not get PC players in either. [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/) said on November 10 that Xbox Cloud Gaming will run GTA 6 from 1 AM ET on November 19, but only on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles, at Series S settings. PC and browser streaming are excluded at launch, [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-confirms-xbox-cloud-gaming-support-but-pc-players-face-a-catch-3427015/) reported. Details on [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|Series S and cloud gaming]].

## Evidence for a later PC version

Take-Two keeps raising PC without announcing anything. On the August 7, 2026 earnings call Strauss Zelnick said PC can now be "40% or 50%" of sales for multiplatform titles, per [The Motley Fool's transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/14/take-two-ttwo-q1-2027-earnings-call-transcript/). <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (quote) He has also said any disc or PC news would come from Rockstar, [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-disc-and-pc-version-update-come-rockstar-take-two/) reported. That is a deferral, not a denial.

The GamesBeat report is specific (a quarter) and has four sources. That is why we grade it reported rather than rumour, and why we do not go further until Rockstar speaks.

## Is the GTA 6 PC download real?

No. There is no PC build to leak or download. Fake installers and crypto wallet drainers are circulating around launch, so treat any "GTA 6 PC" file as malware. The [[guides/gta-6-faq|GTA 6 FAQ]] covers other launch scams.

## What would flip it

A Rockstar Newswire post, a Steam or Rockstar Games Launcher store page, or a PC date named on a Take-Two earnings call would move the Q1 2027 row to confirmed. A first-party statement giving a different window, or March 2027 ending with no PC release, would downgrade it.
