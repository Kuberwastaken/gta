GTA 6's budget has never been disclosed. Rockstar has denied that it exceeded $1.5 billion without giving a number, which makes the long-running $2 billion rumour <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>. Analysts put it above $1 billion <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Anything more precise is a guess.

## Where the $2 billion figure came from

No document and no named source. Wikipedia describes "$1-2B" as a long-running rumour that circulated from 2023 to 2025, usually in "most expensive game ever" headlines. Analysts then layered their own estimates on top. The most shared, a [GamesRadar+ piece](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/analyst-says-i-expect-a-usd100-price-point-for-gta-6-estimates-rockstars-development-cost-is-over-usd1-5-billion-and-predicts-it-will-earn-usd10-billion-lifetime-and-another-usd500-million-annually-from-gta-online), put development cost above $1.5 billion in the same breath as a $100 price prediction that also missed (see [[leaks/gta-6-100-dollar-price-rumour|the $100 price rumour]]).

## What Rockstar said

In a Bloomberg feature around October 2026, Kolbe denied the budget topped $1.5 billion and declined to give a figure, per [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/rockstar-still-wont-reveal-cost-152304936.html). It is the only on-record Rockstar statement about cost, and we saw it through that write-up, not the Bloomberg piece itself.

The analysts have not caught up. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-launch.html) on November 14 still cited Pachter at over $1.5 billion next to Roth's Handler at over $1 billion. Pachter's number contradicts the first-party denial, so we drop it. That leaves $1 billion to $1.5 billion as the defensible range. The range is our inference. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

## Why the number is hard to pin down

Take-Two does not break out per-game costs; the [November 9 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026072175/ttwo2q27earningsrelease.htm) reports company-wide figures only. Development also ran for more than a decade. Pre-production on "Project Americas" began around 2014-15, early development after Red Dead Redemption 2 in late 2018, and principal production in 2020, while Rockstar's headcount grew from about 2,441 to 4,711 across 12 offices, per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI). <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Marketing sits on top of that: Take-Two said marketing spend was up 21% year on year, mostly in the December quarter, [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/all-the-takeaways-on-gta-from-the-take-two-earnings-call-strauss-zelnick/) reported. Budget rumours rarely say whether they include it.

Two hard numbers exist nearby. Rockstar told a UK court the 2022 hack cost it about $5 million (see [[timeline/gta-6-2022-leak|the 2022 leak]]), and Take-Two guides FY27 net bookings at $8.0-8.2 billion. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

## Will it earn the money back?

Retail sales alone cannot establish when development and marketing costs were recouped. [Take-Two's November 20 release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/grand-theft-auto-vi-shatters-entertainment-box-office-records) reports $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales, based on company estimates through the end of November 19 worldwide, and 32.5 million pre-release units. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (company disclosure) That is consumer spending, not net profit or a disclosed development budget. Platform shares, marketing, distribution and other costs prevent a direct break-even calculation from these figures alone. The company disclosure, older forecasts and accounting limits are collected on [[release/gta-6-sales-and-pre-orders|sales and pre-orders]].

## What would flip it

A cost figure from a Take-Two filing or executive, a Rockstar staffer on the record, or a court document would replace the estimates. Until then the analyst range stays reported and $2 billion stays debunked.
