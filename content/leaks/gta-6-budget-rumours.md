GTA 6's budget has never been disclosed. Rockstar has denied that it **exceeded $1.5 billion**, without giving a number, which makes the long-running **$2 billion** rumour <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>. Analysts estimate it at **more than $1 billion** <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Anything more precise is a guess.

## The claim

"GTA 6 cost $2 billion to make," sometimes put as "$1-2 billion". The figure circulated from 2023 to 2025 and was often repeated as fact, usually in headlines about GTA 6 being the most expensive game ever.

## Where did it come from?

No document or named source. Wikipedia describes "$1-2B" as a long-running rumour. Analysts then added their own estimates on top. One widely shared [GamesRadar+ piece](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/analyst-says-i-expect-a-usd100-price-point-for-gta-6-estimates-rockstars-development-cost-is-over-usd1-5-billion-and-predicts-it-will-earn-usd10-billion-lifetime-and-another-usd500-million-annually-from-gta-online) put development cost above $1.5 billion, alongside a $100 price prediction that also missed. See [[leaks/gta-6-100-dollar-price-rumour|the $100 price rumour]].

## What did Rockstar actually say?

In a Bloomberg feature around October 2026, Kolbe denied that the budget topped $1.5 billion but would not give a figure, per [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/rockstar-still-wont-reveal-cost-152304936.html). That is the only on-record statement from Rockstar about cost. We saw that coverage through search results, not the Bloomberg article itself.

### Contradiction: Pachter versus Rockstar

[CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-launch.html) on Nov 14 cited Roth's Handler at over $1 billion and Pachter at over $1.5 billion. Pachter's figure contradicts Kolbe's denial. We side with the first-party statement, so the most defensible range is **$1 billion to $1.5 billion**. That range is our inference. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

## Why is the budget so hard to pin down?

- **Take-Two does not break out per-game costs** in its filings. The [Nov 9 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026072175/ttwo2q27earningsrelease.htm) gives company-wide figures only.
- **Development ran for years.** Pre-production began around 2014-15, early development after Red Dead Redemption 2 in late 2018, and principal production in 2020, per Wikipedia. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>
- **The studio more than doubled.** Rockstar's headcount grew from about 2,441 to 4,711 across 12 offices during development, per Wikipedia. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>
- **Marketing is separate.** Take-Two said marketing spend was up 21% year on year, mostly in the December quarter, [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/all-the-takeaways-on-gta-from-the-take-two-earnings-call-strauss-zelnick/) reported. Budget rumours rarely say whether they include it.

Two hard numbers do exist. Rockstar told a UK court that the 2022 hack cost it about $5 million (see [[timeline/gta-6-2022-leak|the 2022 leak]]), and Take-Two guides FY27 net bookings at $8.0-8.2 billion. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

## Will GTA 6 make its money back?

Almost certainly, on current estimates. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> Sensor Tower put pre-orders at about $1 billion and 10.31 million units by Nov 11. That is an estimate, not a company figure. Analyst forecasts for the first year cluster around 40 million copies. See [[release/gta-6-sales-and-pre-orders|sales and pre-orders]]. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## What would change this grade?

A Take-Two filing or executive naming a cost, a Rockstar staffer on the record, or a court document. Any of these would replace the estimates. Until then this page stays <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> for the analyst range and <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> for $2 billion.
