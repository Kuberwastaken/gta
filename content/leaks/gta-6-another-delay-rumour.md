No, GTA 6 is not delayed again. It launches **Thursday, November 19, 2026** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Take-Two said on Nov 9 that Rockstar has finished development, pre-loads have been live since Nov 10, and the first update rolled out on Nov 13. Every 2026 rumour of a third delay to 2027 is <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>.

## The claim

"GTA 6 slips again, to February 2027 or later." Versions of this did the rounds several times in 2026, usually spiking before each Take-Two earnings call.

## Where did it come from?

4chan posts and parody accounts, according to [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/gta-6-release-date-2027-delay-claims-unconfirmed-1828314). None traced back to Rockstar, Take-Two or a credible reporter. A related rumour said the physical edition would slip.

The rumours had a real basis in history. GTA 6 *was* delayed twice, so people were primed to expect a third.

## Evidence against a third delay

Take-Two reaffirmed the date at every earnings report in 2026:

- **Feb 3, 2026:** on track for Nov 19. Asked about a physical-edition delay, Strauss Zelnick said: "That's not the plan," per [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/krisholt/2026/02/03/gta-6-release-date-physical-edition-delay-rumors-take-two-earnings-borderlands-4-switch-2-ai-games/). <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>
- **May 21, 2026:** Take-Two's [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026037260/ttwo4q26earningsrelease.htm) tied its record FY27 outlook to "the November 19th launch of Grand Theft Auto VI". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>
- **Aug 7, 2026:** the Q1 release reaffirmed Nov 19. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>
- **Nov 9, 2026:** "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away," per the [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026072175/ttwo2q27earningsrelease.htm). On the call, executives said Rockstar has finished development and the marketing campaign is set, [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/all-the-takeaways-on-gta-from-the-take-two-earnings-call-strauss-zelnick/) reported. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

And the launch machinery is already running:

- **Pre-load opened Nov 10** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. See [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|pre-load and file size]].
- **Boxed codes reached stores Nov 12.** See [[release/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc|the physical copy]].
- **Update 1.002.000 rolled out Nov 13.** <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## Did the 2025 firings cause a delay?

No, according to Take-Two, which said the October 2025 UK firings were unrelated to the November 2025 delay. That is a company claim; the firings themselves are disputed at tribunal. See [[timeline/rockstar-uk-firings-and-union|Rockstar UK firings and the union]].
