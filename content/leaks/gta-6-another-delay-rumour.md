No. GTA 6 launches Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and every 2026 rumour of a third slip to February 2027 or later is <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>. Take-Two said on November 9 that Rockstar has finished development, pre-loads opened November 10, and the first patch went out November 13.

## Where the 2027 rumour came from

4chan posts and parody accounts, according to [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/gta-6-release-date-2027-delay-claims-unconfirmed-1828314), which found no link to Rockstar, Take-Two or a named reporter. The claims spiked before each Take-Two earnings call. A spin-off said the physical edition would ship late.

People believed it because the game had already slipped twice.

In May 2025 Rockstar said, "We are very sorry that this is later than you expected", per [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/gta-6-delayed-once-again-rockstar-games-announces-new-launch-date-in-2026-474537-2025-05-02). The November 2025 move cited extra polish, [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-now-officially-delayed-to-November-2026.1157152.0.html) reported. Both came from Rockstar directly; no 2026 rumour did. The full record is on [[timeline/gta-6-delays|GTA 6 delays]].

## Evidence against a third delay

Take-Two restated November 19 at every quarterly report in 2026. On February 3, asked about a physical-edition slip, Strauss Zelnick said, "That's not the plan", per [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/krisholt/2026/02/03/gta-6-release-date-physical-edition-delay-rumors-take-two-earnings-borderlands-4-switch-2-ai-games/). On May 21 the company's [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026037260/ttwo4q26earningsrelease.htm) tied its $8.0-8.2 billion FY27 bookings outlook to "the November 19th launch of Grand Theft Auto VI". The August 7 Q1 release repeated the date. On November 9 the [Q2 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026072175/ttwo2q27earningsrelease.htm) opened with "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away", and executives said development was finished and the marketing campaign set, [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/all-the-takeaways-on-gta-from-the-take-two-earnings-call-strauss-zelnick/) reported. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

The launch machinery is already running, which is harder to fake than a statement. Rockstar's [pre-load post](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10) moved pre-load forward, from November 12 to November 10. Pre-loading consoles are now holding a download of up to 212 GB (see [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|pre-load and file size]]). Code-in-box copies reached stores on November 12 ([[release/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc|the physical copy]]). Update 1.002.000 began rolling out on November 13, about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.2 GB on Xbox. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## Did the 2025 firings cause a delay?

Take-Two says no: it called the October 2025 firings of 31 UK staff unrelated to the November 2025 delay announced a week later. That is a company claim, and the firings themselves are still before a Glasgow tribunal. See [[timeline/rockstar-uk-firings-and-union|Rockstar UK firings and the union]].
