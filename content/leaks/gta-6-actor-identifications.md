## What changed?

These matches were previously fan identifications or press-linked roles on this site. They are now <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> because Rockstar explicitly pairs the character and performer. This is a dated grade change based on new evidence, not retroactive proof that a voice comparison, IMDb entry or every repost was reliable.

## What does not follow from a correct identification?

A credit does not verify unrelated story theories. It also does not authenticate an earlier social account. Our older sources discussed disputed claims of a Perez confirmation; the current role confirmation rests on Rockstar, not those posts. The two questions must stay separate.

The official cast identifies Dylan Rourke as Jason, not Troy Baker or Roger Craig Smith. Manny Perez, credited as Raul, is not Manni L. Perez, credited as Lucia. Exact credited names matter.

The [[characters/gta-6-voice-actors-and-cast|cast guide]] explains the scope of the credits and why a character-performer pairing does not specify every stunt or capture contribution. Other unresolved claims remain on the [[leaks/index|leaks ledger]].

The source list above retains the pre-launch reporting used for the earlier grades. Those older articles are historical context, not competing current verdicts. For the confirmed character-performer pairings in the table, the controlling source is Rockstar's credits page.
