Rockstar has not named a single GTA 6 cast member. Its usual practice is to let the end credits do it at launch. Until then, every actor name you have seen is either a fan identification or an actor talking about themselves.

The short version: **Manni L. Perez as Lucia and Dylan Rourke as Jason are <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>**. **Stephen Root is <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>** to be in the game, from his own words. **Troy Baker and Roger Craig Smith as Jason are <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>**.

## Who plays Lucia?

Fans settled on **Manni L. Perez** early, matching her voice and likeness to Lucia and pointing to a previous GTA Online role, per [SVG](https://www.svg.com/2246330/gta-6-extended-look-actors-voice-cast/) and [inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/gta-6-fans-think-manni-perez-is-lucia-but-rockstar-has-not-confirmed-it). Neither Rockstar nor Perez has confirmed it.

In her first Instagram Live in early September 2026, Perez ignored GTA questions, [LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/entertainment/gaming/gta-6-lucia-manni-perez-instagram-videogame-news-498848-20260903) reported. Silence is consistent with an NDA, but it is not confirmation.

### Did Perez confirm it?

No verified confirmation exists. [EGW News](https://egw.news/gaming/news/36925/mannie-l-perez-officially-confirmed-shes-playing-l-uR_T4gHRD) ran a headline saying she confirmed the role on social media. Other coverage ties that claim to an impersonator account. We grade the "Perez confirmed it" claim <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>, and the identification itself stays <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>.

## Who plays Jason?

Fans identify **Dylan Rourke**. An IMDb listing exists, but IMDb is user-edited, so it is not evidence. Rockstar and Rourke have not commented. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>

Two earlier theories are dead. Troy Baker and Roger Craig Smith both denied voicing Jason, per [Rockstar Report](https://rockstarreport.com/features/gta-6-characters-confirmed-rumoured-so-far). We did not see their original posts, so that rests on Rockstar Report.

## Is Stephen Root in GTA 6?

Yes, by his own account. Root told the Associated Press: "Yeah, you're gonna see me in there", as quoted by [Collider](https://collider.com/gta-6-stephen-root-brian-heder-king-of-the-hill/). Fans had recognised his voice in Trailer 2. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

The catch: in the quotes we saw, Root does not name Brian Heder. Headlines and Wikipedia make that link. Rockstar has not credited him. That still makes Root the closest thing to a confirmed actor in the cast.

## Why grade an actor's own words as "reported"?

Our [[about/how-we-grade|grading rules]] reserve <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> for first-party sources: Rockstar, Take-Two, platform holders, ratings boards, or Rockstar staff on the record. An actor is not Rockstar staff, and we read Root's words via a secondhand quote. If Rockstar credits him, the grade goes up.

## What would change these grades?

- **End credits, Nov 19.** This is the big one. Every row on this page will be regraded once players reach the credits.
- **An actor posting on a verified account.** That would move an ID to <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> until Rockstar credits it.
- **A Rockstar cast announcement.** It has not done this for past games before launch.
