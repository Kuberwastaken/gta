Rockstar has not named a single GTA 6 actor. Its practice on past games is to let the end credits do it, so every name in circulation is either a fan identification or an actor talking about themselves. Manni L. Perez as Lucia and Dylan Rourke as Jason are <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Stephen Root being in the game is <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>, from his own words. Troy Baker or Roger Craig Smith as Jason is <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>.

## Lucia: Manni L. Perez

Fans matched Perez to Lucia early on voice and likeness, and point to an earlier GTA Online role, per [SVG](https://www.svg.com/2246330/gta-6-extended-look-actors-voice-cast/) and [inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/gta-6-fans-think-manni-perez-is-lucia-but-rockstar-has-not-confirmed-it). Neither Rockstar nor Perez has confirmed it. In her first Instagram Live in early September 2026 she ignored GTA questions, [LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/entertainment/gaming/gta-6-lucia-manni-perez-instagram-videogame-news-498848-20260903) reported. That fits an NDA. It is not evidence either way.

The "Perez confirmed it" story is a separate claim, and it fails. [EGW News](https://egw.news/gaming/news/36925/mannie-l-perez-officially-confirmed-shes-playing-l-uR_T4gHRD) ran the headline; other coverage ties the post to an impersonator account. One aggregator summary dated a confirmation to August 28, 2026, and we found no primary post behind it. We grade the confirmation <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> and the identification itself <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>.

## Jason: Dylan Rourke

Fans identify Rourke, and an IMDb credit exists. IMDb is user-edited, so the credit is not evidence, and neither Rourke nor Rockstar has commented. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> The two older theories are dead: Troy Baker and Roger Craig Smith both denied voicing Jason, per [Rockstar Report](https://rockstarreport.com/features/gta-6-characters-confirmed-rumoured-so-far). We did not see their original posts, so that rests on Rockstar Report.

## Stephen Root

Root told the Associated Press, "Yeah, you're gonna see me in there", as quoted by [Collider](https://collider.com/gta-6-stephen-root-brian-heder-king-of-the-hill/) on September 20, 2026. Fans had recognised his voice in [[media/gta-6-trailer-2|Trailer 2]]. In the quotes we saw he does not name Brian Heder; headlines and Wikipedia make that link, and Rockstar has not credited him.

He is still graded reported rather than confirmed. Our [[about/how-we-grade|grading rules]] reserve confirmed for Rockstar, Take-Two, platform holders, ratings boards and Rockstar staff on the record. An actor is none of those, and we have his words secondhand.

## What would flip it

The end credits on November 19 regrade every row at once. Before that, an actor posting on a verified account would move their row to reported, and only a Rockstar credit moves anything to confirmed. Rockstar has never published a cast list before launch on past games, so expect the credits to be the source.
