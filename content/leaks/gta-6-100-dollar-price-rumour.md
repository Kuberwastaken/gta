No, GTA 6 is not $100. Rockstar set the Standard Edition at $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition at $99.99 in the US on June 24, 2026, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The $100 base-price rumour is <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>. It overshot the base game by $20 and landed a cent away from the top edition.

## Where the $100 figure came from

Analysts, not leakers. Michael Pachter and Matthew Ball floated a $100 price point in 2024 and 2025. The most shared version, a [GamesRadar+ piece](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/analyst-says-i-expect-a-usd100-price-point-for-gta-6-estimates-rockstars-development-cost-is-over-usd1-5-billion-and-predicts-it-will-earn-usd10-billion-lifetime-and-another-usd500-million-annually-from-gta-online), paired "I expect a $100 price point" with a development cost estimate above $1.5 billion and a $10 billion lifetime revenue forecast. The budget half of that prediction has its own page: [[leaks/gta-6-budget-rumours|GTA 6 budget rumours]].

The logic was that the biggest game ever, on a reported record budget, could break the $70 ceiling most console releases had settled on. No Rockstar or Take-Two source ever said $100. Bank of America's analyst predicted $80 and got it right. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## How the price was settled

Take-Two kept it quiet for months. In May 2026 Strauss Zelnick told [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/gaming/news/gta-5-no-delay-price-marketing-summer-1236755303/) that no price was baked into the company's earnings guidance. On June 24 Rockstar confirmed $79.99 and $99.99, with the Vintage Vice City Pack as the pre-order bonus, [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/06/24/rockstar-reveals-gta-6s-price-is-80-confirms-preorder-time-bonuses/) reported. Microsoft's [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/) listed the same two prices on November 10. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

The $100 tier is where the money went anyway. Sensor Tower estimates that about 89% of pre-orders were the $99.99 Ultimate Edition, per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI). <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (estimate) So most buyers are paying the price the analysts predicted, just not for the base game.

Canada is the one market where "over $100" is literally true. [CBC News](https://www.cbc.ca/news/entertainment/gta-6-pre-orders-digital-grand-theft-auto-9.7248519) headlined a "$110 price tag", which is C$109.99. Mexico is the only market where the two stores disagree: MX$1,292.24 on PlayStation against MX$1,499 on Xbox.

A €89.99 European Standard price also circulated as a leak. We could not trace its origin, and store data shows €79.99, so it stays <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> at best.

## What would flip it

Only a price rise, and none has been announced. The Standard price has not moved in any store from the June 25 pre-order opening to launch week. A post-launch increase would be logged as a new item in the [[leaks/index|leaks ledger]], not as a regrade of this one.

## Is Ultimate worth the extra $20?

Ultimate adds 16 items that unlock across the story's chapters, including five businesses that only open with that edition. Standard owners can upgrade later for the $20 gap on Xbox; PlayStation hides the upgrade price until you own the game.
