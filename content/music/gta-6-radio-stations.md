On our **November 22** check, [Rockstar's music page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music) shows seven station previews. **Circoloco Records**, with underground house and techno from DJ Seth and DJ Antonio, joins the six previously covered here. Preview cards are selected examples, not proof that the entire game has only seven stations. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

## How many radio stations are in GTA 6?

| Figure | What the source establishes | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 preview cards | Rockstar music landing page checked November 22, including Circoloco Records | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> |
| Over 20 station and mixtape playlists | Spotify's November 20 announcement; mixes and playlists are not a one-to-one in-game station count | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> |
| 23 stations | Earlier Edge preview, relayed by GTAVice.net on October 29; retained as a historical reported total | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |

We have not independently audited every station in the released game or the full music credits. Launch having happened does not automatically verify an earlier total. Spotify also names **Stockyard FM** with PinkPantheress and The Dare, and **Vice City Rap Radio** with JT. Neither needs to appear among Rockstar's selected preview cards for Spotify to identify its playlist.

Spotify's first-party November 20 announcement separately confirms the Bad Bunny/RaiNao, Morgan Wallen/Lana Del Rey and Robyn/Alex Consani host pairings. That is stronger than the earlier press attribution for those particular names; other persona-to-artist mappings above retain their earlier press sourcing.

Cocoteo FM's tagline is "El party que nunca se apaga", and the original six previews remain the historical basis for the sampled songs listed above. Back Country Radio is billed as "airboat-ready", and Den of Geek called it the first dedicated country station since San Andreas after Tammy Wynette showed up in the [[music/gta-6-trailer-songs|Trailer 2 radio snippets]]. Dufrene, who voices Mud Dog Mike, is Lana Del Rey's husband, per Man of Many. AfroBank FM signs off its blurb with "Turn up the gbedu." The Chamber's blurb opens "Crushing metal has landed in Leonida", and Slayer play their own "Raining Blood" on it.

## Which old stations are coming back?

Flash FM is the only one Rockstar has confirmed. Its blurb calls it "The Vice City institution", a straight callback to the 2002 game set in the same [[world/vice-city|Vice City]]. Wikipedia lists V-Rock, Emotion 98.3 and Vice City FM as returning too, but that comes from leaks and press, not Rockstar's page <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Jason wears a V-Rock T-shirt in Trailer 2; Den of Geek read it as either a station revival or a nod <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Kotaku logged songs and stations heard in the [[leaks/gta-6-cyberleek-2026-leaks|CyberLeek footage]], which we have not checked <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Other series callbacks are on the [[media/gta-6-easter-eggs-in-trailers|easter eggs page]].

## Can you listen to the radio outside a car?

Yes. Rockstar's music page and Man of Many both say radio and podcasts play on foot through Jason's and Lucia's phones, a first for the series <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Man of Many adds that the audio runs through in-game earbuds <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The phone's other functions are on the [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]] page.

## Are the trailer songs on the radio?

Rockstar hasn't placed Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road" (Trailer 1) or the Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together" (Trailer 2) on any station. Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane" is on [[music/gta-6-soundtrack-album|The Album]], and Justice also wrote the [[music/gta-6-justice-score|adaptive score]]. Rockstar has not said whether the Album's 34 original songs play on the radio or which stations carry them.
