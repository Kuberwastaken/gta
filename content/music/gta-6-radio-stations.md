On our **November 24** recheck, [Rockstar's music page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music) shows seven station previews. **Circoloco Records**, with underground house and techno from DJ Seth and DJ Antonio, joins the six previously covered here. Preview cards are selected examples, not proof that the entire game has only seven stations. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Spotify also names **Stockyard FM** with PinkPantheress and The Dare, and **Vice City Rap Radio** with JT. Neither needs to appear among Rockstar's selected preview cards for Spotify to identify its playlist.

## What does the Billboard music interview add?

[Billboard's November 23 cover feature](https://www.billboard.com/music/features/gta-6-radio-songs-soundtrack-justice-billboard-cover-1236386805/) is based on direct interviews with Rockstar music VP **Ivan Pavlovich** and Justice. Pavlovich puts the game at **over 800 songs**. The article separately reports **23 stations, 21 music and two talk**. Those are not the same claim: the song figure is his quoted count, while the station split appears in the reporters' account. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

The newer article gives a source beyond the earlier Edge preview's 23-station report. It does **not** turn this guide into a complete station-by-station inventory. We have not checked every station, heard every song or audited the full credits. The interview does not establish that all 800-plus songs are in one streamable album, available in every region or divided evenly among music stations. Talk radio and podcasts are not extra music albums.

### Why licensing more music does not mean more playable tracks

Pavlovich describes clearing music with Rockstar's music, licensing and legal teams, and says they probably license around three times as many songs as they use. That is a description of the selection process, **not an exact licensed-track total or a hidden-playlist promise**. It would be misleading to turn the approximation into a confirmed 2,400-song library.

The feature names **Kaleidoscope** as a station for international covers of Western songs and describes the work of finding rights holders. This supports the station description as reporting, not a complete host roster, tracklist or unlock guide. The music-page previews below remain separately first-party sourced.

The [[music/gta-6-soundtrack-album|57-song Deluxe Album]] and [[music/gta-6-justice-score|Justice score album]] are release products with their own lists and dates. Neither is a substitute for the in-game library count. No mission placements or story examples from the interview are needed here.

Spotify's first-party November 20 announcement separately confirms the Bad Bunny/RaiNao, Morgan Wallen/Lana Del Rey and Robyn/Alex Consani host pairings. Cocoteo FM's tagline is "El party que nunca se apaga", and the original six previews remain the historical basis for the sampled songs listed above. Back Country Radio is billed as "airboat-ready", and Den of Geek called it the first dedicated country station since San Andreas after Tammy Wynette showed up in the [[music/gta-6-trailer-songs|Trailer 2 radio snippets]]. Dufrene, who voices Mud Dog Mike, is Lana Del Rey's husband, per Man of Many. AfroBank FM signs off its blurb with "Turn up the gbedu." The Chamber's blurb opens "Crushing metal has landed in Leonida", and Slayer play their own "Raining Blood" on it.

## Which old stations are coming back?

Flash FM is the only one Rockstar has confirmed. Its blurb calls it "The Vice City institution", a straight callback to the 2002 game set in the same [[world/vice-city|Vice City]]. Wikipedia lists V-Rock, Emotion 98.3 and Vice City FM as returning too, but that comes from leaks and press, not Rockstar's page <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Jason wears a V-Rock T-shirt in Trailer 2; Den of Geek read it as either a station revival or a nod <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Kotaku logged songs and stations heard in the [[leaks/gta-6-cyberleek-2026-leaks|CyberLeek footage]], which we have not checked <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Other series callbacks are on the [[media/gta-6-easter-eggs-in-trailers|easter eggs page]].

## Can you listen to the radio outside a car?

Yes. Rockstar's music page and Man of Many both say radio and podcasts play on foot through Jason's and Lucia's phones, a first for the series <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Man of Many adds that the audio runs through in-game earbuds <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The phone's other functions are on the [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]] page.

## Are the trailer songs on the radio?

Rockstar hasn't placed Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road" (Trailer 1) or the Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together" (Trailer 2) on any station. Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane" is on [[music/gta-6-soundtrack-album|The Album]], and Justice also wrote the [[music/gta-6-justice-score|adaptive score]]. The [[music/gta-6-soundtrack-album|Album now has a 57-song Deluxe Edition]], but that tracklist alone does not assign every song to an in-game station or establish a complete radio library.
