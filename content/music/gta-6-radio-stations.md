Rockstar has officially revealed **six GTA 6 radio stations**: Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics. Each one has a preview on Rockstar's music page with three confirmed tracks. The full station count is not official. Edge magazine reports 23 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

The six were unveiled on October 8, 2026, about six weeks before the [[release/gta-6-release-date|November 19 launch]]. Rockstar's line is that the game "features the biggest and widest-ranging selection of in-game radio and podcasts yet," with stations "dedicated to everything from Classic Rock to Talk Radio". Classic rock and talk radio are not among the six shown, which is one more sign that more stations are coming.

## How many radio stations are in GTA 6?

Two numbers are circulating, and they mean different things.

| Figure | Who says it | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 stations | Rockstar's music page, with previews | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> |
| 23 stations | Edge's cover preview, as summarised by GTAVice.net on Oct 29, 2026 | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |

GTAVice.net's summary of the Edge preview states plainly that "Grand Theft Auto VI will feature 23 radio stations". Edge had hands-on access and spoke to Rockstar, so the figure is credible. But it is not on any Rockstar page, and Rockstar has not listed the other 17 stations. We will move it to confirmed when the game is out or Rockstar publishes it.

Twenty-three would sit comfortably with Rockstar's "biggest and widest-ranging" claim, but that is our reading, not a Rockstar statement <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>.

## Which old stations are coming back?

Only **Flash FM** is confirmed. Wikipedia's GTA VI article lists V-Rock, Emotion 98.3 and Vice City FM as returning, but that rests on leak and press reporting, not on Rockstar's page <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Jason wears a V-Rock T-shirt in Trailer 2, which Den of Geek read as either a station revival or simply a nod <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Kotaku catalogued songs and stations spotted in the 2026 [[leaks/gta-6-cyberleek-2026-leaks|CyberLeek footage]], which we have not verified <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>.

## Can you listen to the radio outside a car?

Yes. Jason and Lucia can play radio and podcasts on foot through earbuds connected to their in-game phones, which Man of Many describes as a series first <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar's own copy confirms the game includes "in-game radio and podcasts" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. The phone itself is covered in [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]].

## Are the trailer songs on the radio?

Not confirmed for Trailers 1 and 2. Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road" and the Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together" sound like radio picks, but Rockstar has not placed them on a station. Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane" is on [[music/gta-6-soundtrack-album|The Album]] and the [[music/gta-6-justice-score|Justice score]]. How the 34 original Album tracks are spread across stations is not yet known.
