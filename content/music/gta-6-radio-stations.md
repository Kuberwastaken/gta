Rockstar has shown six GTA 6 radio stations, all on October 8, 2026: Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics. Each has a preview page on Rockstar's site with three tracks. Edge puts the full count at 23 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>, which leaves 17 stations nobody outside Rockstar and the press preview has named.

Rockstar's own copy points the same way. It promises "the biggest and widest-ranging selection of in-game radio and podcasts yet" with stations "dedicated to everything from Classic Rock to Talk Radio". Neither classic rock nor talk radio is among the six, so at least two formats are still unannounced four days before the [[release/gta-6-release-date|November 19 launch]].

GTAVice.net quotes Edge directly: "Grand Theft Auto VI will feature 23 radio stations". Edge played the game and interviewed Rockstar staff, so the number is credible, but it appears on no Rockstar page. It moves to confirmed when the game is out or Rockstar lists the stations.

Cocoteo FM's tagline is "El party que nunca se apaga", and its preview is the landing page for all six. Back Country Radio is billed as "airboat-ready", and Den of Geek called it the first dedicated country station since San Andreas after Tammy Wynette showed up in the [[music/gta-6-trailer-songs|Trailer 2 radio snippets]]. Dufrene, who voices Mud Dog Mike, is Lana Del Rey's husband, per Man of Many. AfroBank FM signs off its blurb with "Turn up the gbedu." The Chamber's blurb opens "Crushing metal has landed in Leonida", and Slayer play their own "Raining Blood" on it.

## Which old stations are coming back?

Flash FM is the only one Rockstar has confirmed. Its blurb calls it "The Vice City institution", a straight callback to the 2002 game set in the same [[world/vice-city|Vice City]]. Wikipedia lists V-Rock, Emotion 98.3 and Vice City FM as returning too, but that comes from leaks and press, not Rockstar's page <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Jason wears a V-Rock T-shirt in Trailer 2; Den of Geek read it as either a station revival or a nod <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Kotaku logged songs and stations heard in the [[leaks/gta-6-cyberleek-2026-leaks|CyberLeek footage]], which we have not checked <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Other series callbacks are on the [[media/gta-6-easter-eggs-in-trailers|easter eggs page]].

## Can you listen to the radio outside a car?

Yes. Rockstar's music page and Man of Many both say radio and podcasts play on foot through Jason's and Lucia's phones, a first for the series <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Man of Many adds that the audio runs through in-game earbuds <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The phone's other functions are on the [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]] page.

## Are the trailer songs on the radio?

Rockstar hasn't placed Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road" (Trailer 1) or the Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together" (Trailer 2) on any station. Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane" is on [[music/gta-6-soundtrack-album|The Album]], and Justice also wrote the [[music/gta-6-justice-score|adaptive score]]. Rockstar has not said whether the Album's 34 original songs play on the radio or which stations carry them.

The remaining stations, hosts and tracklists become checkable on November 19. Until then, this page lists only what Rockstar has published. The [[music/index|music hub]] covers the album and score release dates.
