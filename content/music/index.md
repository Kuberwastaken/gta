GTA 6's music comes in three separate releases. The in-game radio plays mostly licensed songs on stations hosted by celebrity DJs. *GTA VI: The Album* began with 34 original songs; its November 23 Deluxe Edition adds 23, for 57 total. Justice's adaptive *Original Score* plays in the game from day one, with its own album on December 11.

## How many radio stations are in GTA 6?

The music landing page rechecked November 24 has **seven station-preview cards**, including Circoloco Records with DJ Seth and DJ Antonio. That updates the earlier six-preview list without establishing a complete station total. The [[music/gta-6-radio-stations|radio guide]] separates preview cards, the older Edge count and Spotify's larger playlist offering. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Spotify's November 20 first-party announcement identifies Stockyard FM with PinkPantheress and The Dare and Vice City Rap Radio with JT, alongside other named hosts. Its **over 20 station and mixtape playlists** are not a one-for-one count of in-game stations.

## What songs are in the trailers?

Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road" in [[media/gta-6-trailer-1|Trailer 1]] (December 2023), the Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together" in [[media/gta-6-trailer-2|Trailer 2]] (May 2025), six licensed tracks led by Depeche Mode's "People Are People" in the August 2026 Extended Look, and Justice's original "Flower in a Hurricane" in the October 2026 Launch Trailer. Rockstar has not said whether any of the older songs are on the in-game radio.

## What is the difference between The Album and the score?

The Album began with 34 original songs by many artists. **Rockstar now lists all 57 Deluxe titles and artists**, adding 23 bonus tracks to that original edition. The [[music/gta-6-soundtrack-album|Album guide]] follows that current order, including the corrected Young Thug title.

The score is Justice's adaptive music, with its album still dated **December 11**. Rockstar currently lists **Starting from Zero**, **Flower in a Hurricane** and **Pusher** as score singles. The first two also appear on The Album, so the earlier claim of only one shared track is stale. See the [[music/gta-6-justice-score|Justice score guide]] for the distinction between available singles and the later album.

## Listen through the official Spotify destination

Spotify's [November 20 announcement](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-11-20/grand-theft-auto-vi-spotify/) launches a GTA listening destination in 33 markets. It combines soundtrack and score selections, radio and mixtape playlists, classic GTA music, personalized mixes and podcasts. Spotify says playlists will grow; it does not supply a market list in this announcement. We have not verified access for every country or account. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>
