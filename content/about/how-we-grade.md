Every factual claim on GTA VI Wire carries one of five grades, and every page carries a page-level status. **Confirmed means first-party. Reported means credible press. Rumour means unproven.** Speculation is our own inference; debunked means shown false. A claim is stated as fact only if Rockstar, Take-Two, a platform holder or a ratings board has said it.

## What do the grades mean?

| Grade | What it means | Typical source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> | First-party: Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony, Microsoft, a ratings board, or a named Rockstar staffer on the record | Rockstar Newswire, SEC filings, Xbox Wire, ESRB |
| <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> | Credible press, not first-party | Bloomberg, GamesBeat, IGN, VGC |
| <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> | Unproven: leaks, anonymous insiders, fan IDs, social posts | Leak accounts, forums, IMDb-style listings |
| <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> | Our own inference from the evidence | GTA VI Wire analysis |
| <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> | Shown false or overtaken by first-party facts | Contradicted by an official statement |

A sixth status, `mixed`, only applies to whole pages. It means the page blends grades, so each claim is tagged inline. This page is one of them.

## How do page grades and inline tags work?

Each page has a status at the top. A page like [[release/gta-6-release-date|the release date]] can be confirmed end to end. A page like [[characters/gta-6-voice-actors-and-cast|the cast list]] mixes grades, so it is marked mixed.

On any mixed page, every claim that is not first-party gets a colored tag next to it. If you see no tag on a mixed page, the claim is confirmed and the source is listed at the bottom.

## How are sources ranked?

We rank sources in three tiers:

1. **Primary.** Rockstar's own [GTA VI site](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) and Newswire, Take-Two's [earnings filings with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026072175/ttwo2q27earningsrelease.htm), Sony and Microsoft announcements, and ratings boards. These are tagged `primary` in each page's source list.
2. **Press.** Outlets with editors and track records. A Rockstar quote relayed by a magazine counts as first-party for the quote itself, but we still say where it came from.
3. **Fan and insider.** Leakers, aggregator sites, anonymous accounts, datamines. Useful for leads, never enough for confirmed.

A named Rockstar developer in an interview, like Rob Nelson of Rockstar North, counts as first-party when the words are quoted directly.

## How do grades change over time?

Grades move with evidence. Four cases from the site:

- **Female lead (reported to confirmed).** Bloomberg reported in July 2022 that the game would star a Latina woman in a Bonnie and Clyde-style duo ([GamesRadar+ summary](https://www.gamesradar.com/gta-6-to-feature-series-first-female-protagonist-as-rockstar-overhauls-workplace-culture)). It stayed <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> until Trailer 1 showed Lucia in December 2023. See [[characters/gta-6-first-female-protagonist|the first female protagonist]].
- **Hurricanes (rumour to confirmed).** Storms were a fan rumour for years. On September 29, 2026 Rockstar told [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/exclusive/2026/09/29/exclusive-grand-theft-auto-vi-features-tropical-storms-and-hurricanes) the game has tropical storms and seasonal hurricanes. See [[world/gta-6-weather-and-hurricanes|weather and hurricanes]].
- **$100 base price (rumour to debunked).** Analysts predicted $100 for years. Rockstar set Standard at $79.99 and Ultimate at $99.99 in June 2026. See [[leaks/gta-6-100-dollar-price-rumour|the $100 price rumour]].
- **60fps mode (rumour to debunked).** Fan tables showed a performance mode. Rockstar's November 10 pre-load post set a 30fps target on every console, as [VGC reported](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/rockstar-confirms-gta-6-targets-30-fps-with-ps5-pro-exclusive-ray-tracing/). See [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|PS5 Pro performance]].

## What stays reported or rumour?

Three claims still sit in the middle:

- **PC in Q1 2027.** GamesBeat cited four unnamed sources in October 2026 ([via PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/3260660/we-guessed-late-2027-for-gta-6-on-pc-a-report-says-q1-2027.html)). Credible outlet, no Rockstar comment: <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. PC at launch is <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>. See [[leaks/gta-6-pc-at-launch-rumour|PC at launch]].
- **Actor IDs.** Manni L. Perez as Lucia and Dylan Rourke as Jason are fan identifications: <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Stephen Root told AP he is in the game, so his part is <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>, but Rockstar has not credited him as Brian Heder. See [[leaks/gta-6-actor-identifications|actor identifications]].
- **Review embargo.** November 18 comes from NateTheHate and Insider Gaming ([tbreak](https://tbreak.com/gta-6-review-codes-embargo-november-18/)). Rockstar has not confirmed it, so it is <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[release/gta-6-reviews-and-review-embargo|reviews and embargo]].

## How do you handle contradictions?

When sources disagree, we show both versions and say which one we trust and why. Pre-load: older coverage said November 12, Rockstar's November 3 Newswire said November 10. We go with the newer first-party post and note the stale one. Character switching: Red Bull reported you can swap between Jason and Lucia "at any time", while Rob Nelson told Famitsu switching is restricted by story progression. The developer on the record wins; see [[gameplay/gta-6-character-switching|character switching]].

If a claim is debunked, we keep it on the [[leaks/index|leaks ledger]] with the date it was settled. Rumours that turned out wrong stay part of the record. Our own factual errors go on the [[about/corrections|corrections page]].
