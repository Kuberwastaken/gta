Help improve an independent GTA 6 reference. **Contribute through a pull request** to [GTA VI Wire on GitHub](https://github.com/Kuberwastaken/gta), whether it fixes an error, adds a better source, improves a guide or repairs the site.

## How to send a pull request

1. Fork the repository and create a focused branch.
2. Edit the relevant page in `content/` or the site code. Improve existing coverage before adding a duplicate.
3. Cite direct sources, check current dates and label reporting, rumours and speculation. Follow [[about/how-we-grade|our evidence grades]]. Never submit stolen footage, private information or credentials.
4. Follow `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository. Run the content check, TypeScript and build; inspect mobile and desktop screenshots for visual changes.
5. Open a PR explaining the change, evidence, checks and unresolved questions. Maintainers review before merging. A PR does not guarantee publication.

Bug reports and source leads can also go into issues. A sourced PR is the preferred route for a fix. See [[about/corrections|corrections]] for our approach to errors.

## Reuse original articles with credit

Copyright 2026 Kuber Mehta and credited contributors. Unless a page says otherwise, our original editorial text is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). You can copy, share, translate and adapt it, including commercially. These freedoms cannot be revoked while you comply with the license.

When sharing our original text, including in a content-bearing fork:

- Credit the named author(s) and **GTA VI Wire**. Retain supplied copyright, license and disclaimer notices.
- Retain a link to the **original article** where reasonably practicable, not only your fork or a source we cited.
- Link to **CC BY 4.0** and indicate whether you changed the text, including previous modifications where indicated.
- Do not suggest we endorse your project or add restrictions that prevent exercise of the licensed rights.

Attribution can be reasonable for the medium and context. The [full legal license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) controls; no warranties are given. The repository's `LICENSE-CONTENT.md` records the scope.

A suggested credit line: **"[Article title] by [credited author(s)], GTA VI Wire, [original article URL], CC BY 4.0. [Changes, if any.]"** Replace the placeholders with the article's actual information.

If you mention the site or report on our work, please credit and link us too. That is a courtesy request, not an extra CC BY restriction: the license does not impose conditions on public-domain facts, a mere mention or uses permitted by copyright exceptions.

## What is not CC BY?

**Rockstar/Take-Two and other third-party screenshots, artwork, video, music, logos, trademarks, quotations and embedded material are excluded.** Their owners' rights and applicable law still apply. A credit or source link is not a grant of permission to reuse them. Fonts and dependencies keep their own licenses. Site branding/trademarks are not licensed by CC BY.

The software retains its **MIT** license and upstream notices in `LICENSE.txt`. A code-only fork follows MIT rather than the editorial license. Original article contributions are accepted under CC BY 4.0; code contributions use MIT unless an existing file states otherwise. Contributors retain ownership and must have the right to submit their work.
