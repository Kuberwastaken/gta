When GTA VI Wire gets something wrong, we fix it, say what changed and log it here. The log includes the **November 20 heat-system correction**, **November 19 bonus corrections** and **November 18 weapon-wheel correction**.

## How do I report a mistake?

Send it to [@Kuberwastaken on X](https://x.com/Kuberwastaken) or open an issue at [github.com/Kuberwastaken](https://github.com/Kuberwastaken). The most useful reports include:

- The page and the exact sentence.
- What you think is wrong.
- A source, ideally first-party, such as [Rockstar's GTA VI site](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) or a Take-Two filing.

## What counts as a correction?

A correction is when we stated something that was wrong at the time we published it. Examples: a misquoted price, a wrong unlock time on [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|the unlock times page]], a misattributed quote, a broken source, or a claim graded higher than its evidence supported.

Each is fixed in place, with a dated note on the page and a line in the log below.

## Is a grade change a correction?

No. Grades move when new evidence arrives. When Rockstar told Game Informer on September 29, 2026 that the game has tropical storms and hurricanes, a long-running rumour became confirmed. That was news, not an error. Those moves are dated in each page's changelog and on the [[leaks/index|leaks ledger]]. The method is explained on [[about/how-we-grade|how we grade]].

If we had graded something as confirmed when it was only <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>, that would be a correction.

## What happens to the page?

- The error is fixed in the body and frontmatter.
- The `updated` date changes.
- A short note explains what was wrong and what it now says.
- Big changes, like a wrong date on [[release/gta-6-release-date|the release date page]] or [[news/2026-11-15-gta-6-launch-week-briefing|the launch week briefing]], also get flagged in [[news/index|news]].

We do not silently delete mistakes.
