GTA 6 has been in the works for more than a decade. Preliminary work began around **2014-2015**, after GTA V. Early development picked up once **Red Dead Redemption 2** shipped in late 2018, and principal production began in **2020** <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>; Take-Two has said development began "in earnest" that year. Rockstar North in Edinburgh leads, and executives said on November 9, 2026 that the game was finished <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

The early phases come from Wikipedia, which cites IGN and Bloomberg. Rockstar has published no development history of its own. From principal production to launch is about six years; from preliminary work, about twelve.

## Who is making GTA 6?

**Rockstar North** in Edinburgh leads, with Rockstar San Diego, Rockstar Toronto and other offices contributing. **Rockstar Los Angeles** was set up in 2023 to work on NPCs <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Per Wikipedia, Rockstar's headcount grew from 2,441 to 4,711 across 12 offices during development <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

The people who spoke for the game in 2026 <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>:

| Aaron Garbut | Co-studio head, Rockstar North |

## Did Dan Houser work on GTA 6?

Not on the story. Dan Houser, co-founder and lead writer from GTA III through Red Dead Redemption 2, took an extended break in 2019. Take-Two announced his resignation in February 2020 and he left on **March 11, 2020** <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. He went on to found Absurd Ventures, registered in February 2021 and announced in June 2023.

In late September 2025 Houser said GTA VI is "not going to be a story I wrote" and that "the world's probably had enough GTA from me" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. It is the first mainline GTA story without him since the 1997 original. Humphries is now the public face of the writing team; the story built around the two leads is on [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|Jason and Lucia's relationship]].

## How much did GTA 6 cost to make?

Rockstar has not disclosed it. In an October 2026 Bloomberg feature, Kolbe denied the budget exceeded $1.5 billion but gave no number <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The long-running $1-2 billion figure is a rumor <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Roth's Eric Handler estimates more than $1 billion and Michael Pachter more than $1.5 billion; both are analyst estimates. All figures are on [[leaks/gta-6-budget-rumours|GTA 6 budget rumors]].
