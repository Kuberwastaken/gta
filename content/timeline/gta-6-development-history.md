GTA 6 has been in the works for roughly a decade. Preliminary work began around **2014-2015**, after GTA V. Early development picked up after **Red Dead Redemption 2** shipped in late 2018, and principal production began in **2020** <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Take-Two itself has said development began "in earnest" in 2020. Rockstar North in Edinburgh leads, and executives said on November 9, 2026 that development was finished <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

The early phases come from Wikipedia's article, which cites press reporting including IGN and Bloomberg. Rockstar has not published its own development history. Counted from principal production, GTA 6 took about six years; counted from preliminary work, closer to twelve.

## Who is making GTA 6?

**Rockstar North** in Edinburgh leads. Rockstar San Diego, Rockstar Toronto and others contribute. **Rockstar Los Angeles** was set up in 2023, focused on NPC development <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Per Wikipedia, Rockstar's headcount grew from 2,441 to 4,711 across 12 offices during development <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## Did Dan Houser work on GTA 6?

Not on the story. Dan Houser, co-founder and lead writer from GTA III through Red Dead Redemption 2, took an extended break in 2019. Take-Two announced his resignation in February 2020, and he left on **March 11, 2020** <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. He founded Absurd Ventures, registered in February 2021 and announced in June 2023.

In late September 2025, Houser said GTA VI is "not going to be a story I wrote", adding that "the world's probably had enough GTA from me" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. That makes VI the first mainline GTA story without him since the original in 1997. Rupert Humphries, Rockstar's SVP of narrative, is now the public face of the story team. The story it has built around the two leads is covered on [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|Jason and Lucia's relationship]].

## Key decisions along the way

- **A female co-lead.** Bloomberg's Jason Schreier reported in July 2022 that the game would star a Latina woman in a Bonnie and Clyde-style duo, in a fictional Miami, alongside an overhaul of Rockstar's workplace culture after 2018 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Trailer 1 confirmed the duo. See [[characters/gta-6-first-female-protagonist|first female protagonist]].
- **Two leads, not four.** Rob Nelson told Famitsu that Rockstar considered four protagonists and rejected it, because more leads would mean less time with each one <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. The [[leaks/gta-6-four-protagonists-rumour|four-protagonist rumor]] grew out of that.
- **A much bigger, denser state.** Rockstar told Edge the landmass is "nearly as large as the playable maps of GTA 4, GTA 5, and RDR2 combined", and Aaron Garbut cited an "order of magnitude more interiors" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. More on [[world/gta-6-map-size|map size]].
- **No generative AI, no launch monetization.** Strauss Zelnick said generative AI has "zero part in what Rockstar Games is building", and Nelson said there is no monetization at launch <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.
- **Console first.** GTA VI launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. A PC version is reported for Q1 2027, not announced <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## How much did GTA 6 cost to make?

Rockstar has not disclosed it. In an October 2026 Bloomberg feature, Jennifer Kolbe denied the budget exceeded $1.5 billion without giving a number <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Long-running rumors put it at $1-2 billion <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Roth's Eric Handler estimates more than $1 billion and Michael Pachter more than $1.5 billion, both analyst estimates. Every figure is on [[leaks/gta-6-budget-rumours|GTA 6 budget rumors]].
