GTA 6 has no online multiplayer at launch. Rockstar's June pre-order post and the store listings describe it as "a single player experience", Xbox Wire calls it "single-player", and no GTA VI online mode, name or date has been announced. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

The ESRB listing backs that up. It carries an "In-Game Purchases" notice but no "Users Interact" label, which is consistent with no online mode. Tech Times read the missing label as confirmation on Oct 2. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> More on the [[release/gta-6-age-rating|age rating]] page.

Rob Nelson, co-head of Rockstar North, told Kinda Funny in late August that single-player has no real-money purchases and that generative AI was not used in development. Rockstar declined to discuss the next GTA Online in the same interview. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> The full rumour trail is on [[leaks/gta-6-online-at-launch-rumour|the online-at-launch rumour page]].

## Is GTA Online shutting down?

No. GTA Online on GTA V got the Kortz Center Heist summer update on July 14, 2026, per Take-Two's 8-K, and Take-Two says support continues after GTA VI launches. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

GTA V is still selling, too. Take-Two put it at "over 230M" units in August; GamesBeat's write-up of the Nov 9 call says it passed 235 million as of Sept 30, with about 5M sold in that quarter. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (the 8-K we read has no GTA V unit figure)

## Will GTA Online progress carry over?

Rockstar has said nothing about moving GTA Online characters, GTA$, property or vehicles into a future GTA VI online mode. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (absence) Any site claiming a transfer plan is guessing.

## Where does GTA+ fit?

GTA+ is Rockstar's GTA Online membership on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. It pays a monthly GTA$ deposit (reportedly GTA$500,000), adds 15% GTA$ to Shark Card purchases and includes a back catalogue of Rockstar games; console members recently got GTA Online access bundled in. Digital GTA VI pre-orders include a free month. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

The sources conflict on PC: GameSpot says the GTA Online-via-GTA+ perk is console-only, while Rockstar's page lists PC benefits. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Whether GTA+ extends to a GTA VI online mode is not announced. Terms and renewal costs are in the [[guides/gta-6-gta-plus-explained|GTA+ explainer]].

## Can I play GTA 6 with friends at all?

Not inside the game. PS5 Remote Play lets you stream your own console to another device such as a PlayStation Portal, and the in-game Snapmatic app lets Jason and Lucia DM photos to each other, though as fugitives they cannot post publicly. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> See [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]].

Rockstar has not explained the single-player launch. Its marketing has centred on the Jason and Lucia story and their [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|relationship system]], and Rob Nelson puts a full playthrough with optional content at about 80 hours. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>
