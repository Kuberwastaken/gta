**No, GTA 6 does not have online multiplayer at launch.** Rockstar's pre-order announcement and the store listings describe GTA VI as "a single player experience", and Xbox Wire calls it "single-player". **No GTA VI online mode, name or date has been announced.** <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

GTA 6 launches as a pure story game.

## Is GTA Online shutting down?

**No.** GTA Online on GTA V keeps running. The **Kortz Center Heist** summer update came out on **July 14, 2026**, per Take-Two's 8-K, and Take-Two says it will keep supporting GTA Online after GTA VI launches. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

GTA V itself is still selling: Take-Two put it at "over 230M" units in August, and GamesBeat's write-up of the Nov 9 call says it passed **235 million** as of Sept 30. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (235M; the 8-K we read has no GTA V unit figure)

## Will my GTA Online character, cars or money carry over?

**Nothing has been announced.** Rockstar has not said whether GTA Online characters, GTA$, property or vehicles will transfer to any future GTA VI online mode. Be wary of any site that claims to know. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (absence)

## What about GTA+?

**GTA+** is Rockstar's membership for GTA Online on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. Benefits include a monthly GTA$ deposit (reportedly GTA$500,000), +15% GTA$ on Shark Cards and a back catalogue of Rockstar games, and GTA Online access was recently added to GTA+ on consoles. Digital GTA VI pre-orders include a free month. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

GameSpot says the GTA Online-via-GTA+ perk is **console-only**, while Rockstar's page lists PC benefits. Both may be true if PC gets other perks, but the sources conflict. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

**Not announced:** whether GTA+ will carry over to a GTA VI online mode. Our [[guides/gta-6-gta-plus-explained|GTA+ explainer]] has the details.

## Can I play GTA 6 with friends at all?

Not in the game. GTA 6 is single-player. You can share the experience in other ways: Remote Play is supported on PS5, and Snapmatic, the in-game social app, lets Jason and Lucia DM photos to each other but not post publicly. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> See [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]].

## Why is there no online at launch?

Rockstar has not said. The single-player push is clear in its marketing: the Jason and Lucia story, the [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|relationship system]] and a reported 80-hour playthrough. Anything beyond that is guesswork. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>
