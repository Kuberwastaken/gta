GTA 6 runs natively on Xbox Series S at the same 30 fps target as Series X and PS5. Xbox Wire calls the Series S "the most affordable way to play natively". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Xbox One owners can play through Xbox Cloud Gaming from 1 AM ET on Nov 19, 2026. The stream runs at Series S settings, and you still have to buy the game: it is not in Game Pass. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Microsoft)

## What runs where

| Device | How | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Xbox Series X | Native | 30 fps target |
| Xbox Series S | Native | 30 fps target; DF measures ~720p internally, with frequent dips |
| Xbox One | Xbox Cloud Gaming only | Series S settings; from 1 AM ET Nov 19 |
| PC / browser | Not at launch | No cloud streaming to PC |
| PS5 / PS Portal cloud | Listed by PlayStation Store | Premium required; game ownership separate |
| PS4 | No announced native version | The PlayStation cloud listing names PS5 and PS Portal, not PS4 |

Official resolution specifications remain separate from independent measurements. [Digital Foundry's November 18 Xbox tech review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F3lxGD8HX2k) measures **about 720p internally on Series S**, compared with about 1440p on Series X in sampled shots. This supersedes our earlier blanket description of Series S figures as guesses. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (independent testing)

### How steady is Series S at launch?

The test finds much of the core visual technology retained, including strand hair and ray-tracing features, but reduced effect and asset quality. Its larger reservation is frame rate: traversal and combat can remain in the mid-to-upper 20s, with roughly 20 fps in demanding tests. Series X also drops under stress but generally holds its target more consistently in the reviewer's typical play.

If you already own a Series S, this is a warning about the tested experience, not a reason to assume it cannot run the game. If you are buying hardware specifically for GTA VI, compare the measured performance before treating the cheapest native console as an equivalent experience. See the [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|performance guide]] for the targets, measurements and limitations.

These are **local console tests**, not cloud latency or service-quality measurements. Xbox Wire's cloud-settings description does not prove an identical rendered frame rate or end-to-end latency for a streamed session. Network, service hardware and display conditions need their own evidence.

## What did Digital Foundry measure on Xbox One cloud?

**November 27 source check:** [Digital Foundry's November 26 Xbox One cloud test](https://www.digitalfoundry.net/features/gta-6-on-xbox-one-tested-the-power-of-the-cloud-delivers-save-for-one-big-issue), written up by William Judd, supplies the streaming measurements that the earlier local-console review above could not. It compares the streamed Series S version on Xbox One S with local Series S play. These are **the outlet's measurements**, not our own service test or Microsoft's performance guarantee. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (independent testing)

| Area | Digital Foundry's result | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| End-to-end input latency | 184.5ms through cloud versus 175.2ms locally on Series S, about 9ms extra | Not 9ms total latency, zero lag, or a prediction for every connection |
| Rendered frame rate | Broadly comparable to Series S in tested play, typically in the 20s to 30fps | Not a locked 30fps service or a new 60fps mode |
| Frame delivery | Cloud adds uneven 16.7ms and 50ms frames around the expected 33.3ms intervals | Similar average frame rates do not mean equally smooth delivery |
| Stream quality | Limited compression artefacts in the test; the top 1440p stream option reached up to 37Mbps | Stream resolution is not native rendering resolution; observed bitrate is not a minimum connection requirement |

The latency figures came from **Ethernet, a high-speed fibre connection and a wired controller**. Location, network conditions, controller and display can change the result. Even in that favourable setup, the game's substantial local input delay remains: a small streaming penalty does not make the total response time small.

Frame pacing is a separate concern from input lag. Frames arriving at uneven intervals can make motion look less smooth even when the average count resembles local Series S play. DF says it reported this to Microsoft, which was looking into it. Its suggestion that this might be latency mitigation is a hypothesis, **not a confirmed cause or an announced fix**. The article also notes crackling audio in both its cloud experience and local Series S, not a cloud-only defect.

The report identifies a Series S rendering profile, with a roughly 720p base image upscaled to 1080p, and separately discusses a 1440p stream option. Those are different stages of the image pipeline. Do not call the stream native 1440p or use its bitrate to promise a particular picture on every account.

DF says unnamed sources told it Microsoft runs one GTA VI instance per blade, rather than the two virtual Series S instances used in its general account of the service. That remains **source-based reporting**, not our inspection of Microsoft's servers or an official capacity disclosure. The article's power and bandwidth explanation for not serving the Series X version is speculation.

For an Xbox One owner, this is evidence that cloud play can be usable under the tested conditions, with frame-delivery compromises. It is not a native Xbox One port. If you already have Series S or X, DF recommends local play. Game ownership, cloud entitlement, regional access and any paid hours still need their own checks; this performance test does not validate a particular price or subscription offer.

## Will GTA 6 fit on a 512 GB Series S?

Yes, with little room left over. The console reports the download as about 209.86 GB (the store shows 225.34 GB for the same file), and Rockstar asks for up to 212 GB free. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> The Series S has well under 512 GB usable after system files, so plan to uninstall most other games. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> The unit math and the expansion question are in the [[guides/gta-6-storage-space-guide|storage space guide]].

## Do I need Game Pass to stream it?

You need to own the game. Xbox sells cloud playtime without a Game Pass subscription in supported markets. The [US 10-hour pack](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/cloud-playtime-10-hours/9pl4njhpdn7l) currently lists **$3.99** for select owned games. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> Microsoft's general cloud page also offers ad-supported sessions for eligible games, with session limits. Neither general offer is a promise that every GTA VI account can use that playtime method. Check the specific game's cloud entitlement before buying hours.

The [[guides/gta-6-cheapest-way-to-play|cost comparison]] separates game ownership, hardware and cloud access, including the limits of the "$10 GTA VI" claim. GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 are due on cloud "in the coming months", per Xbox Wire.

## Can I stream to PC, a phone or a browser?

Not at launch. Dexerto reports that Xbox's Matthew Ball confirmed no PC or browser streaming, and the fan aggregator gta6times reported the same on Oct 7. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> There is no native PC build either; GamesBeat's Q1 2027 report is covered on [[release/gta-6-pc-release-date|GTA 6 PC release date]]. Anything selling a "GTA 6 PC download" is a scam.

## Buying on Xbox

Xbox pricing matches PlayStation in every market but one: Mexico, where Xbox charges MX$1,499 against MX$1,292.24 on PS Store. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> The Xbox store shows the Ultimate upgrade price up front ($20 / £20 / €20), while PlayStation hides it until you own the game. Xbox codes in the box are not region-locked, per Rockstar; PS codes are. Full price table: [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]].

Microsoft calls GTA VI "the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> Sensor Tower's Nov 11 estimate puts Xbox at about 2.58M of 10.31M pre-orders (about $250M), roughly a quarter of the total. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (estimate)

## Can I play GTA 6 on PS4?

There is no announced native PS4 version. The newer PlayStation cloud listing below names **PS5 and PS Portal**, not PS4. Our earlier statement that Sony had no cloud option is now out of date; that does not make GTA VI a PS4 release.

## Can I cloud-stream GTA 6 on PS5 or PS Portal?

**Yes, the current US PlayStation Store listing supports it with Premium.** On our November 24 check, [the official GTA VI product page](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10000730/) explicitly lists PS5 and PS Portal cloud streaming as supported only with a Premium subscription. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

[Push Square's November 24 report](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-owners-can-now-stream-the-game-to-ps5-ps-portal-with-ps-plus-premium) says Sammy Barker tested it successfully on a PS5 in the UK. He reports that this was not available at launch, that players still need to own the game and that GTA VI has **not joined the PS Plus Premium catalogue**. The report's UK test is not our own service test or proof that every account and region has access. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

| Route | Where the game runs | Access distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PlayStation cloud streaming | Sony's servers, streamed to PS5 or PS Portal | Premium required; the game purchase is separate, not catalogue inclusion |
| PS5 Remote Play | Your own PS5, streamed to another device such as Portal | A separate route using your console, not proof of cloud entitlement |
| Xbox Cloud Gaming | Xbox cloud hardware at Series S settings, per Xbox Wire | Separate Xbox ownership and cloud-access terms; not a PlayStation entitlement |

The Sony listing does not add PS4, PC or browser support. Neither source here establishes a GTA VI cloud resolution, frame-rate mode, input-lag result or PS5 Pro rendering profile. Our local-console benchmarks must not be read as cloud benchmarks. Before paying for access, check the game's stream option and eligibility on your account; the US listing and one UK test do not establish worldwide availability.
