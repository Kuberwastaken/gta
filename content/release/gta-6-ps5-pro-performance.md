GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S. There is no 60 fps mode, no 40 fps mode and no performance toggle at launch. Rockstar put this in its Nov 10, 2026 pre-load post, as reported by The Shortcut, Notebookcheck, GTA BOOM and VGC. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported)

The PS5 Pro gets two upgrades, PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows, which Rockstar says are "only available on PlayStation 5 Pro". The frame rate stays at 30. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported)

## GTA 6 performance on every console

| Console | Frame rate | Extras | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PS5 | 30 fps | DualSense haptics, Tempest 3D Audio, faster loading | Not disclosed |
| PS5 Pro | 30 fps | Base PS5 features plus PSSR and ray-traced shadows | Not disclosed |
| Xbox Series X | 30 fps | — | Not disclosed |
| Xbox Series S | 30 fps | Native; Xbox Wire calls it "the most affordable way to play natively" | Not disclosed |
| Xbox Cloud Gaming | Streams at Series S settings | Plays on Xbox One, from 1 AM ET Nov 19 | — |

Rockstar's site says GTA VI "Plays Best On PS5", and every trailer plus the 26-minute Extended Look was captured on a base PS5, not a Pro. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported) Digital Foundry found no sign of Sony's upscaler in the [[media/gta-6-launch-trailer|Oct 15 launch trailer]], which fits that. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> What you have watched is the standard console version.

## Does GTA 6 have 60 fps?

No. Two claims say otherwise and both conflict with Rockstar's post. gta6-life.com publishes a table with a 60 fps "Performance" mode, and an older Rock and Borys podcast rumour promised 30 and 60 fps modes. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> A "performance mode" settings screenshot did not come from Rockstar. Claims like these are tracked in [[leaks/index|leaks and rumours]].

Rockstar has not explained the 30 fps choice.

## What does the PS5 Pro add?

PSSR (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution) is Sony's machine-learning upscaler, so the Pro should produce a sharper image at the same 30 fps. Ray-traced shadows are the only ray-tracing feature Rockstar lists for any console. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Without resolution figures, nobody can say yet how much sharper the Pro is. Expect pixel counts from Digital Foundry and others after the reported [[release/gta-6-reviews-and-review-embargo|Nov 18 review embargo]].

A ComicBook.com "PlayStation leak" before the official post promised ray-traced reflections, shadows and lighting on Pro. Rockstar's list names shadows only, so treat reflections and lighting as not happening at launch. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> (as stated)

### Should you buy a PS5 Pro for GTA 6?

Not for frame rate. A base PS5 runs the same 30 fps game with the same content; the Pro buys image quality and shadows. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> Sony has announced no GTA VI console bundle. Sony does launch a cheaper Midnight Black PS5 on Nov 19, but it is sold only in Japan and region-locked there. Forbes argues that console prices, pushed up by AI-driven memory costs, may blunt any GTA hardware bump. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

The Limited Edition DualSense controllers ($84.99) use standard DualSense hardware, so they add nothing to performance. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

## Xbox Series X and Series S

Both run natively at 30 fps. Series S resolution is undisclosed, and the "720p–900p" figures online are guesses. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> The cloud version streams at Series S settings. Storage and cloud details are on [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|Series S and cloud gaming]].

## Will a 40 fps mode arrive later?

Nothing is announced. Notebookcheck found two hints in the files: 120 Hz output settings, which a 40 fps mode needs because 40 divides evenly into 120, and references to PSSR version 13.60. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Update 1.002.000 shipped Nov 13 with no patch notes, so nobody knows whether it touches performance; see [[release/gta-6-day-one-patch|day-one patch]].

## Remote Play and PC

Remote Play is supported, so you can stream from your own PS5 to a PlayStation Portal, per The Shortcut. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

There is no PC version, so there are no PC specs. GamesBeat reported a Q1 2027 PC target on Oct 13, citing four unnamed sources. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Any "GTA 6 PC requirements" list is invented, and any "GTA 6 PC download" is malware. See [[release/gta-6-pc-release-date|GTA 6 PC release date]] and the rest of the [[release/index|release hub]].

## What we're watching

Two numbers will matter after launch: measured resolution on each console, and whether the 30 fps target holds steady. A target is not a lock.
