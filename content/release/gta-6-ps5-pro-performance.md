GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S. There is no 60 fps mode, no 40 fps mode and no performance toggle at launch. Rockstar put this in its Nov 10, 2026 pre-load post, as reported by The Shortcut, Notebookcheck, GTA BOOM and VGC. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported)

The PS5 Pro gets two upgrades, PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows, which Rockstar says are "only available on PlayStation 5 Pro". The frame rate stays at 30. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported)

## GTA 6 performance on every console

| Console | Frame rate | Extras | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PS5 | 30 fps | DualSense haptics, Tempest 3D Audio, faster loading | Not disclosed |
| PS5 Pro | 30 fps | Base PS5 features plus PSSR and ray-traced shadows | Not disclosed |
| Xbox Series X | 30 fps target, with tested dips | — | About 1440p internally in DF samples |
| Xbox Series S | 30 fps target; more frequent tested dips | Native; core ray-tracing features retained in DF tests | About 720p internally in DF samples |
| Xbox Cloud Gaming | Streams at Series S settings | Plays on Xbox One, from 1 AM ET Nov 19 | — |

Rockstar's site says GTA VI "Plays Best On PS5", and every trailer plus the 26-minute Extended Look was captured on a base PS5, not a Pro. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported) Digital Foundry found no sign of Sony's upscaler in the [[media/gta-6-launch-trailer|Oct 15 launch trailer]], which fits that. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> What you have watched is the standard console version.

## Does GTA 6 have 60 fps?

No. Two claims say otherwise and both conflict with Rockstar's post. gta6-life.com publishes a table with a 60 fps "Performance" mode, and an older Rock and Borys podcast rumour promised 30 and 60 fps modes. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> A "performance mode" settings screenshot did not come from Rockstar. Claims like these are tracked in [[leaks/index|leaks and rumours]].

## Launch tests: Series X and Series S are not equally steady

[Digital Foundry's original Xbox tech review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F3lxGD8HX2k) is now available. Oliver Mackenzie measured about **1440p internally on Series X** in sampled shots and **720p internally on Series S**. Series X generally held 30 fps in typical play, with drops in demanding chases, crowds and combat. Series S fell below target more often, including sustained mid-to-upper-20s traversal and combat, and around 20 fps in demanding tests. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (independent testing, Nov 18)

The findings supersede our earlier statement that all resolution figures were guesses. They are **measured samples**, not official fixed-resolution guarantees. The video does not establish every patch/build outcome, and a regular four-console comparison was still planned rather than completed in that video. Do not read the table as a ranked comparison of all four versions from one identical test run.

Mackenzie also measured roughly **180 ms input lag on his Series X setup**, including the wireless controller and display. That is setup-specific, not an engine-only value or a cloud measurement. Switching output refresh settings did not substantially change his measured result; a 120 Hz signal is not a 120 fps game mode.

The test finds key ray-tracing systems and strand hair retained on Series S, with lower-quality effects and assets. The original video is uncertain about the exact Series S upscaler; [Digital Foundry's later written article](https://www.digitalfoundry.net/features/why-didnt-rockstar-use-fsr-4-on-series-x-for-gta-6-microsofts-stats-have-the-answer) identifies FSR 4, while explicitly labeling its explanation of Rockstar's choice as conjecture. It is not an announcement of a future 40/60 fps mode.

## Rockstar explains the 30 fps choice

[TechRadar's November 18 report](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/why-no-40fps-mode-for-gta-6-rockstar-says-the-complexity-of-simulating-and-rendering-our-world-requires-it-to-carefully-balance-how-we-spend-every-millisecond-of-both-the-central-processor-and-the-graphics-processor) contains responses to questions the outlet sent directly to Rockstar. Senior vice president of technology **Jonathon Ashcroft** attributes the target to the time needed for intertwined world simulation and rendering across **both CPU and GPU**. His explanation uses an approximately **33-millisecond frame budget** and describes the chosen frame rate as a balance between presentation, simulation and gameplay. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (direct developer response published by TechRadar)

Technology production director **Marc Chambers** also describes Rockstar's tools and technology as custom-built. That explains the developer's design approach, not a promise that every console will always sustain the target.

This is stronger evidence for Rockstar's reasoning than an analyst's inference, but it still is **not a released-game benchmark**. It does not identify a single component as the sole bottleneck, establish per-console resolutions, or rule out all future optimization. The current launch offering has no 40 fps balanced mode. A separate direct response to Kotaku below goes further about the current-console outlook for later modes.

## Why is 40 fps harder than it sounds?

In [Digital Foundry Direct Weekly episode 290](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7PLhEMgXdQs), the team discusses the announced console targets from the 24:06 chapter. Richard Leadbetter explains the CPU and simulation budget behind the choice: a higher frame rate leaves less time to process the same dense world. This is **technical analysis**, not a benchmark of the released game. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

| Target | Time available for each frame |
| --- | --- |
| 30 fps | About 33.3 milliseconds |
| 40 fps | 25 milliseconds |
| 60 fps | About 16.7 milliseconds |

These are frame-time calculations, not measured GTA 6 results. Moving from 30 to 40 fps removes a quarter of the time available for each frame. Leadbetter argues that meeting that budget could require substantial optimization or cutbacks. Better upscaling can help image quality, but it does not by itself prove that the CPU can finish the simulation faster.

That is why a 120 Hz setting or a rumoured file reference is not evidence of a working 40 fps mode. Nor does this analysis prove a future patch is impossible. Wait for announced modes and repeatable tests of the final game, including frame pacing, rather than treating a target as a locked result.

## What does the PS5 Pro add?

PSSR (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution) is Sony's machine-learning upscaler, so the Pro should produce a sharper image at the same 30 fps. Ray-traced shadows are the only ray-tracing feature Rockstar lists for any console. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Xbox measurements now exist, but they do not by themselves quantify a PS5 Pro image-quality advantage. Keep platform-specific testing separate from the now-published [[release/gta-6-reviews-and-review-embargo|Nov 18 reviews]].

A ComicBook.com "PlayStation leak" before the official post promised ray-traced reflections, shadows and lighting on Pro. **Nov 19 update:** the earlier marketing list named Pro shadows only, which did not settle base ray tracing. Chambers' direct response to Kotaku now explicitly confirms ray-traced reflections and global illumination in the base presentation. The later developer statement supersedes our earlier uncertainty; see the future-mode section below. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (developer statement)

## Will a 40 fps mode arrive later?

**Do not buy current hardware expecting a later performance-mode patch.** [Kotaku's November 18 article](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-wont-get-a-60-fps-mode-even-on-ps5-pro-because-its-not-powerful-enough-2000755979) reports that Zack Zwiezen directly asked Rockstar about future **60 fps or 40 fps** modes. Rockstar's technology production director **Marc Chambers** says PS5 Pro's improved GPU and upscaling support higher visual fidelity, but its CPU is not powerful enough to run the game's complex simulation at higher frame rates. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (developer response published by Kotaku)

That is stronger evidence than our earlier launch-only wording or file-based speculation. It is Rockstar's stated position for **current-generation consoles**, including PS5 Pro. It does not mean frame pacing cannot improve through fixes, that every scene is already CPU-limited, or that a PC version or a later hardware port has been announced. Kotaku's final paragraphs speculate about those ports; we do not treat that speculation as a release plan.

Chambers also explicitly identifies **ray-traced reflections and global illumination in the base presentation**, with **ray-traced shadows and PSSR added on PS5 Pro**. That replaces our earlier uncertainty about whether the advertised Pro shadows established the base ray-tracing features. It is a developer description, separate from the measured Xbox samples above.

Notebookcheck's earlier 120 Hz output and PSSR-file references remain file-based reports, not proof of a hidden or forthcoming 40 fps mode. Patch downloads without official notes likewise do not establish a new mode; see [[release/gta-6-day-one-patch|patch and firmware tracking]].

## PlayStation Portal, cloud streaming and PC

The [current US PlayStation product listing](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10000730/), checked November 24, supports **PS5 and PS Portal cloud streaming with Premium** as well as Remote Play. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-owners-can-now-stream-the-game-to-ps5-ps-portal-with-ps-plus-premium) reports a working UK PS5 test and says GTA VI still requires ownership: it is not part of the Premium catalogue. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

Cloud streaming runs from Sony's servers. **Remote Play uses your own PS5**, so they are different routes. The listing does not promise PS5 Pro rendering, a higher frame rate or a particular cloud resolution. The local-console performance measurements on this page do not measure streaming quality or latency. See the [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming#can-i-cloud-stream-gta-6-on-ps5-or-ps-portal|cloud and Remote Play comparison]] for access limits. We have not tested every account or region.

There is no PC version, so there are no PC specs. GamesBeat reported a Q1 2027 PC target on Oct 13, citing four unnamed sources. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Any "GTA 6 PC requirements" list is invented, and unofficial installers should not be trusted. The newer reported Xbox-build leak and black-screen PC boot attempt do not establish a playable PC port.
