The GTA 6 Collector's Box, officially "The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection", costs $399.99 and contains no copy of the game. Rockstar's store listing says the game is "sold separately". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Rockstar Store listing, as reported)

PCWorld and some retailers call it the "Collector's Edition", but it is a merchandise box. The only game editions are Standard ($79.99) and Ultimate ($99.99), covered on [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]].

## How much is it, and where do you buy it?

| Region | Price | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US | $399.99 | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> |
| UK | £349.99 | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |
| Eurozone | €399.99 | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |
| Canada | CA$559.99 in one source, "$559 CAD" in another | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |

Adding the game brings the US total to $479.98 with Standard or $499.98 with Ultimate, before shipping. Shipping is not free on this item.

The box is sold only on the Rockstar Store, listed under Shop on rockstargames.com/VI. Rockstar limits it to one per account, requires buyers to be 18 or older, and lists quantities as limited. Orders ship from Nov 19, 2026. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported by VICE) It went on sale Sept 24, 2026. Rockstar has not said whether it has sold out or will be restocked, and we cannot see live stock.

## Is the Panini trading-card collection part of this box?

**No. It is a separate announced collection.** [Panini America's November 24 announcement](https://blog.paniniamerica.net/panini-and-rockstar-games-announce-grand-theft-auto-vi-trading-card-collection/), checked November 25, describes a partnership with Rockstar Games for the licensed **GTA VI: Sports Trading Card Collection**, planned for **December 2026**. It brings the game's collectible sports cards into a physical set. It is not a new game edition, an addition to the eleven-item box above or a game-access entitlement. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Panini announcement)

| Detail | What Panini announces |
| --- | --- |
| Release window | December 2026, without an exact day in the announcement |
| Four-pack box inserts | On average: two Rookies, one Retro, one Illustrated and four Special Silvers |
| Other parallels | Silver, Pink, Teal and Light Blue |
| Rare numbered Legend parallels | Bronze numbered to 49, Silver to 25 and 1-of-1 Gold |

The insert figures are **averages**, not a guarantee that each box has that exact mix. Numbered parallels are not a published probability of pulling one, a total print-run figure or evidence of resale value. Panini's release does not state a complete checklist, cards per pack, price, shipping terms or country-by-country availability. Its broader international distribution network does not establish that this collection will be sold in every country.

This page is an announcement summary, not a live stock or pre-order check. We have not ordered a box or verified a checkout. ## How did the box leak the map?

Between Nov 12 and 14, the Leonida map poster was seen on display at Selfridges Manchester, and a full photo then surfaced on Instagram, reportedly from a box sold early at that store. RockstarINTEL broke the story. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

New names read off the poster include Southwind (on Goose Key) and Pelican Valley (in Grassrivers). PC Games adds Lummox County and Moreau; GameRant warns that Moreau and Manzana may be misreads because the poster is folded. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Rockstar has not commented, and the poster is stylized art, not the in-game map. A GamesRadar+ headline floated a "UFO easter egg" on it. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>
