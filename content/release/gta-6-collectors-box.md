The **GTA 6 Collector's Box**, officially **"The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection"**, costs **$399.99** and **does not include the game**. Rockstar's store listing says the game is "sold separately". It is a box of Leonida-themed merchandise, not a game edition. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Rockstar Store listing, as reported)

PCWorld and some retailers call it the "Collector's Edition", which causes confusion. The only game editions are Standard and Ultimate; see [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]].

## How much is the GTA 6 Collector's Box?

| Region | Price | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |

Add the game and you are at $479.98 for Standard or $499.98 for Ultimate in the US, plus shipping, which is **not free** on this item.

## Where and how can I buy it?

- **Only on the Rockstar Store.** It is shown under the Shop section of rockstargames.com/VI.
- **One per Rockstar account.**
- **Buyers must be 18+.**
- **Limited quantities.**
- **Ships from Nov 19, 2026**, and is **not eligible for free shipping**.

<span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Rockstar Store listing, as reported by VICE)

It went on sale on **Sept 24, 2026**. Rockstar has not said whether it has sold out or whether more stock is coming. We have not been able to check live stock.

## What is in the GTA 6 Collector's Box?

Eleven items, per VICE's Sept 24 report: <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

1. **Macca the Gator figure** with a twist-open base. Macca is the star of *Macca & Friends*, an animated show inside the game; meet him on [[characters/macca-the-gator|Macca the Gator]].
2. **Oakley Frogskins** with Prizm Sapphire lenses and a pink GTA VI logo.
3. **Leonida Keys crossbody bag**, a nod to [[world/leonida-keys|the Keys]].
4. **New Era 9FORTY snapback.**
5. **Macca magnetic mirror/tray.**
6. **Chunkee the Manatee shot glass.**
7. **Vice City swizzle spoon.**
8. **Razor-blade-style keychain.**
9. **Leonida Keys enamel pin set** (9 pins).
10. **Sticker pack** (9 stickers).
11. **Double-sided Macca / Leonida map poster.**

The regular boxed game has **no paper map**. Push Square reports that Rockstar will post a free printable map on Nov 19 and sell a $0.99 physical map later <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>; see our [[guides/gta-6-printable-map|printable map guide]].

## The Collector's Box map leak

The map poster has already caused a leak. Between **Nov 12 and 14**, the Leonida map was seen on display at **Selfridges Manchester**, and a full photo then appeared on Instagram, reportedly from a box sold early at that store. RockstarINTEL broke it. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

New place names on the poster include **Southwind** (Goose Key) and **Pelican Valley** (Grassrivers). PC Games also reports **Lummox County** and **Moreau**, though some readings of the poster may be misreads. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> GamesRadar+ ran a headline about a possible "UFO easter egg" on the map. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>

Full coverage: [[news/2026-11-14-collectors-box-map-leak|the Collector's Box map leak]] and [[world/gta-6-leaked-map-and-counties|the leaked map and counties]].

## Is the Collector's Box worth it?

It depends on whether you want the objects. You get no in-game content, no game code and no exclusive digital items that we know of. The Oakleys and the New Era cap are real branded products, and the Macca figure is the centrepiece. If you want in-game extras, the $20 jump from Standard to Ultimate buys you 16 items. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>
