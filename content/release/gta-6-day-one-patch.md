GTA 6 already has its first update. **Version 1.002.000 began rolling out on Friday, Nov 13, 2026**, to people who had pre-loaded. It is about **1.07 GB on PS5** and about **2.18–2.2 GB on Xbox Series X|S**, and it is reported as mandatory. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (from console download screens)

**Rockstar has not published patch notes** and has not called it "the day-one patch". VICE and others are using that label. Our launch-week news write-up is on [[news/2026-11-13-gta-6-update-1-002|update 1.002]].

## How big is update 1.002?

| Platform | Size | Rollout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PS5 / PS5 Pro | ~1.07 GB | Staggered: about 50% of users first, the rest about 11 hours later |
| Xbox Series X\|S | ~2.18–2.2 GB | Not reported as staggered |

<span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

The Xbox figure is roughly double the PS5 one. Nobody has explained why. Different packaging per platform is a common reason, but that is our guess. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

## What does update 1.002 change?

**Officially: nothing has been said.** Rockstar has released no notes.

An insider going by **"Text Funds 2"** claims the update touches **story data, executables, levels, shaders and scripts**. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> That would make it a broad fix-up of the pre-load build rather than new content, but we cannot verify it.

## Is there a separate day-one patch on Nov 19?

Unknown. Rockstar has not said whether 1.002 is the launch-day version or whether another update arrives on Nov 19. Big launches often get a further update on release day. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> Keep auto-updates on and check again on launch night.

## What happened to the 50 GB day-one patch rumour?

Before pre-load, guesses of a **15–50 GB day-one patch** circulated widely. They were never confirmed, and the actual pre-launch update is about 1–2 GB. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> (as far as 1.002 goes)

That said, the game itself is huge. Rockstar says you need **up to 212 GB**, and Timesaver recommends clearing about **250 GB** to cover updates. See [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|pre-load and file size]] and our [[guides/gta-6-storage-space-guide|storage guide]].

## I don't have update 1.002 yet. What should I do?

- **PS5:** the rollout was staggered over about 11 hours, so wait, then check manually. Highlight the game, press Options, then Check for Update.
- **Xbox:** go to My games and apps, then Manage, then Updates.
- On both, leave automatic updates on and keep the console in **Rest Mode** (PS5) or **Sleep** (Xbox).

Because the update is reported as mandatory, you will likely need it before you can play at your [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|unlock time]]. Do it the night before, not at midnight.

## Does update 1.002 add a 60 fps mode?

There is nothing to suggest it. Rockstar says GTA 6 targets **30 fps on every console** with no performance mode at launch. Datamined hints at a future 40 fps mode are unconfirmed. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> See [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|PS5 Pro and performance]].
