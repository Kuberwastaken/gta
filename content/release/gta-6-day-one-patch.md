## November 23: post-launch update 01.002.002 / 1.0.2.2

[VICE's November 23 report](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-update-01-002-002-patch-notes/) describes the first update since launch, identifying **01.002.002 on PS5** and **1.0.2.2 on Xbox Series X|S**. It attributes the download and staged-rollout information to **Tex2 (@TexFunz2)**. We read the outlet's reproduction, not a console download screen or the original social post. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

| Platform | Reported version | Reported download |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PS5 / PS5 Pro | 01.002.002 | 603.8 MB |
| Xbox Series X\|S | 1.0.2.2 | About 1.6 GB |

[Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-update-01-002-002-all-changes-download-size/) reports the same download sizes. These are download reports, not total install sizes or a guarantee for every installed build and region.

### Why might the update not appear on PS5 yet?

VICE reproduces a three-wave plan: roughly 33% initially, 66% around three hours later and all users around six hours after the rollout began. This is a **reported PS5 schedule**, not a Rockstar or Sony service guarantee. The report does not establish an exact start time for each account, an equivalent Xbox wave plan or that every player has received it at the time of our check. Check the console normally; do not change account regions or reinstall the base game just to force a wave.

### What does the November 23 patch fix?

**Later November 23 update:** [Rockstar's official launch-update article](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/sN5pEdFYZdlvijKXd6xpJ/grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-updates-ps5-xbox-series-x-or-s), last updated November 23, now lists seven fixes. Our earlier same-day check found no notes in the Support listing; that status is now superseded. The first-party list confirms the changes below, while the version numbers and download sizes above remain attributed to console-update reporting. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (official patch notes)

| Area | What Rockstar says it fixed |
| --- | --- |
| Stability | Several issues that caused crashes |
| Weather | Weather remaining unchanged for extended periods |
| Autosave | An issue that prevented autosave from working correctly |
| Phone calls | Several calls failing to trigger, including calls that progress the story or unlock mission content |
| Wanted level | An issue that prevented the player from losing their wanted level |
| Gym | An issue that prevented use of the deadlift bar |
| Fences | Partners failing to hand over bags for sale to fences |

### What do the official notes not establish?

Rockstar does **not name the affected missions, phone calls, crash codes or save states**. The notes do not say whether every already-affected save will recover automatically. A general crash fix is not a promise that every PS5 CE-108255-1 report is resolved; a phone-call fix is not proof that every progression blocker has the same cause.

The autosave item confirms a bug fix, **not an announced redesign of every save trigger**. Keep checking the saved slot and use the [[guides/gta-6-tips-before-you-start#save-before-you-stop-playing|manual-save guidance]] rather than assuming every small activity now creates an autosave. We have not tested the update or recovered lost progress ourselves.

The seven-item list announces no new frame-rate mode, audio-desync fix or money-exploit change. It also does not prove that an unlisted exploit still works, that no other code changed or why Rockstar issued this build. The [[guides/gta-6-day-one-checklist|checklist]] separates the game update from PS5 system software. Earlier November 13 and 16 builds remain below as history.

## PS5 system software is separate from the game patch

The [November 18 post from @PS_Support_US](https://x.com/PS_Support_US/status/2122863683881619685), read at its original URL, says a PS5 system software update is required before playing GTA VI. The [@PlayStation account also shared that notice](https://x.com/PlayStation/status/2122870764760420863). [PCMag's hands-on check](https://uk.pcmag.com/games/168442/preloaded-gta-6-check-you-dont-need-any-extra-updates-before-playing) reports that its console had not installed the firmware while in rest mode. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Do not assume a completed game pre-load means the console is ready.

[Sony's GTA VI support page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/gta-vi-support/) separately directs players to **Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings** and says to keep system software up to date. It also lists automatic game updates and staying connected in rest mode as separate settings. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Sony support guidance)

Check the console's current system-update screen as well as GTA VI's game-update screen. **Firmware is not a GTA VI game update such as 01.002.002**. We are not assigning an unverified firmware version or promising that rest mode installs every update. No primary source recovered here explains a GTA-specific security purpose, so jailbreak and anti-piracy theories remain speculation.

## November 16: a second pre-launch update is reported

[VICE's Brent Koepp reports](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-second-day-one-patch-ps5-xbox/) that another update began reaching pre-loaded copies on November 16. The PS5 version is **01.002.001**, at around **602.5 MB**; the Xbox download is reported at around **1.6 GB**. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> These are not the November 13 file sizes below. VICE attributes the details to data accounts and reproduces a post from iGrandTheftAuto.com. We could not independently retrieve that original X post.

VICE reproduces this staged **PS5** timetable. Treat it as a reported rollout plan, not an official Sony guarantee or a reason to change your account region:

| Claimed share reached | UTC | India, IST |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 33% | Mon Nov 16, 10:00 AM | Mon Nov 16, 3:30 PM |
| 66% | Mon Nov 16, 10:00 PM | Tue Nov 17, 3:30 AM |
| 100% | Tue Nov 17, 10:00 AM | Tue Nov 17, 3:30 PM |

A console still showing the earlier version does not by itself mean your preload failed. Check for updates normally and keep automatic downloads enabled; do not reinstall a 200-plus-GB game merely because a reported wave has not reached you. The source does not give an equivalent staged Xbox timetable.

**What changed remains unknown.** We have not recovered official notes for this build. A small download does not prove a stability fix, new mission, frame-rate mode or early access. Calling it a second "day-one patch" is VICE's wording, not proof that this is the final update before the [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|November 19 unlock]].

## Earlier update: November 13

GTA 6's first update, version 1.002.000, began rolling out on Friday, Nov 13, 2026, to consoles that had pre-loaded. It is about **1.07 GB on PS5** and about 2.18 to 2.2 GB on Xbox Series X|S, and it is reported as mandatory. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> All of that comes from console download screens, because Rockstar had posted no notes for that build and had not called it "the day-one patch". VICE and KeenGamer use that label. Our news write-up is at [[news/2026-11-13-gta-6-update-1-002|update 1.002]].

## What was claimed about the first update?

No official change list for that earlier build was recovered. An insider posting as "Text Funds 2" claims the update touches story data, executables, levels, shaders and scripts. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> If accurate, that is a broad refresh of the pre-load build rather than new content.

No verified change list establishes that this update adds a 60 fps mode. Reporting on Rockstar's November 10 console targets describes 30 fps, PS5 Pro included. Datamined 120 Hz output and PSSR v13.60 strings hint at a future 40 fps mode, unconfirmed. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> See [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|PS5 Pro and performance]].

## How do the launch and post-launch updates differ?

The November 13 and 16 downloads above were pre-launch updates. The November 23 report is a separate post-launch build. Their similar download sizes do not make them the same patch, and the November 23 official notes should not be applied retroactively to those earlier builds.

## What happened to the 50 GB day-one patch?

Before pre-load opened, guesses of a 15 to 50 GB day-one patch circulated. None came from Rockstar, and the actual pre-launch update is 1 to 2 GB. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> (for 1.002) The base game is the big download: Rockstar says up to 212 GB, and Timesaver suggests clearing about 250 GB to leave room for updates. See [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|pre-load and file size]] and the [[guides/gta-6-storage-space-guide|storage guide]].

## How to get update 1.002 manually

On PS5, highlight GTA VI, press Options and choose Check for Update. On Xbox, go to My games and apps, then Manage, then Updates. On both, leave automatic updates on and keep the console in Rest Mode (PS5) or Sleep (Xbox) overnight.
