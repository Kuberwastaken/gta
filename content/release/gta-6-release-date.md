**GTA 6 releases on Thursday, November 19, 2026**, on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. Take-Two reconfirmed the date in its Nov 9, 2026 earnings release, which opens: "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away." On the call, executives said Rockstar has **finished development** and the marketing campaign is fully set. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

The game unlocks at **midnight local store time**, so in practice New Zealand starts at 11:00 UTC on Nov 18 and US West Coast players start at 9 PM PT on Nov 18. Every region is on our [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|release time by country]] page.

## Is GTA 6 delayed again?

**No.** There is no third delay. Take-Two has reaffirmed Nov 19 at every earnings report since the second delay:

| Date | Event | What Take-Two said |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feb 3, 2026 | Q3 FY26 | On track; marketing starts in summer. Physical-edition delay rumours: "That's not the plan." |
| May 21, 2026 | Q4 FY26 | FY27 "will establish new record levels of operating performance driven by the November 19th launch" |
| Aug 7, 2026 | Q1 FY27 | Date reaffirmed; pre-orders "unprecedented and astonishing" |
| Nov 9, 2026 | Q2 FY27 | "ten days away"; development finished |

Rumours of a slip to February 2027 or later, spread by 4chan posts and parody accounts through 2026, have been **debunked**. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> We track that history on [[leaks/gta-6-another-delay-rumour|the another-delay rumour page]].

## GTA 6 delay history

GTA 6 has moved twice. Both times Rockstar said it needed more time for quality.

| Announced | Change | Rockstar's reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dec 4, 2023 (Trailer 1) | Window set: "2025" | — |
| May 16, 2024 | Narrowed to "Fall of Calendar 2025" (Take-Two guidance) | — |
| **May 2, 2025** | Fall 2025 → **Tuesday, May 26, 2026** | "We need this extra time to deliver at the level of quality you expect and deserve." |
| **Nov 6, 2025** | May 26, 2026 → **Thursday, Nov 19, 2026** | Extra months to "finish the game with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve" |

Trailer 2 dropped on May 6, 2025, four days after the first delay. The second delay was posted on Rockstar Newswire and X, timed with Take-Two's earnings that day. Take-Two said the firing of 31 UK staff a week earlier was unrelated to the delay. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (company claim)

### When was the first delay announced?

Most outlets, and our own timeline research, date the first delay announcement to **May 2, 2025**. Gfinity dates it to **July 2025**. Every other source says May, and Trailer 2's May 6 date fits that, so we go with May 2. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> (the July date)

## What day of the week is the GTA 6 release?

**Thursday.** November 19, 2026 falls on a Thursday. Because of the midnight-Eastern US unlock, much of the US actually starts on Wednesday night, Nov 18.
