GTA 6 releases on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. Take-Two's Nov 9, 2026 earnings release opens with "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away", and on the call executives said Rockstar has **finished development** and the marketing campaign is set. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

The game unlocks at midnight local store time. New Zealand starts at 11:00 UTC on Nov 18, and the single US store timestamp (midnight Eastern) means West Coast players start at 9 PM PT on Wednesday, Nov 18. Every region is on the [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|release time by country]] page.

## Is GTA 6 delayed again?

No. Take-Two has repeated Nov 19 at every quarterly report since the second delay:

| Date | Report | What Take-Two said |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feb 3, 2026 | Q3 FY26 | On track; marketing starts in summer. On physical-edition delay rumours, Zelnick: "That's not the plan." |
| May 21, 2026 | Q4 FY26 | FY27 "will establish new record levels of operating performance driven by the November 19th launch" |
| Aug 7, 2026 | Q1 FY27 | Date reaffirmed; Zelnick calls pre-orders "unprecedented and astonishing" but adds "we haven't sold one unit yet" |
| Nov 9, 2026 | Q2 FY27 | "ten days away"; development finished; "we still aren't ready to declare victory" |

Zelnick also told IGN on Nov 9 that the game is not being delayed again. The February 2027 slip that 4chan posts and parody accounts pushed through 2026 is <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>; the history is on [[leaks/gta-6-another-delay-rumour|the another-delay rumour page]].

## GTA 6 delay history

| Announced | Change | Rockstar's wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dec 4, 2023 (Trailer 1) | Window set: "2025" | End card |
| May 16, 2024 | "Calendar 2025" narrowed to "Fall of Calendar 2025" | Take-Two guidance |
| May 2, 2025 | Fall 2025 to Tuesday, May 26, 2026 | "We are very sorry that this is later than you expected… we need this extra time to deliver at the level of quality you expect and deserve." |
| Nov 6, 2025 | May 26, 2026 to Thursday, Nov 19, 2026 | Extra months to "finish the game with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve" |

[[media/gta-6-trailer-2|Trailer 2]] dropped unannounced on May 6, 2025, four days after the first delay. The second delay went out on Rockstar Newswire and X on the same day as Take-Two's FY26 Q2 earnings, a week after Rockstar fired 31 UK staff. Take-Two said the firings were unrelated to the delay. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (company claim) The labor dispute is covered under [[timeline/rockstar-uk-firings-and-union|Rockstar UK firings and union]].

Gfinity dates the first delay to July 2025. Every other source says May 2, 2025, and the May 6 Trailer 2 date fits that, so the July date is wrong. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span> The full guidance language at each step is on [[timeline/gta-6-delays|GTA 6 delays]].

## What platforms is GTA 6 releasing on?

PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S run it natively. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> Xbox One owners can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming from 1 AM ET on Nov 19, after buying it; it is not on Game Pass. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> There is no PS4 version, no PC version and no Switch 2 announcement.
