GTA 6 reviews are **reported to go live on Wednesday, November 18, 2026**, one day before launch. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> **Rockstar has not confirmed the embargo**, so treat the date as strong but unofficial. As of Nov 15, there are **no reviews and no Metacritic score**. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (absence)

## Where does the Nov 18 date come from?

| Source | Claim | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **NateTheHate** (X, Oct 26, 2026) | Review codes are out, with an embargo on Nov 18 | <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> |
| **Insider Gaming** | Independently verified the date; codes went out at the end of October | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |
| **tbreak** (Oct 27) | Relays both | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |
| **Rockstar** | Has said nothing | — |

Two separate sources landing on the same date, one of them saying it checked independently, makes Nov 18 credible. But neither is Rockstar. Timing can still move.

## What time does the GTA 6 review embargo lift?

**Unknown.** One claim, from a source called BlackBate and relayed by Gizbot, says **6–7 AM EST**, which it gives as 12:01–1:01 PM CET. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> If accurate, that would be about 11:00–12:00 UTC on Nov 18, or 11 AM to noon in London.

Note that this would land almost exactly when New Zealand players start playing: NZ unlocks at **11:00 UTC on Nov 18**. See [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|release time by country]].

## Did every outlet get a review code?

Reportedly not. tbreak says **some outlets and YouTubers may have to wait until launch**. YouTuber **Shirrako** may have a review code. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Expect a mix of full reviews on Nov 18 and "review in progress" pieces through launch week.

## What will reviews tell us that Rockstar has not?

A lot of open questions should be answered once reviews land:

- **Story length.** Rockstar North's Rob Nelson reportedly said his playthrough took about 80 hours, but outlets disagree on whether that includes side content. See [[guides/gta-6-how-long-to-beat|how long to beat]].
- **Real-world performance**: how stable the [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|30 fps target]] is, and console resolutions.
- **How the new systems feel**, such as the [[gameplay/gta-6-wanted-system-and-heat|wanted and heat system]] and character switching.
- **Radio**: Edge reported 23 stations; Rockstar has revealed six. See [[music/gta-6-radio-stations|radio stations]].

## How to avoid GTA 6 spoilers before launch

Nov 18 is the risky day. Reviews may land, New Zealand players will be streaming, and earlier leaks are still around:

- The **CyberLeek** leaks included about 25 minutes of spoiler-heavy footage, possibly from an older build, which Engadget warned about on Oct 8. Rockstar's legal team has been removing clips. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> See [[leaks/gta-6-cyberleek-2026-leaks|the CyberLeek leaks]].
- New Zealand unlocks about **18 hours before** the US store.
- Mute keywords like "GTA 6 ending" and character names on social media.
- Read reviews that promise to be spoiler-free, and skip video thumbnails.
