GTA 6 launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S, and Xbox One owners can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming. Take-Two said on Nov 9 that development is finished. Pages in this section grade every claim, because launch week has produced plenty of wrong numbers.

## When can I play GTA 6?

At midnight local store time on Nov 19. New Zealand goes first at 11:00 UTC on Nov 18 and the UK at midnight GMT. The whole US store unlocks at midnight Eastern, so Pacific players start at 9 PM on Wednesday, Nov 18; Xbox Cloud Gaming follows at 1 AM ET. Every region is in the [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|release time by country]] table.

## What has to be done before launch?

Three things. Finish the pre-load, open since Nov 10, with up to 212 GB free. Install update 1.002.000, about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.2 GB on Xbox, rolling out since Nov 13. If you bought a box, redeem the code inside, because there is no disc. The [[guides/gta-6-day-one-checklist|day-one checklist]] covers the order.

## Which launch numbers are wrong?

| You may have read | Correct |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-load starts Nov 12 | Nov 10; Rockstar moved it on Nov 3 |
| 60 fps performance mode | 30 fps target on every console, PS5 Pro included |
| Physical disc confirmed | Download code in a box; Zelnick hasn't ruled out a later disc |
| $100 base price | $79.99 Standard; $99.99 is Ultimate |
| Xbox download is 225 GB | Same ~210 GB file, counted in decimal units |
| Reviews confirmed for Nov 18 | Reported by NateTheHate and Insider Gaming, not Rockstar |
