Four days to go. **GTA 6 launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026**, on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S, with Xbox One players able to stream it via Xbox Cloud Gaming. Take-Two confirmed on Nov 9 that development is finished. This section covers everything about getting the game running on day one, and we flag which numbers are confirmed and which are not.

## When can I play GTA 6?

At **midnight local store time on Nov 19**. New Zealand goes first, at 11:00 UTC on Nov 18. The UK unlocks at midnight GMT. The **whole US store unlocks at midnight Eastern**, so Pacific players start at **9 PM on Wednesday, Nov 18**, and Xbox Cloud Gaming starts at 1 AM ET. Every region is in our [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|release time by country]] table.

## What do I need before launch?

- **The download.** Pre-load opened Nov 10. Rockstar says you need **up to 212 GB**; Xbox's 225 GB listing is the same size in decimal units.
- **The patch.** Update 1.002.000 (about 1.07 GB on PS5) has been rolling out since Nov 13.
- **A redeemed code** if you bought a box. There is no disc.

Our [[guides/gta-6-day-one-checklist|day-one checklist]] walks through it step by step.

## Which numbers are right?

The launch has produced a lot of stale or wrong figures. The quick corrections:

- Pre-load is **Nov 10**, not Nov 12.
- There is **no 60 fps mode**; every console targets 30 fps.
- There is **no disc**, despite older "disc confirmed" headlines.
- The base price is **$79.99**, not the long-rumoured $100.
- The review embargo of **Nov 18** is reported, not confirmed by Rockstar.
