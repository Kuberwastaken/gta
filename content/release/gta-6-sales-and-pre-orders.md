GTA 6 is the most pre-ordered game in the history of both console stores. On Nov 11, Sony called it "the most pre-ordered game in the history of PlayStation Store", and Microsoft called it "the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported by GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer)

**November 23 update:** this guide previously said Take-Two had not published a count. We have now read its **November 20 release**, which reports 32.5 million pre-release units and $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales. The announcement is dated November 20, not today. Older analyst snapshots below are historical context, not the latest company result.

## What are the official launch sales figures?

[Take-Two's November 20 news release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/grand-theft-auto-vi-shatters-entertainment-box-office-records) reports the following. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (company disclosure, estimates)

| Measure | Company figure | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day-one retail sales | $3.79 billion | Company estimates through the end of November 19 worldwide |
| Units sold pre-release | 32.5 million | A pre-release unit total, not a count of additional launch-day copies |
| Record claim | Biggest entertainment launch; pre-release unit record across platforms | Take-Two's comparison, not an independent audit by this site |

**Do not add pre-release units to the retail-sales dollars, derive a launch-day unit total by dividing by one edition's price, or treat the retail figure as net profit.** The release does not give a full platform split, a refund-adjusted methodology or a bridge from retail spending to Take-Two's accounting measures. It also does not supply a launch-weekend cumulative total. The third-party estimate below is not an update issued by Take-Two.

## What does Alinea estimate after GTA 6's first week?

These are **Alinea Analytics estimates**, not a new Rockstar or Take-Two disclosure, and we have not independently audited the model or underlying transactions. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (third-party estimates)

| Measure | Alinea's November 27 report | Scope and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copies sold | 53 million | Cumulative week-one estimate, not 53 million additional copies after the weekend |
| Revenue | $4.7 billion | Analyst revenue estimate, not profit or Take-Two net bookings |
| PS5 share | 75.7% of estimated copies | Unit share within Alinea's model, not Sony's unique-player count |
| Xbox share / copies | 24.3%; about 13 million | Same report and model, not Microsoft's platform disclosure |
| Longer playtime | Nearly 7M players over 50 hours; around 250K over 100 hours | Estimated engagement thresholds, not completion counts or a required campaign length |

The post also puts the United States at **33.5% of estimated PS5 copies**. That is not a share of all-platform sales. Its opening total is described as a week-one result; the **November 19–25** dates appear explicitly in its separate comparison of other games below. We do not assign a more precise hour or timezone cutoff to the 53 million figure than the post provides.

The earlier 44.9 million weekend estimate remains below as a dated snapshot. **Do not add it to 53 million**, combine it with Sensor Tower's separate 47.8 million model, or subtract Take-Two's pre-release count to claim verified new purchases. Alinea's newer post gives Xbox a 25.2% share for November 22, while its earlier post used 24.9%. The public posts do not reconcile that difference, so this guide preserves each dated account rather than presenting every percentage as one consistent historical series.

### Did GTA 6 reduce spending on other games?

Alinea also examines **11 selected recent premium releases on PlayStation**, none in PS Plus at the time according to its account. Their combined estimated revenue fell **24%, from $19.8M to $15.0M**, in the November 19–25 comparison week. Excluding *Phantom Blade Zero*, which it identifies as an outlier, the remaining ten fell **12%, from $13.4M to $11.8M**.

That is **a selected sample, not all PlayStation spending or the global games market**. Elliott explicitly notes normal post-release sales decay, changing prices and Black Friday discounts. Some sampled games grew. The comparison does not isolate how much GTA VI caused, establish that every lost purchase moved to it, or prove a permanent decline. The post's explanations involving new-console buyers and co-marketing remain the analyst's interpretation, not a controlled causal test or a new hardware-unit disclosure.

These are **Alinea Analytics estimates**, not a Rockstar or Take-Two four-day disclosure. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (third-party estimates)

| Revenue | More than $4 billion | Four-day sold-through revenue estimate, not profit or net bookings |

The post puts about 76% of that Sunday's daily active players on PS5. That share measures **players**, while 75.1% above measures estimated **copies**. Neither is Sony's separate four-day unique-player disclosure below. A daily count, a multi-day unique-player count and a unit total cannot be added together or treated as interchangeable.

Do not add the 44.9 million to Take-Two's 32.5 million pre-release units: the later cumulative estimate is not a count of additional purchases after launch. It is useful evidence of PS5 engagement during a four-day window, separate from the November 19 retail-sales cutoff above. We have not independently audited SIE's data.

## What did GTA VI earn at UK boxed retail?

**November 27 source check:** [NielsenIQ's November 26 release](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2026/gta-vi-sends-uk-games-retail-sales-soaring-99-to-142m-in-launch-week/) estimates **£32 million in GTA VI physical sales** during the **November 19–21** launch period. Its definition includes **boxed software and code-in-a-box games sold through retailers**, not just games containing a disc. It excludes purchases from digital storefronts such as PlayStation Store, Xbox Store and Nintendo eShop. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (NielsenIQ retail-panel estimates)

| Measure | NielsenIQ figure | Scope and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GTA VI boxed/code-in-box revenue | £32 million | UK launch period, November 19–21; not all UK channels or worldwide revenue |
| Share of weekly physical software revenue | About 74% | GTA VI's share of the UK physical-software market that week, not its global platform share |
| GTA VI first-week sales on launch day | 88% | NIQ's reported share within its retail measure, not 88% of all worldwide purchases |
| Historical rank | No.17 by first-week boxed revenue since 1995 | GTA V is No.1 and GTA IV No.11 in this revenue comparison |
| All boxed software | About £43 million, up 409% week on week | All games in the covered UK retail market, not GTA VI alone |
| Combined games retail | £142 million, up 99% week on week | Physical/code-in-box software, console hardware and gaming accessories combined |

NIQ describes these as estimates for the total traditional UK retail market, based on its retailer panel. The reporting week is **November 15–21, 2026**, with GTA VI's three-day launch period ending November 21. Black Friday falls in the following reporting week, so this is not a Black Friday sales result. We have read the issuer's methodology notes, not audited the underlying retailer data.

### Why do reports say biggest physical launch since 2019?

[Christopher Dring's November 26 report for The Game Business](https://www.thegamebusiness.com/p/no-disc-no-problem-gta-6-is-the-biggest) compares **copies**, saying the last UK game to sell that many in its first week was **FIFA 20 in September 2019**. NIQ's own release instead ranks **boxed revenue** and identifies **FIFA 19 in 2018** as the most recent title above GTA VI on that measure. These are differently worded comparisons with different metrics. This guide does not merge them into one record claim or infer an exact UK unit total from the £32 million figure.

The Game Business also reports more than one million boxed copies in France over three days and a Spanish physical-launch record. Those are its separately attributed regional reports, not numbers supplied in the UK NIQ release or a basis for extrapolating worldwide sales.

### What do the hardware increases establish?

NIQ reports **PS5 sales up 46%** and **Xbox Series sales up 75%** week on week, alongside a **38% increase in console hardware sales overall**. These are growth rates from different baselines, not console-unit totals or evidence that Xbox outsold PS5. The release also reports accessory demand increases. They show a retail uplift around launch, not proof that every extra purchase was caused only by GTA VI.

Do not add the £142 million market total to GTA VI's £32 million: the former includes the latter and covers hardware and accessories as well. Neither should be added to Take-Two's worldwide day-one disclosure or used as a new cumulative company sales figure. The dates, channels, currencies and issuers differ.

## What has Take-Two said?

The November 20 company figures above replace the earlier no-count position. **Before launch**, Take-Two used the following descriptions without supplying a unit count. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (historical statements)

- Zelnick in the Nov 9 release: "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away and, with pre-orders continuing to reach unprecedented levels, we expect to deliver an historic year."
- On the Nov 9 call, pre-orders were tracking "far in excess" of initial expectations, yet Take-Two declined to raise guidance or give FY28 guidance. Zelnick: "we still aren't ready to declare victory."
- CFO Lainie Goldstein: "the level of pre-orders generated is extraordinary."
- Zelnick to CNBC on Nov 14: "our expectations were high and they are being vastly exceeded so far."

Back on Aug 7, Zelnick called pre-orders "unprecedented and astonishing" and added, "we haven't sold one unit yet."
