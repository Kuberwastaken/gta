GTA 6 is not on PC at launch. On Nov 19, 2026 it releases on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Rockstar has announced no PC version, store page or system requirements. Trailer 1 in December 2023 already listed PS5 and Xbox Series X|S with no PC. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (absence)

The only concrete date comes from GamesBeat. On Oct 13, 2026, citing four unnamed sources, it reported a PC release planned for Q1 2027. Rockstar did not respond. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

Q1 2027 means January to March, so two to four months after the console launch, if the report holds. The [[leaks/gta-6-pc-at-launch-rumour|PC at launch rumour]] page tracks how the claim evolved.

## What has Take-Two said about PC?

Strauss Zelnick has said any disc or PC news would come from Rockstar, not Take-Two, per Insider Gaming. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (exec quote, as reported)

On the Aug 7, 2026 earnings call, Zelnick said PC can now be "40% or 50%" of sales for multiplatform titles. He was speaking about the industry in general, not announcing a GTA VI port. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Motley Fool transcript) Take-Two's FY27 guidance of $8.0–8.2B in net bookings, reiterated Nov 9, does not split out a PC release.

## Can I play GTA 6 on PC with Xbox Cloud Gaming?

No. Cloud support at launch covers Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles only, starting 1 AM ET on Nov 19. The fan aggregator gta6times reported on Oct 7 that PC streaming was off the table, and Dexerto reports Xbox's Matthew Ball confirmed it. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Details are on the [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|cloud gaming page]].

## Does the reported Xbox-build leak mean GTA 6 is playable on PC?

**No playable PC port is established by this report.** [GameSpot's November 24 article](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-hacker-releases-full-xbox-build-and-pc-modders-are-already-trying-to-get-it-running/), by Darryn Bonthuys, reports an Xbox-build release attributed to CyberLeek and an unofficial PC boot attempt described through Eurogamer. GameSpot says the attempt reaches a black screen after starting. We have not downloaded, authenticated or run the files, and do not link to them. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

### Later November 24 claim: limited in-game interaction, not verified here

A [newer DSOGaming report by John Papadopoulos](https://www.dsogaming.com/news/grand-theft-auto-6-unofficial-pc-port-cgbj-now-reaches-in-game/) says a private project called **CGBJ** can reach an in-game state with controller movement, NPC interaction and the menu. The same article describes missing lighting and poor performance, says the project has no public download and explicitly warns that the footage **could be fake**. Its mention of another account's claimed verification is not an independent authentication by this site. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>

That means the earlier GameSpot black-screen account should not be read as the latest claimed ceiling for every project. It does **not** establish a verified playable PC port, complete missions, stability, safe files, a public release or a changed Rockstar schedule. We have not watched or authenticated the underlying footage or accessed the projects. We link only to the reporting, not its code, file or setup destinations.

A console build is not a released PC port. The group's confidence and the article's suggestion that this could pressure Rockstar are not evidence that the official schedule has changed. The older **Q1 2027** report remains separate and unconfirmed. Rockstar's [current out-now page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/out-now), rechecked November 24, names PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, not PC.
