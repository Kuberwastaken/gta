**GTA 6 is not on PC at launch.** On Nov 19, 2026 it releases on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Rockstar has announced **no PC version, no store page and no system requirements**. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (absence)

The one concrete report: **GamesBeat**, citing four unnamed sources in October 2026, said a **PC release is planned for Q1 2027**. Rockstar did not comment. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

Q1 2027 would mean January to March 2027. Rockstar's history is to release on PC after consoles, but the gap has varied, and nothing is official. Our [[leaks/gta-6-pc-at-launch-rumour|PC at launch rumour]] page tracks the claims over time.

## What has Take-Two said about PC?

- **Strauss Zelnick** has said any disc or PC news "would come from Rockstar", not from Take-Two. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (exec quote, as reported by Insider Gaming)
- On the **Aug 7, 2026** earnings call, Zelnick said PC can now be "40% or 50%" of sales for multiplatform titles. That is a general industry comment, not a GTA VI PC announcement. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Motley Fool transcript)

Reading between the lines, Take-Two clearly values PC sales. That is our inference, not a statement. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

## Can I play GTA 6 on PC with Xbox Cloud Gaming?

**No.** Xbox Cloud Gaming support at launch covers **Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S** only. There is **no PC or browser cloud streaming**, which Dexerto reports Xbox's Matthew Ball confirmed. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Details on our [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|cloud gaming page]].

## "GTA 6 PC download": every one is fake

There is no PC build to download. Anything offering a GTA 6 PC download, crack, "early access installer" or "beta key" is fake, and malware and crypto wallet drainers are actively spreading under the GTA 6 name. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

- Do not run any GTA 6 ".exe" from outside an official store.
- Do not connect a crypto wallet to any "GTA 6" site.
- Leaked console footage, like the [[leaks/gta-6-cyberleek-2026-leaks|CyberLeek clips]], is not a playable build either.

## What will GTA 6 PC system requirements be?

Unknown. Rockstar has published none, and specs circulating online are invented. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> On console the game targets 30 fps, even on the [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|PS5 Pro]], and needs up to 212 GB, which hints that a PC version will be demanding. That is a guess. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

If you want to play at launch, console is the only option. If you only play on PC, the best information right now says early 2027 at the soonest, with no guarantee.
