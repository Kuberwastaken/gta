Every copy of GTA 6 bought before Nov 20, 2026 comes with the Vintage Vice City Pack, whatever the edition or format. That includes the boxed code and launch-day purchases. Digital pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S also get a free month of GTA+. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Pre-orders opened at midnight local time on June 25, 2026. A fan wiki dates them to Sept 10 via PlayStation Direct; Sept 10 was actually the pre-order date for the Limited Edition DualSense controllers. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>

## What is in the Vintage Vice City Pack?

The contents below come from Consequence, VICE, gtasix.org and Xbox Wire. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Two-tone '55 Vapid Stanier | Classic sedan; see [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|GTA 6 vehicles]] |
| Shore Court Garage | Rockstar places it "a stone's throw from" Ocean Beach in [[world/vice-city|Vice City]] |
| 1980s Vice City outfits and hairstyles | For Jason and Lucia, including a pastel linen suit |
| Tropical-pattern weapon skin | "Inspired by Tommy Vercetti's iconic palm tree button-up" |

Every item points back to the 2002 *Vice City*. Rockstar's other Vercetti references, from the Ocean View Hotel to the Vercetti Estate revolvers in Ultimate, are collected in [[media/gta-6-easter-eggs-in-trailers|trailer easter eggs]]. Whether Tommy himself appears is a separate question, tracked on the [[leaks/tommy-vercetti-return-rumour|Vercetti return rumour]] page.

## What is the deadline?

Rockstar says "all pre-orders and purchases before Nov 20". The Xbox Store says "prior to 19 November 2026 23:59:59". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> Both include launch-day purchases within the applicable store cutoff. The Xbox listing does not name the time zone for its cutoff, so a late-night Nov 19 purchase is cutting it close.

Rockstar has not said whether the pack will be sold separately after Nov 20.

## Who gets the free GTA+ month, and how do you claim it?

Digital pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S qualify. The boxed copy does not. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Xbox Wire; Rockstar Support as quoted)

| Platform | Terms | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Xbox | Redeem within 180 days of placing the pre-order, once per platform account, not on restricted child accounts. Claim via Store, then profile, then "Offers & credits". | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> |
| PS5 | Sony counts eligible PS Store digital Standard or Ultimate purchases on or before Nov 19. Claim by Mar 31, 2027 on the purchasing account; confirmation email and GTA+ product-page offer. Upgrade alone excluded. | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> |

[Sony confirms](https://www.playstation.com/support/games/gta-vi-offer-terms) the PS5 month continues as a monthly subscription at the regular price until canceled. Its claim screen shows the next payment date; cancel through Subscriptions Management before that date if you do not want to pay. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> For Xbox, read the platform's terms when claiming. A rounded US price from press coverage is not a universal renewal charge.

GTA+ is a membership for GTA Online on GTA V plus a back catalogue of Rockstar games, with a monthly GTA$ deposit reported at GTA$500,000. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> **November 19 correction:** it does now offer optional GTA VI story-mode benefits, explicitly advertised on [Rockstar's GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI). The [[guides/gta-6-gta-plus-explained|GTA+ explainer]] separates reported perks from unresolved expiry terms. This does not announce a GTA VI online mode, which remains [[release/gta-6-gta-online-status|a separate question]]. Full terms are in the [[guides/gta-6-gta-plus-explained|GTA+ explainer]].

## Was the Stanier a Standard-only bonus?

No. VICE's June 24 article lists the Stanier and weapon skins as Standard pre-order bonuses and adds pistols, a Dinka Enduro and a Crest Kayak to Ultimate. That conflicts with Rockstar Support's wording as quoted by gtasix.org, which gives the pack to all editions. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>

## Is Ultimate a pre-order bonus?

No. Ultimate is a separate $99.99 edition with 16 extra items, available any time, or as a $20 upgrade on Xbox.
