Every copy of GTA 6 bought **before Nov 20, 2026** comes with the **Vintage Vice City Pack**, whether it is Standard or Ultimate, digital or the boxed code. **Digital** pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox also get **a free month of GTA+**. So if you buy on launch day, you still get the pack. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Pre-orders opened at midnight local time on **June 25, 2026**. (A fan wiki's claim that they opened on Sept 10 via PlayStation Direct is wrong: Sept 10 was when the [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|limited-edition DualSense controllers]] went up for pre-order.) <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>

## What is in the Vintage Vice City Pack?

Per Consequence, VICE, gtasix.org and Xbox Wire: <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

| Item | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Two-tone '55 Vapid Stanier | A classic sedan; see [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|GTA 6 vehicles]] |
| Shore Court Garage | A garage near Ocean Beach in [[world/vice-city|Vice City]] |
| 1980s Vice City outfits and hairstyles | Exclusive looks for Jason and Lucia, including a pastel linen suit |
| Tropical-pattern weapon skin | Rockstar says it is "inspired by Tommy Vercetti's iconic palm tree button-up" |

The pack leans hard on the 2002 *Vice City*. The Vercetti nods are part of a pattern in Rockstar's own material; we collect them in [[media/gta-6-easter-eggs-in-trailers|trailer easter eggs]] and track the [[leaks/tommy-vercetti-return-rumour|Tommy Vercetti return rumour]] separately.

## What is the deadline?

- **Rockstar:** "all pre-orders and purchases before Nov 20". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as quoted)
- **Xbox Wire:** "before 19 November 2026 at 23:59:59". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Those mean the same thing: buy any time up to the end of launch day. Xbox Wire does not say which time zone the 23:59:59 cutoff uses, so if you are buying late on Nov 19, do not leave it to the last minute.

## Who gets the free month of GTA+?

**Digital pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.** The boxed copy does not include it. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Xbox Wire; Rockstar Support as quoted)

| Platform | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| Xbox | Redeem within **180 days**, once per platform account; not available to restricted child accounts. Claim under Store, then your profile, then "Offers & credits". <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> |
| PS5 | Sony reportedly also counts PS Store digital purchases made **through Nov 19**, with the month claimable until **Mar 31, 2027**. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |

### Does it auto-renew?

**Reportedly yes, at about $8 a month**, unless you cancel. games.gg flagged it. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> If you do not want to keep paying, turn off auto-renew right after you redeem.

### What does GTA+ actually give you?

GTA+ is Rockstar's membership for **GTA Online on GTA V**, plus a back catalogue of Rockstar games. It does nothing inside GTA 6's single-player story. Rockstar has not said whether GTA+ will carry over to any future GTA VI online mode. Our [[guides/gta-6-gta-plus-explained|GTA+ explainer]] covers the benefits and the fine print.

## Did VICE's list say the Stanier was a Standard bonus?

Yes, and it is probably wrong. VICE's June 24 article lists the Stanier and the weapon skins as Standard pre-order bonuses and adds pistols, a Dinka Enduro and a Crest Kayak to the Ultimate list. That does not match Rockstar Support's wording as quoted by gtasix.org. Stick with the pack contents above. <span class="tag-debunked">debunked</span>

## Is the Ultimate Edition a pre-order bonus?

No. Ultimate is a separate $99.99 edition with 16 extra items, and you can buy it any time or upgrade later for the difference ($20 on Xbox). See [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]] and our [[guides/gta-6-which-edition-should-you-buy|which edition to buy]] guide.

## What we're watching

- Whether the Vintage Vice City Pack is sold separately after Nov 20. Rockstar has not said.
- Whether the GTA+ month links to a future GTA VI online mode, which is [[release/gta-6-gta-online-status|not announced]].
