GTA 6 is not coming to Switch 2 at launch, and no Switch 2 version has been announced. On Nov 19, 2026 it releases on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S, with Xbox One covered by cloud streaming. Rockstar's site lists only PlayStation and Xbox. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (absence)

## What each company has said

| Who | What they said | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strauss Zelnick, Take-Two CEO (Nov 9, 2026) | Platform "information always comes from our labels", meaning Rockstar | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported by Variety) |
| Devon Pritchard, Nintendo of America | Asked about GTA 6, told people to ask Rockstar and Take-Two | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported by GameSpot) |
| NateTheHate, insider (2025) | Rockstar ran "tests to bring it to Switch 2, but tests don't always equal release" | <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> |
| Rockstar | Nothing on Switch 2 | — |

No company has ruled it out and none has confirmed it. Zelnick's Nov 9 answer came on the same earnings call where he said pre-orders were running "far in excess" of expectations, and he gave the same deflection he uses for disc and PC questions. The only positive signal is NateTheHate's report, and he attached the warning himself.

## Could Switch 2 run GTA 6?

Nobody outside Rockstar knows. What Rockstar has published about the current consoles sets the bar:

| Spec | Current consoles |
| --- | --- |
| Frame rate | 30 fps target on every console, PS5 Pro included |
| Storage | Up to 212 GB free space |
| Lowest native hardware | Xbox Series S |
| Cloud version | Streams at Series S settings |

A Switch 2 version would have to scale below the Series S and fit a 212 GB install. That is a large gap to close, but not one we can call impossible. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> Details on [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|console performance]] and [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|file size]].

## What comes after the console launch?

The next platform on the radar is PC, not Nintendo. GamesBeat reported on Oct 13, 2026, citing four unnamed sources, that a PC version is planned for Q1 2027; Rockstar has not confirmed it. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> See [[release/gta-6-pc-release-date|GTA 6 PC release date]]. Any Switch 2 announcement would come from Rockstar, per Zelnick, and our [[leaks/index|rumours tracker]] logs claims until then.

## Can I play GTA 6 on a Switch 2 another way?

No. Xbox Cloud Gaming streams GTA 6 only to Xbox One and Series X|S consoles from 1 AM ET on Nov 19, with no PC or browser streaming, so there is no path to a Nintendo device. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> PlayStation Remote Play needs your own PS5 as the source, so it is no workaround for Switch 2 owners. See [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|cloud gaming]].

To play this year you need a PS5, an Xbox Series X|S, or an Xbox One with cloud access, and the game at $79.99 (Standard) or $99.99 (Ultimate). The full platform list is on the [[release/index|release hub]], and the state you'd be playing in is covered under [[world/index|Leonida]].
