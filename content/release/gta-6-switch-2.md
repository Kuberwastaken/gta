**GTA 6 is not coming to Switch 2 at launch, and no Switch 2 version has been announced.** On Nov 19, 2026 it releases on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S, with Xbox One via cloud streaming. Rockstar's own site lists only PlayStation and Xbox. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (absence)

## What has each company said?

| Who | What they said | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strauss Zelnick**, Take-Two CEO (Nov 9, 2026) | Platform "information always comes from our labels", i.e. from Rockstar | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported by Variety) |
| **Devon Pritchard**, Nintendo of America | Asked about GTA 6, told people to ask Rockstar and Take-Two | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (as reported by GameSpot) |
| **NateTheHate**, insider (2025) | Rockstar ran "tests to bring it to Switch 2, but tests don't always equal release" | <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> |
| **Rockstar** | Nothing on Switch 2 | — |

Nobody has said no. Nobody has said yes. The only positive signal is a 2025 insider report about tests, and even that came with a warning.

## Could Switch 2 run GTA 6?

We do not know. Consider what Rockstar has said about the current consoles:

- GTA 6 targets **30 fps on every console**, including the PS5 Pro. See [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|performance]].
- It needs **up to 212 GB** of storage. See [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|file size]].
- The lowest-spec native version is the **Xbox Series S**.

Any Switch 2 version would have to scale below the Series S. That is a big ask, but not something we can rule out. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

## Will GTA 6 come to Switch 2 later?

Rockstar has brought earlier games to Nintendo consoles, but there is no GTA VI announcement and no date. Rockstar's next platform move appears to be PC: GamesBeat reports a **Q1 2027** target, which Rockstar has not confirmed. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> See [[release/gta-6-pc-release-date|GTA 6 PC release date]].

If a Switch 2 version is announced, per Zelnick it will come from Rockstar. We will update this page and the [[leaks/index|rumours tracker]] when it does.

## Can I play GTA 6 on a Switch 2 another way?

Not officially. Xbox Cloud Gaming at launch covers Xbox One and Series X|S consoles only, with **no PC or browser streaming**, so there is no route to a Nintendo device. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> See [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|cloud gaming]].
