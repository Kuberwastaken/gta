GTA 6 pre-load opened on Tuesday, November 10, 2026: at midnight local time on PS5, and at one worldwide moment, 00:00 GMT, on Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar says you need **up to 212 GB of free space**. A finished pre-load does not let you play early; the game stays locked until it [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|unlocks in your region]] on Nov 19.

## When did GTA 6 pre-load start?

Rockstar's Nov 3 Newswire post, "Grand Theft Auto VI Pre-Load Begins on November 10", set the dates. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

| Platform | Pre-load start | In other time zones |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PS5 / PS5 Pro | Midnight local store time, Tue Nov 10 | New Zealand first, at 11:00 UTC on Mon Nov 9 |
| Xbox Series X\|S | 12:00 AM GMT, Tue Nov 10, worldwide | 7 PM ET / 4 PM PT Mon Nov 9; 5:30 AM IST Nov 10 |

PS5 rolled region by region, like the launch itself. Xbox opened everywhere at once.

Rockstar first gave Nov 12 when pre-orders opened in June, then moved pre-load two days earlier on Nov 3 without touching the release date. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> Consequence's June pre-order guide, VICE's Oct 15 launch-trailer article and a Tech Times piece from Nov 9 still say Nov 12. They are out of date.

Rockstar marked the opening with a 30-second "Pre-Load Now" video on Nov 10, captured on PS5 with PlayStation branding. GameRant calls it "Trailer 4"; the bigger October video is covered under [[media/gta-6-launch-trailer|the launch trailer]].

## How big is GTA 6?

Rockstar's Nov 3 figure is "up to 212 GB" of free space, "depending on device", on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Series S, with no per-console split. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> The download screens show this: <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

| Console | Download | Installed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PS5 / PS5 Pro | ~209.8 GB (209.75 GB on the first pre-load screens) | ~212 GB |
| Xbox Series X\|S, worldwide | 225.34 GB in the store; ~209.86 GB on the console | — |
| Xbox, Japan package | About 1.2 GB smaller than worldwide | — |

The PS5 numbers come from RockstarINTEL (credited to @MrDalekJD) via Timesaver.gg and from GTA BOOM. The Xbox numbers are Microsoft display-catalog data compiled by Timesaver. The Xbox package is 225,336,918,016 bytes; the store divides by a billion, the console by 1,073,741,824, so both consoles download about 210 GB.

Photos of the German PS5 box reportedly print "230 GB" minimum free space, which Rockstar has not explained. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Timesaver recommends clearing about 250 GB to cover the install, update 1.002 and later patches. What to delete, whether you need an expansion drive and how tight a Series S gets are in the [[guides/gta-6-storage-space-guide|storage space guide]].

## How to pre-load GTA 6 on PS5

1. Buy the game on PS Store, or redeem your boxed copy's code.
2. Open the game in your library and start the download if it hasn't begun.
3. Turn on auto-downloads: Settings, Saved Data and Game/App Settings, Automatic Updates.
4. Let Rest Mode stay online: Settings, System, Power Saving, Features Available in Rest Mode, Stay Connected to the Internet.
5. Leave the console on or in Rest Mode, not fully off.

PCWorld and Timesaver both say auto-downloads plus Rest Mode is what the PS5 needs. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## How to pre-load GTA 6 on Xbox Series X|S

1. Buy the game on the Microsoft Store, or redeem the boxed code.
2. Open My games and apps, then Manage queue, and check it is downloading.
3. Turn on automatic updates.
4. Leave the console on or in Sleep mode. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## Can physical copy buyers pre-load?

Yes. The [[release/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc|physical edition has no disc]]; it is a download code, and the boxes reached retail on Nov 12 so buyers could redeem and pre-load before launch. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> PlayStation codes are locked to your PSN account's region. Rockstar says Xbox codes are not region-locked.

Some buyers, including Best Buy and Rockstar Store customers, report codes that are already used or missing. Kotaku's editor-in-chief could not reproduce the problem, and Rockstar has announced no specific fix. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Keep the receipt and contact the retailer and Rockstar Support.

## Can Spectrum customers download GTA 6 faster for free?

Some existing US customers can. [Charter's November 4 announcement](https://corporate.charter.com/newsroom/spectrum-giving-free-gig-speed-lift-thanksgiving-grand-theft-auto) offers eligible residential customers a temporary **Gig Speed Lift through November 30, 2026**, activated in the My Spectrum App. It requires a qualifying Internet plan, compatible equipment and no outstanding obligation to Spectrum. Restrictions apply and the offer is subject to change. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

The lift raises eligible plans to 1 Gig; existing 1 Gig customers can move to 2 Gig in select areas. Charter says the bill does not change and the connection returns to its original speed tier when the promotion ends. Check the offer in your own app before opting in. This is not a reason to buy a new broadband plan or equipment just for GTA 6.

Treat the advertised "less than 30 minutes" download as a provider claim, not a measured result or guarantee. Your connection, console, home network and download servers affect the time. A faster download still cannot bypass the [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|game's unlock time]]. The promotion is for eligible Spectrum customers, not a global or India offer.

## GTA 6 pre-load not working?

These are general console steps, not Rockstar instructions.

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Download not showing | Check the purchase went through and you're signed in to the account that bought it |
| Stuck at 0% or queued | Open the download queue, resume it, restart the console if needed |
| Not enough space | Free the full ~212 GB plus working room for patches |
| Code rejected on PS5 | Redeem on the store for your PSN account's region |
| Finished but "unavailable" | Expected; it unlocks at midnight local store time on Nov 19 |

## Is there a patch on top of the pre-load?

Yes. Update 1.002.000 started rolling out on Nov 13: about 1.07 GB on PS5, staggered so half of users got it first and the rest about 11 hours later, and about 2.18 to 2.2 GB on Xbox. It is reported as mandatory, and Rockstar has published no patch notes. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> The 15 to 50 GB day-one patch guesses from before pre-load never materialized. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Details on the [[release/gta-6-day-one-patch|day-one patch page]].

A second update, **01.002.001**, began reaching pre-loads on November 16: about 602.5 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox, according to the reports collected on our [[release/gta-6-day-one-patch|patch tracker]]. The PS5 rollout was staged. These download sizes and rollout times are reported, not official patch notes. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

Auto-download settings alone do not prove the latest update installed. Open your console's download queue and check for updates before following the [[guides/gta-6-day-one-checklist|day-one checklist]].
