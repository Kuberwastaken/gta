**GTA 6 pre-load is live.** It opened on **Tuesday, November 10, 2026**: at midnight local time on PS5, and at one worldwide moment, 00:00 GMT, on Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar says you need **up to 212 GB of free space**. If your download finishes before Nov 19, the game will be ready the second it [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|unlocks in your region]].

Pre-loading does **not** let you play early. It only downloads the files.

## When did GTA 6 pre-load start?

Rockstar's Nov 3 Newswire post, "Grand Theft Auto VI Pre-Load Begins on November 10", set the dates. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

| Platform | Pre-load start | What that means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PS5 / PS5 Pro | Midnight local time, Tue Nov 10 | New Zealand first, at 11:00 UTC on Mon Nov 9; then region by region |
| Xbox Series X\|S | 12:00 AM GMT, Tue Nov 10 (one global moment) | 7 PM ET / 4 PM PT on Mon Nov 9; 5:30 AM IST Nov 10 |

The PS5 pre-load followed the same store-region logic as the launch itself, so US players likely got it at midnight Eastern. Xbox did the opposite and opened everywhere at once.

### Nov 10 or Nov 12? The date that moved

You will still find articles saying pre-load starts **Nov 12**. That was Rockstar's original date, given at the June pre-order launch. On Nov 3 Rockstar brought it forward two days to **Nov 10**. The release date did not move. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Stale sources that still say Nov 12 include Consequence's June pre-order guide, VICE's Oct 15 launch-trailer article and a Tech Times piece from Nov 9. If you read Nov 12 anywhere, it is out of date.

Rockstar marked the moment with a 30-second "Pre-Load Now" video on Nov 10, captured on PS5 with PlayStation branding. GameRant calls it "Trailer 4". More on that in [[media/gta-6-launch-trailer|the launch trailer]] coverage.

### Why does Xbox say 225 GB?

This is the most common confusion this week, and it has a simple answer: **it is the same size, measured in two different units.**

- The Xbox package is exactly **225,336,918,016 bytes**.
- Divide by 1,000,000,000 (decimal gigabytes, what the Microsoft Store uses) and you get **225.34 GB**.
- Divide by 1,073,741,824 (binary gigabytes, what the console's storage screen uses) and you get **about 209.86 GB**.

So the Xbox version is not 13 GB bigger than PS5. Both are about 210 GB in the units consoles use. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## How to pre-load GTA 6 on PS5

1. Buy the game digitally on PS Store, or redeem your boxed copy's code.
2. Open the game's page in your library and start the download if it has not begun.
3. Turn on auto-downloads (Settings, Saved Data and Game/App Settings, Automatic Updates) and let the console stay online in Rest Mode (Settings, System, Power Saving, Features Available in Rest Mode, Stay Connected to the Internet).
4. Leave the console on or in **Rest Mode**, not fully off.

PCWorld and Timesaver both say auto-downloads plus Rest Mode is what the PS5 needs. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## How to pre-load GTA 6 on Xbox Series X|S

1. Buy the game on the Microsoft Store, or redeem the boxed code.
2. Go to My games and apps, then Manage queue, to make sure it is downloading.
3. Turn on automatic updates.
4. Leave the console on or in **Sleep** mode.

## Can physical copy buyers pre-load?

Yes. The [[release/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc|physical edition has no disc]]. It is a download code in a box, and those boxes reached retail on **Nov 12** specifically so buyers could redeem and pre-load. Once you redeem the code, you pre-load exactly like a digital buyer. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>

Remember that PlayStation codes are region-locked to your PSN account's region, while Rockstar says Xbox codes are not region-locked.

Some buyers have reported codes that show as already used or are missing from the box. The scope is unclear and Kotaku could not reproduce it; contact the retailer and Rockstar Support if it happens to you. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## GTA 6 pre-load not working? Quick fixes

These are general console steps, not Rockstar instructions:

- **Download not showing:** check the purchase went through and that you are signed in to the account that bought it.
- **Stuck at 0% or queued:** open the download queue manually and resume it; restart the console if needed.
- **Not enough space:** the console needs room for the full ~212 GB plus a little working space. Delete or move games until you clear it.
- **Code-in-box problems:** redeem on the right region's store for PS5. If the code is invalid, keep your receipt and contact the retailer.
- **Downloaded but "unavailable":** that is expected. The game stays locked until midnight local store time on Nov 19.

## Is there a day-one patch on top of the pre-load?

There is already an update. **Version 1.002.000** started rolling out on **Nov 13**: about **1.07 GB on PS5** (staggered, half of users first and the rest about 11 hours later) and about **2.18–2.2 GB on Xbox**. It is reported as mandatory. Rockstar has not published patch notes or called it "the day-one patch". <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Earlier guesses of a 15–50 GB day-one patch were never confirmed. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>
