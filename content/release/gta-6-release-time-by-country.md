GTA 6 unlocks at **midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, 2026**, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar's support page says the game "will release midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions". The catch is that "local" means **your store region's midnight**, not your own clock, and that matters a lot if you live in the western half of the US.

The short version:

- **New Zealand is first**, at 11:00 UTC on Wednesday, Nov 18.
- **The UK** unlocks at midnight GMT, the start of Nov 19.
- **The entire US store** unlocks at midnight Eastern. Pacific players start at **9 PM on Wednesday, Nov 18**.
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming** starts at **1 AM ET**, an hour after native US consoles.

If you have not downloaded yet, [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|pre-load has been live since Nov 10]], and the game needs up to 212 GB. Our [[guides/gta-6-day-one-checklist|day-one checklist]] covers the rest.

## What time does GTA 6 unlock in each US time zone?

The US PlayStation Store uses a single timestamp: **05:00 UTC on Nov 19, which is midnight Eastern**. Everyone in the country gets the game at the same instant, so the further west you are, the earlier on Wednesday evening you start. Forbes and Tech Times both reported this, and KeenGamer's table agrees. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

| US time zone | Unlock time | Day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eastern (New York, Miami, Atlanta) | 12:00 AM | Thu Nov 19 |
| Central (Chicago, Dallas, Houston) | 11:00 PM | Wed Nov 18 |
| Mountain (Denver, Salt Lake City) | 10:00 PM | Wed Nov 18 |
| Arizona (no daylight saving; on MST all year) | 10:00 PM | Wed Nov 18 |
| Pacific (Los Angeles, Seattle, Las Vegas) | 9:00 PM | Wed Nov 18 |
| Alaska | 8:00 PM | Wed Nov 18 |
| Hawaii | 7:00 PM (disputed, see below) | Wed Nov 18 |

So yes, **you can play GTA 6 the night before** if you live west of the Eastern time zone. A Los Angeles player gets three hours in before the calendar even reaches Nov 19.

### Hawaii: 7 PM Wednesday or midnight Thursday?

This is one of the genuine open questions. If Hawaii follows the single US store timestamp, it unlocks at **7 PM HST on Wednesday, Nov 18**. Some countdown sites instead give **10:00 UTC on Nov 19**, which is true local midnight in Hawaii. We think the 7 PM reading is more consistent with how the US store works, but that is our inference. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> Check the countdown on your own console's store page, which will be correct for your account.

### Alaska

Rockstar and the outlets above do not mention Alaska. If it follows the same US store timestamp, like the rest of the country, the unlock is 8 PM AKST on Wednesday. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

## Why does "midnight local" mean 9 PM in California?

Rockstar's wording and the US West Coast 9 PM start are both true. They only sound contradictory.

- The **store region** decides the timestamp. Sony and Microsoft schedule one unlock per store, and the US store's single timestamp is midnight Eastern.
- Your console's clock and time-zone setting do not change it. Neither does where you physically are.
- Countries with one time zone (UK, India, Japan) see no difference. Countries spanning several time zones (US, Canada, Australia, Brazil) get one unlock moment across all of them.

This is how big PlayStation and Xbox launches usually work, and it is why US Pacific players are often the night-before winners.

## When does GTA 6 unlock on Xbox and Xbox Cloud Gaming?

**Xbox Series X|S consoles:** Rockstar uses the same "midnight local" wording for Xbox, and Microsoft Store data lists per-market timestamps that match the PS5 windows above. That rests on store-page data, not an explicit Microsoft statement. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

**Xbox Cloud Gaming:** Xbox Wire says GTA VI arrives on cloud on **Nov 19 from 1 AM ET**, on Xbox One and Series X|S. That is one hour **after** the native US unlock, so a Pacific player streaming from the cloud starts at 10 PM PT on Nov 18 rather than 9 PM. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> Microsoft gave only the Eastern time; we do not know whether other countries get cloud access at their own local times or at the same 06:00 UTC moment. You still have to buy the game, since it is not in Game Pass. Details are on our [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|Series S and cloud page]].

## Can you play GTA 6 early with a New Zealand account or a VPN?

New Zealand gets the game 18 hours before the US store. That makes the "NZ trick" the most-asked question this week. What is reported:

- On PS5, early play reportedly needs a **New Zealand PSN account and a New Zealand payment method**. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>
- **A VPN alone does not work**, and neither does changing the console clock. The store region of the account that owns the game decides the unlock. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>
- Sources disagree about Xbox region switching. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>
- Buying through another region's store may breach store terms. You would also be buying a second copy, since a US-store copy will not unlock on NZ time.

Our advice: it is not worth it for most people. If you just want to dodge spoilers, see below.
