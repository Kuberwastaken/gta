GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar's support article says it "will release midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions". "Local" means the midnight of your console store's region, not your own clock. The US PS5 listing has one timestamp, midnight Eastern, so **US Pacific PS5 players start at 9 PM on Wednesday, Nov 18**. Native Xbox storefront hours have not been independently confirmed here.

New Zealand goes first, at 11:00 UTC on Nov 18, 18 hours before the US store. Xbox Cloud Gaming starts last in the US, at 1 AM ET. None of it matters if the download isn't finished: [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|pre-load]] has been open since Nov 10 and the final space required can differ from earlier preload reports. Check the [[guides/gta-6-storage-space-guide|storage guide]] and your console.

Berlin and Paris get the game an hour before London. India runs on one time zone, so every Indian player starts at the same midnight. Brazil dropped daylight saving in 2019, so 03:00 UTC holds for São Paulo and Rio.

## What did we verify directly before launch?

The [US PlayStation Store listing](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/EP1004-PPSA01547_00-GTAVISTANDARD001) was checked directly on **November 17** and shows **November 19, 2026, 05:00 AM UTC**. That converts to midnight EST and **9 PM PST on November 18**. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> The arithmetic uses November's standard-time offsets, not summer EDT/PDT.

Some recent press guides paraphrase Rockstar's "midnight local time" sentence as a separate midnight in every US time zone. That is not what the current US PS5 listing's single timestamp shows. The general Rockstar sentence does not spell out that distinction. For an **Xbox native purchase**, confirm the countdown on your own console: we have not recovered an equally explicit current public Xbox storefront hour, so do not treat a PS5 timestamp as an independent Xbox confirmation. Xbox Wire's **1 AM ET cloud start** is a separate service, not the native unlock.

The regional table is still a reported compilation outside that directly checked US PS5 entry. If your account's countdown differs, follow it and check the store region before arranging your night around a press table.

## Canada and Australia: one store, several time zones

PS Store data groups Canada with the US at 05:00 UTC. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Applied coast to coast, that puts Vancouver at 9 PM, Calgary and Edmonton at 10 PM and Winnipeg at 11 PM on Nov 18, with Toronto and Montreal at midnight. Halifax would wait until 1:00 AM and St. John's until 1:30 AM on Nov 19, past their own midnight. No outlet has confirmed the Atlantic times. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

Australia works the same way off Sydney. KeenGamer lists Perth at 9 PM on Wednesday, Nov 18, which only fits if the whole store uses the 13:00 UTC timestamp. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> On that basis Adelaide unlocks at 11:30 PM, Brisbane (no daylight saving) at 11:00 PM and Darwin at 10:30 PM on Nov 18. Those three are our conversions. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

## When does GTA 6 unlock on Xbox and Xbox Cloud Gaming?

Rockstar uses the same "midnight local" wording for Xbox Series X|S. We have not independently confirmed the native Xbox storefront hours here, so check your own console countdown rather than treating the US PS5 listing as Xbox evidence.

Xbox Wire's Nov 10 post puts cloud play on Xbox One and Series X|S at 1 AM ET on Nov 19, an hour after the US PS5 listing, so a Pacific player on cloud starts at 10 PM PT. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> Microsoft gave only the Eastern time; whether other countries get cloud access at their own midnight or at one 06:00 UTC moment is unknown. You still have to buy the game, since it is not on Game Pass. Details on the [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|Series S and cloud page]].

## Can you play GTA 6 early with a New Zealand account?

On PS5, GTA BOOM reports that early play needs a New Zealand PSN account and a New Zealand payment method. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> A VPN or a changed console clock does nothing, because the store region of the account that owns the game sets the unlock. Sources disagree about Xbox region switching. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Do not treat a second purchase or a false-region account as a safe launch-night shortcut.

[Sony's actual account terms](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/legal/psn-terms-of-service/) require accurate account information and selection of your country or region of residence. Sections 3.1 and 3.2 allow account restrictions for false information and say the account's country cannot be changed after creation. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> That is a concrete account risk, not a guaranteed early-unlock method.

Reports of suspended New Zealand accounts are circulating, but a suspension screenshot does not prove that GTA 6 or the account region caused it. We have not independently verified a GTA-specific ban wave. Wait for your own store countdown rather than buying another copy or using an account supplied by an unknown seller.
