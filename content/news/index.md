GTA 6 is four days from launch. It unlocks at **midnight local store time on Thursday, November 19, 2026**, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Take-Two said on Nov 9 that Rockstar has finished development. This is where we file dated news: what happened, why it matters and what comes next, each claim graded like the rest of the site.

## What's the biggest GTA 6 news this week?

Three things. Pre-load opened on Nov 10, two days earlier than first planned, and needs up to 212 GB. Update 1.002 started downloading on Nov 13 with no patch notes. And photos of the official Collector's Box map poster leaked between Nov 12 and 14, adding new Leonida place names <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## Is GTA 6 still coming out on November 19?

Yes. Take-Two reaffirmed the date in its Nov 9 results and said there is no further delay. Rumours of a slip to 2027 are graded debunked in our [[leaks/gta-6-another-delay-rumour|delay rumour entry]]. The whole history is on [[timeline/gta-6-delays|GTA 6 delays]].

## When do reviews come out?

Reportedly on Wednesday, Nov 18, the day before launch <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it. We track it on [[release/gta-6-reviews-and-review-embargo|reviews and embargo]].
