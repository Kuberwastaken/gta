GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local store time on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Take-Two said on November 9 that Rockstar has finished development, so the date holds. This section files dated stories; each page carries the event date as its published date and grades every claim.

## The last two weeks in one paragraph

Rockstar posted 39 Launch Gallery screenshots on November 5. Take-Two reported on November 9: bookings down 18% to $1.60B for the quarter, $3.35-3.5B guided for the launch quarter, and no pre-order count. Pre-load opened November 10, two days earlier than first announced, needing up to 212 GB, and Rockstar confirmed a 30fps target on every console. Update 1.002 began downloading on November 13 at about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.2 GB on Xbox, with no patch notes. Between November 12 and 14, photos of the Collector's Box map poster leaked from Selfridges Manchester, adding Goose Key and Pelican Valley <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.
