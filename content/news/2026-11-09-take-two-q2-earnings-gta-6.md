Take-Two Interactive reported its fiscal second-quarter results on **Monday, November 9, 2026**, ten days before GTA 6 launches. The headline line from CEO Strauss Zelnick: "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away and, with pre-orders continuing to reach unprecedented levels, we expect to deliver an historic year for our company." Take-Two did not disclose a pre-order count and did not raise its guidance.

## What did Take-Two report?

The quarter ended Sep 30, before GTA 6 earned anything, so the numbers are soft. From the SEC filing:

| Metric | Q2 FY27 |
| --- | --- |
| Net bookings | $1.60B, down 18% year on year |
| Own guidance for the quarter | $1.62-1.67B (missed) |
| GAAP revenue | $1.50B |
| Net loss | $54.0M ($0.29 per share) |
| Recurrent consumer spending | Down 9%, 81% of bookings |

The forward numbers are where GTA 6 shows up:

| Guidance | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Q3 FY27 (Oct-Dec 2026) net bookings | **$3.35-3.5B**, versus about $1.9B a year earlier |
| Q3 FY27 GAAP net income | $250-276M |
| FY27 net bookings | **$8.0-8.2B**, reiterated |
| FY27 operating cash flow | Over $1B |

## What did executives say about GTA 6?

According to GamesBeat's write-up of the call:

- Rockstar **has finished development** and the marketing campaign is fully set. There is no third delay. That closes out the [[leaks/gta-6-another-delay-rumour|2027 delay rumour]].
- Pre-orders are tracking "far in excess" of initial expectations.
- CFO Lainie Goldstein: "the level of pre-orders generated is extraordinary".
- Marketing spend is up 21% year on year, mostly in the December quarter.
- Zelnick on not raising guidance: "we still aren't ready to declare victory."

Take-Two declined to give FY28 guidance before launch. On new platforms, Zelnick said "information always comes from our labels" <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (Variety), which means any [[release/gta-6-pc-release-date|PC]] or [[release/gta-6-switch-2|Switch 2]] news will come from Rockstar.

GamesBeat also reports GTA V passed 235 million units as of Sep 30 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. That figure is not in the 8-K text we read; Take-Two's August results said "over 230M".

## How many pre-orders are there really?

Take-Two won't say, so the numbers in circulation are third-party estimates <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

- Sensor Tower, around Nov 3: about $750M.
- Sensor Tower, as of Nov 11: about **$1.0B and 10.31M units**, net of returns, about 75% on PlayStation.
- CNBC, Nov 14: Sensor Tower's estimate passed **12M** through Nov 13.

These don't conflict; they are the same rolling estimate at different dates. A separate snippet claiming "$1.18B from 12M units" could not be verified, so we don't use it. Sony and Microsoft have both confirmed it is the most pre-ordered game ever on their stores, which is first-party but gives no number. More on [[release/gta-6-sales-and-pre-orders|sales and pre-orders]].

## How did the market react?

Shares rose about 8% after the report, on a down day for the market <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Analyst forecasts for the first year range widely: Wolfe Research says 31-35M units, Morgan Stanley about 40M in FY27, and Bank of America 45M in the first year <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. These are forecasts, not company figures.

## Why it matters

This was Take-Two's last scheduled chance to move the date, and it didn't. The same filing notes GTA Online on GTA V got its Kortz Center Heist update on July 14, and Take-Two says it will keep supporting GTA Online after GTA 6 launches; see [[release/gta-6-gta-online-status|GTA Online status]].
