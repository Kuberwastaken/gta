Take-Two reported fiscal second-quarter results on Monday, November 9, 2026, and told investors GTA 6 is "ten days away" with pre-orders at "unprecedented levels". It gave no pre-order count, kept full-year guidance at $8.0-8.2B, and confirmed there is no further delay.

The quote from CEO Strauss Zelnick in the release: "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away and, with pre-orders continuing to reach unprecedented levels, we expect to deliver an historic year for our company."

## The quarter: a miss nobody cared about

The quarter ended September 30, before GTA 6 earned a cent. From the SEC filing:

| Metric | Q2 FY27 |
| --- | --- |
| Net bookings | $1.60B, down 18% year on year |
| Own guidance for the quarter | $1.62-1.67B (missed) |
| GAAP revenue | $1.50B |
| Net loss | $54.0M ($0.29 per share) |
| Recurrent consumer spending | Down 9%, 81% of bookings |

The guidance is where GTA 6 shows up:

| Guidance | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Q3 FY27 (Oct-Dec 2026) net bookings | $3.35-3.5B, versus about $1.9B a year earlier |
| Q3 FY27 revenue | $3.27-3.42B |
| Q3 FY27 GAAP net income | $250-276M |
| FY27 net bookings | $8.0-8.2B, reiterated |
| FY27 operating cash flow | Over $1B |

Shares rose about 8% after the report on a down day for the market <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## What executives said on the call

GamesBeat's write-up has Rockstar finished with development and the marketing campaign fully set, which closes the [[leaks/gta-6-another-delay-rumour|2027 delay rumour]]. Zelnick said pre-orders are tracking "far in excess" of initial expectations. CFO Lainie Goldstein called the level "extraordinary". Marketing spend is up 21% year on year, mostly landing in the December quarter.

Take-Two still declined to raise guidance or give FY28 numbers before launch. Zelnick's line: "we still aren't ready to declare victory." On new platforms he said "information always comes from our labels" <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (Variety), so any [[release/gta-6-pc-release-date|PC]] or [[release/gta-6-switch-2|Switch 2]] announcement will come from Rockstar.

GamesBeat also reports GTA V passed 235 million units as of September 30, about 5M of them in the quarter <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. That figure is not in the 8-K text we read; Take-Two's August results said "over 230M".

## How many pre-orders, then?

Only third-party estimates exist <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Sensor Tower put pre-orders at about $750M around November 3, then about $1.0B and 10.31M units net of returns as of November 11, with PlayStation at about 75% (7.73M units). CNBC reported on November 14 that the estimate passed 12M through November 13. Those are one rolling estimate at three dates, not conflicting figures. A snippet claiming "$1.18B from 12M units" could not be verified and we don't use it.

The only first-party numbers are store rankings: Sony and Microsoft both say GTA 6 is the most pre-ordered game ever on their stores. Running totals are on [[release/gta-6-sales-and-pre-orders|sales and pre-orders]].

First-year analyst forecasts span 31-35M units (Wolfe Research) to about 40M in FY27 (Morgan Stanley) and 45M (Bank of America) <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. For scale, GTA V sold 11.21M copies in its first 24 hours in 2013.

## What it means for players

The date is locked: this was Take-Two's last scheduled update before November 19, and the full delay record ends at two ([[timeline/gta-6-delays|GTA 6 delays]]). The filing also notes GTA Online on GTA V got the Kortz Center Heist update on July 14, and Take-Two says it will keep supporting it after launch, which matters because GTA 6 ships single-player only ([[release/gta-6-gta-online-status|GTA Online status]]).

Take-Two's next results, covering the October-December launch quarter, are the first place official sales numbers are likely to appear. The [[news/2026-11-15-gta-6-launch-week-briefing|launch week briefing]] and the [[news/index|news hub]] carry anything sooner.
