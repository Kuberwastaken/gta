Rockstar published the **Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Gallery** on **Thursday, November 5, 2026**: **39 new screenshots** on rockstargames.com/VI, announced in a Newswire post titled "Explore the Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Gallery". It landed exactly two weeks before the Nov 19 release, Gamereactor reported.

## What's in the Launch Gallery?

Gamereactor counts 39 new images, and about 12 other screenshots had come out roughly three weeks earlier. Rockstar's Newswire page rendered empty when our research team fetched it, so the shot-by-shot detail comes from Gamereactor and other outlets rather than Rockstar's own captions.

November's screenshots also showcased six named cars <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

- Grotti Cheetah
- Albany Buccaneer
- Progen Emerus
- Invetero Coquette
- Albany Manana
- Pegassi Tempesta

Those come from fan databases via search results, not from Rockstar's captions, so treat the model list as provisional until launch. Rockstar has given no official vehicle count; fan databases disagree. See [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|GTA 6 vehicles]].

## What else did Rockstar show in early November?

Around the same time Rockstar gave a first look at *Macca & Friends*, an animated in-game TV show starring [[characters/macca-the-gator|Macca the Gator]] <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Insider Gaming says it premieres Nov 19 "exclusively on WEAZEL", and its teaser features a talking Sun in sunglasses and a crab landlord. Outlets disagree on the crab's name: Insider Gaming says "Pinchy", Rockstar Report "Pinche".

## Why it matters

The gallery is the last big official image drop before launch, and it came after the August and October CyberLeek leaks had put unofficial footage in circulation. Rockstar's own marketing has stuck to footage captured on a base PS5, so these images are the best guide to what most players will see. Performance details are on [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|PS5 Pro performance]].

Rockstar followed the gallery with pre-load on Nov 10 ([[news/2026-11-10-gta-6-preload-live|pre-load story]]) and a 30-second "Pre-Load Now" video. A printable Leonida map is reported for launch day. All launch-month stories are in the [[news/index|news hub]].
