Rockstar posted 39 new GTA 6 screenshots to rockstargames.com/VI on Thursday, November 5, 2026, under a Newswire post titled "Explore the Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Gallery". It landed exactly two weeks before the November 19 release, Gamereactor reported, and about three weeks after a smaller set of roughly 12 images that came with gameplay details.

Rockstar's Newswire page rendered empty when we fetched it, so there are no official captions to quote. The count and descriptions come from Gamereactor and GameSpot.

The countable sets add up to about 121 images. One aggregator puts the full official pack at 138 <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>; nobody has shown its working. The per-set breakdown is on [[media/gta-6-screenshots-and-galleries|screenshots and galleries]].

## Which cars are in the screenshots?

Fan databases picked out six models in November's images <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>: the Grotti Cheetah, Albany Buccaneer, Progen Emerus, Invetero Coquette, Albany Manana and Pegassi Tempesta. These are fan identifications seen through search results, not Rockstar captions. The Cheetah matters to buyers because a '95 Grotti Cheetah is one of the 16 Ultimate Edition items ([[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|editions compared]]). Rockstar has given no total vehicle count; see [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|GTA 6 vehicles]].

## What else did Rockstar show in early November?

A first look at *Macca & Friends*, an animated in-game TV show starring [[characters/macca-the-gator|Macca the Gator]] <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Insider Gaming says the 30-second teaser introduces a talking Sun in sunglasses and a crab landlord, and that the show premieres November 19 "exclusively on WEAZEL". The crab is "Pinchy" in Insider Gaming and "Pinche" in Rockstar Report.

## What it tells players

These are the last official images before launch, and they followed the August and October CyberLeek dumps ([[leaks/gta-6-cyberleek-2026-leaks|CyberLeek leaks]]). Rockstar's marketing has stayed on base-PS5 capture, so the gallery is a fair guide to what a standard console shows at the confirmed 30fps target. The Pro differences (PSSR, ray-traced shadows) are on [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|PS5 Pro performance]].

We don't reproduce Rockstar's screenshots; the Newswire link in the sources has them. The trailers are covered from the [[media/index|media hub]], starting with the [[media/gta-6-launch-trailer|Launch Trailer]].
