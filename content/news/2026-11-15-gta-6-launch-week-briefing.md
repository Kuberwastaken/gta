Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks on **Thursday, November 19, 2026, at midnight local store time** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Four days out, the game is fully pre-loadable, its first update is already on consoles, and Take-Two says development is finished. This briefing collects everything that matters this week, graded, with links to our deeper pages.

## When exactly does GTA 6 unlock?

Rockstar's rule is that the game "will release midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions". "Local" means your **store region's** midnight, not your own clock. That is why the US West Coast gets in on Wednesday night. Store data compiled by fan sites gives these windows <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

| Store region | Unlock (UTC) |
| --- | --- |
| New Zealand | 11:00, Nov 18 (first in the world) |
| Australia (Sydney) | 13:00, Nov 18 |
| Japan, Korea | 15:00, Nov 18 |
| India | 18:30, Nov 18 |
| Germany, France | 23:00, Nov 18 |
| UK, Ireland | 00:00, Nov 19 |
| Brazil, Argentina | 03:00, Nov 19 |
| US, Canada | 05:00, Nov 19 (midnight ET) |

The whole US store follows Eastern time: 11 PM Central, 10 PM Mountain and **9 PM Pacific on Nov 18** <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Hawaii is unclear: some countdowns say 7 PM Nov 18, others local midnight. Check the countdown on your own console. Xbox Cloud Gaming starts at **1 AM ET**, an hour after the native US unlock, per Xbox Wire. The full table for 20+ regions is on our [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|release time by country]] page.

Reports that a New Zealand account gets PS5 players in early come with caveats: it reportedly needs a New Zealand payment method, and it may breach store terms <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. We don't recommend it.

## Is pre-load open, and how big is the download?

Yes. Rockstar moved pre-load forward from Nov 12 to **Nov 10**, announced on Nov 3. PS5 opened at local midnight (New Zealand first), Xbox at a single worldwide moment, 00:00 GMT on Nov 10. Some older articles still say Nov 12; they are out of date. Our [[news/2026-11-10-gta-6-preload-live|pre-load news story]] has the details.

Rockstar asks for **up to 212 GB** of free space. The PS5 download is about 209.8 GB <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The Xbox store shows 225.34 GB, but that is the same package counted in decimal gigabytes; the console shows about 209.86 GB <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Clear more than you think you need. See [[release/gta-6-preload-and-file-size|pre-load and file size]] and the [[guides/gta-6-storage-space-guide|storage space guide]].

## What is update 1.002?

Update 1.002.000 started rolling out on **Nov 13**: about 1.07 GB on PS5 and about 2.2 GB on Xbox <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. PS5 rolled it out in two waves about 11 hours apart. Rockstar has published no patch notes and has not called it a day-one patch. Read [[news/2026-11-13-gta-6-update-1-002|our update 1.002 report]] and the [[release/gta-6-day-one-patch|day-one patch page]].

## What do the editions cost, and is there a disc?

US prices are **$79.99 Standard and $99.99 Ultimate**. UK is £69.99 / £89.99 and the eurozone €79.99 / €99.99 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (store listings). Buy before Nov 20 and you get the Vintage Vice City Pack; Xbox Wire puts the cutoff at 23:59:59 on Nov 19. Digital pre-orders also come with a free month of GTA+, which reportedly auto-renews <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

The boxed Standard edition is **a download code, not a disc**. Boxes reached shops on Nov 12 so buyers could pre-load. There is no physical Ultimate Edition. Some buyers report codes that were already used or missing; Kotaku's editor-in-chief could not reproduce it, and the scope is unknown <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Full breakdowns: [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]], [[release/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc|no disc]], and [[guides/gta-6-which-edition-should-you-buy|which edition to buy]].

## How will it run on my console?

Rockstar targets **30 fps on every console**, PS5 Pro included. There is no 60 fps or performance mode at launch. PS5 Pro adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows. Xbox Series S runs it natively, and Xbox One owners can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming at Series S settings, but only if they buy the game; it is not on Game Pass. There is no PC or browser cloud streaming at launch. More on the [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|PS5 Pro page]] and the [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|Series S and cloud page]].

## When do the reviews land?

NateTheHate said on Oct 26 that review codes were out with an embargo on **Wednesday, Nov 18**, and Insider Gaming says it verified the date <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. A relayed claim puts the lift at 6-7 AM EST <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Rockstar has confirmed neither. As of today there are no reviews and no Metacritic score. Track it on [[release/gta-6-reviews-and-review-embargo|reviews and embargo]].

## What is not in the game at launch?

- **No online mode.** Rockstar calls GTA 6 "a single player experience". Talk of an online mode in 2027 is unconfirmed <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. See [[leaks/gta-6-online-at-launch-rumour|the online-at-launch rumour]] and [[release/gta-6-gta-online-status|GTA Online status]].
- **No PC version.** GamesBeat's four-source report of a Q1 2027 PC release is unconfirmed <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[leaks/gta-6-pc-at-launch-rumour|PC at launch]]. Any "GTA 6 PC download" is fake or malware.
- **No single-player microtransactions**, per Rockstar North's Rob Nelson <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.
- **No third delay.** On Nov 9 Take-Two said Rockstar has finished development. The 2027 delay claims are dead: [[leaks/gta-6-another-delay-rumour|another delay rumour]].

## How big are pre-orders?

Sony calls it the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history, and Microsoft says the same for its store. Take-Two has given no unit count. Sensor Tower estimated about **$1.0B and 10.31M units** as of Nov 11, and CNBC reports its estimate passed **12M** through Nov 13 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. These are rolling third-party estimates, not company figures. Strauss Zelnick told CNBC that "our expectations were high and they are being vastly exceeded so far." Our [[news/2026-11-09-take-two-q2-earnings-gta-6|earnings story]] and [[release/gta-6-sales-and-pre-orders|sales page]] break it down.
