Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks on Thursday, November 19, 2026, at midnight local store time on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. At this briefing's original Nov 15 publication, pre-load has been open for five days, update 1.002 is already on consoles, and Take-Two says Rockstar has finished development.

## When does GTA 6 unlock?

Rockstar says the game "will release midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions". Local means the store region's midnight, not your own clock. Store data compiled by fan sites gives these windows <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

| Store region | Unlock (UTC) |
| --- | --- |
| New Zealand | 11:00, Nov 18 (first in the world) |
| Australia (Sydney) | 13:00, Nov 18 |
| Japan, Korea | 15:00, Nov 18 |
| India | 18:30, Nov 18 |
| Germany, France | 23:00, Nov 18 |
| UK, Ireland | 00:00, Nov 19 |
| Brazil, Argentina | 03:00, Nov 19 |
| US, Canada | 05:00, Nov 19 (midnight ET) |

The whole US store runs on Eastern time, so Central gets in at 11 PM, Mountain at 10 PM and Pacific at 9 PM on Wednesday, November 18 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Hawaii is unresolved: some countdowns say 7 PM on the 18th, others local midnight, so check your console. Xbox Cloud Gaming starts at 1 AM ET, an hour after the native US unlock, per Xbox Wire. The 20-plus-region table is on [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|release time by country]].

The New Zealand account trick for PS5 reportedly needs a New Zealand payment method as well, and may breach store terms <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Changing the console clock or using a VPN alone does not work.

## Pre-load, download size and update 1.002

Rockstar moved pre-load from November 12 to November 10 in a November 3 post. PS5 opened at local midnight, New Zealand first; Xbox opened worldwide at 00:00 GMT ([[news/2026-11-10-gta-6-preload-live|pre-load story]]). Rockstar asks for up to 212 GB free. The PS5 download is about 209.8 GB; the Xbox store's 225.34 GB is the same package counted in decimal units, shown as about 209.86 GB on the console <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

Update 1.002.000 began rolling out on November 13: about 1.07 GB on PS5, in two waves about 11 hours apart, and about 2.2 GB on Xbox <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. No patch notes, and Rockstar has not called it a day-one patch ([[news/2026-11-13-gta-6-update-1-002|update 1.002]]). Storage planning is in the [[guides/gta-6-storage-space-guide|storage space guide]].

## Prices, bonuses and the missing disc

US prices are $79.99 Standard and $99.99 Ultimate; UK £69.99 / £89.99; eurozone €79.99 / €99.99 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (store listings). The Vintage Vice City Pack goes to every purchase before November 20; Xbox Wire puts the cutoff at 23:59:59 on November 19. Digital pre-orders add a free month of GTA+, which reportedly auto-renews at about $8 a month <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

The boxed Standard edition is a download code, not a disc. Boxes reached shops on November 12 so buyers could pre-load, and there is no physical Ultimate Edition. Some buyers report codes already used or missing, including Best Buy and Rockstar Store orders; Kotaku's editor-in-chief could not reproduce it and the scope is unknown <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[release/gta-6-price-and-editions|price and editions]], [[release/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc|no disc]] and [[guides/gta-6-which-edition-should-you-buy|which edition to buy]].

## How it runs

Rockstar targets 30fps on every console, PS5 Pro included, with no 60fps or performance mode at launch. Pro adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows. Series S runs the game natively. Xbox One owners can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming at Series S settings if they buy the game; it is not on Game Pass, and PC or browser streaming is not offered. Details: [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|PS5 Pro]] and [[release/gta-6-xbox-series-s-and-cloud-gaming|Series S and cloud]].

## What is not in the game at launch

There is no online mode. Rockstar calls GTA 6 "a single player experience", and talk of online in 2027 is unconfirmed <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> ([[leaks/gta-6-online-at-launch-rumour|online-at-launch rumour]], [[release/gta-6-gta-online-status|GTA Online status]]).

There is no PC version. GamesBeat's four-source Q1 2027 report is unconfirmed <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> ([[leaks/gta-6-pc-at-launch-rumour|PC at launch]]), and any "GTA 6 PC download" is malware.

Rockstar North's Rob Nelson says single-player has no real-money purchases <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. And there is no third delay: Take-Two said on November 9 that development is finished ([[leaks/gta-6-another-delay-rumour|delay rumour]]).
