GTA 6's first update, version 1.002.000, began rolling out to pre-loaded copies on Friday, November 13, 2026, six days before launch. Console download screens put it at about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.18 to 2.2 GB on Xbox Series X|S, per VICE, KeenGamer and Timesaver. Rockstar has posted no patch notes and no statement.

## Update 1.002 by platform

All of this comes from what players see on their consoles <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

| | PS5 | Xbox Series X\|S |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Size | About 1.07 GB | About 2.18-2.2 GB |
| Rollout | About 50% of users first, the rest about 11 hours later | Not reported as staggered |
| Mandatory | Reported as mandatory | Not stated |
| Patch notes | None | None |

Nobody has explained why the Xbox file is twice the size. Do not confuse it with the store-unit quirk on the base game, where Xbox counts decimal gigabytes: the base package is 225,336,918,016 bytes, shown as 225.34 GB in the store and about 209.86 GB on the console ([[news/2026-11-10-gta-6-preload-live|pre-load story]]).

## Is it the day-one patch?

VICE's headline says so. Rockstar has not used the label or said whether more updates come before November 19, so we treat 1.002 as the first pre-launch update and keep the [[release/gta-6-day-one-patch|day-one patch page]] open.

An insider account, "Text Funds 2", claims the update touches story data, executables, levels, shaders and scripts <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Nobody has shown evidence, and Rockstar has not commented.

The 15-50 GB day-one patch guesses that circulated before November <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> now look wrong by an order of magnitude. A larger patch later is still possible; the claim stays in the [[leaks/index|leaks ledger]].

## What to do before launch night

If you pre-loaded and then turned off auto-downloads, you will hit this download at midnight. On PS5, enable auto-downloads and leave the console on or in Rest Mode. On Xbox, enable automatic updates and use Sleep mode.

Storage is the other constraint. Rockstar asks for up to 212 GB free before patches, and photos of the German PS5 box reportedly print 230 GB. The [[guides/gta-6-storage-space-guide|storage guide]] covers how much headroom to keep. Boxed copies contain a download code, not a disc, so redeem the code first or the patch can't download ([[release/gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc|no disc explained]]).
