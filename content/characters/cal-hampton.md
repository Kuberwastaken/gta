Cal Hampton is [[characters/jason-duval|Jason Duval]]'s friend and, like Jason, one of the people who work for Keys smuggler [[characters/brian-heder|Brian Heder]]. He is one of the eight bio entries Rockstar posted on its Only in Leonida page alongside [[media/gta-6-trailer-2|Trailer 2]] on May 6, 2025. That page has not gained a single new profile since.

## What Rockstar says about Cal

The bio is two sentences. Cal "feels safest hanging at home, snooping on Coast Guard comms with a few beers and some private browser tabs open." He "is at the low tide of America and happy there," and the bio sets him against Jason, who "has bigger plans."

All three of his pull quotes are paranoia:

- "What if everything on the internet was true?"
- "There are way too many birds flying around in perfect formation."
- "The psychopaths are in charge. Get used to it."

That puts Cal in the same satirical lane as the [[characters/real-dimez|Real Dimez]], whose bio is built on "a relentless social media presence." Online culture is a recurring Rockstar target in these bios, and the in-game [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social apps]] carry it into play.

## What has Cal done on screen?

Almost nothing. Wikipedia's summary of the 26-minute [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] (August 27, 2026) has him heard in dialogue near the end, telling Jason and Lucia to make the most of being young. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> No Cal scene was reported from the October 15 launch trailer, which centered on Jason's backstory.

Rockstar has not said whether Cal gives missions. A friend who listens to Coast Guard traffic is an obvious asset to a crew that moves product by boat, and Brian's bio says he still runs his through a boat yard in the [[world/leonida-keys|Leonida Keys]]. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

### Is Cal GTA 6's Lester?

Fans keep comparing him to Lester Crest, GTA V's housebound heist planner. Rockstar has not made that comparison, and nothing official says Cal plans jobs or handles tech. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> His home isn't named either. Fans place him in the Keys because that's where Jason and Brian are.

## Who voices Cal Hampton?

Fans identify Bobby Moynihan, and SVG and timesaver.gg both list that identification. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Neither Rockstar nor Moynihan has confirmed it in any source we have seen. Rockstar has published no cast list and normally credits actors at the end of the game. The full graded list is on [[characters/gta-6-voice-actors-and-cast|voice actors and cast]].

## Open questions

- Does Cal give missions, or is he comic relief? Rockstar's copy only says Jason wants more than he does.
- Does the Coast Guard eavesdropping turn into a mechanic, such as patrol warnings on boat runs?
- Will the credits confirm Moynihan after launch on November 19?
