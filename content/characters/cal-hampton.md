Cal Hampton is [[characters/jason-duval|Jason Duval]]'s friend, a fellow associate of [[characters/brian-heder|Brian Heder]], and GTA 6's resident conspiracy theorist. Rockstar's bio sets him up as the guy who would rather stay home with the radio scanner than go out on a job.

## Who is Cal Hampton?

Rockstar's Only in Leonida bio says Cal "feels safest hanging at home, snooping on Coast Guard comms with a few beers and some private browser tabs open."

The rest of the bio, summarized:

- **Ties:** Jason's friend, and one of Brian's associates.
- **Outlook:** Rockstar says he is "at the low tide of America and happy there."
- **Contrast with Jason:** Cal is content where he is, while the bio says Jason wants more.

His pull quotes on Rockstar's page lean hard into paranoia: wondering whether everything on the internet is true, suspicious birds flying in formation, and a resigned take on who runs the world.

## What does Cal do in the story?

Rockstar has not said what Cal does in missions. His bio shows how he fits in, but not what he does for the plot.

What we have from the trailers:

- **Extended Look (August 27, 2026):** Cal is heard in dialogue near the end, urging Jason and Lucia to make the most of being young. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (Wikipedia's Extended Look summary)
- **Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025):** his bio went up on Rockstar's site alongside the trailer.

The Coast Guard detail suggests a practical use: someone who knows when the water is being watched is useful to a crew that moves product by boat through the [[world/leonida-keys|Keys]]. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

### Is Cal GTA 6's Lester?

Fans compare Cal to GTA V's Lester Crest. That comparison is fan shorthand, not Rockstar's. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> Nothing official says Cal plans heists or provides tech support.

## Where is Cal based?

Rockstar does not name Cal's home location. Because he is Jason's friend and part of Brian's circle, which is rooted in the Keys, fans assume he lives nearby. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> See [[world/leonida-keys|Leonida Keys]] for the region.

## Why Cal matters for GTA 6's satire

Rockstar's own bios lean on internet culture. Cal's "private browser tabs" and conspiracy streak sit next to the [[characters/real-dimez|Real Dimez]]' social media hustle as part of the game's online-culture satire. That theme carries into gameplay through the in-game social apps covered on [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]].

## Who voices Cal Hampton?

Unconfirmed. Fans identify **Bobby Moynihan**, as reported by SVG and timesaver.gg. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Rockstar has published no cast list, and Moynihan has not confirmed it in any source we have seen. See [[characters/gta-6-voice-actors-and-cast|voice actors and cast]].
