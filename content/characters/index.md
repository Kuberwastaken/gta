GTA 6 has two playable leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, and a supporting cast Rockstar has profiled in eight bio entries covering nine people. That list, on the Only in Leonida page, went up with [[media/gta-6-trailer-2|Trailer 2]] on May 6, 2025. When we checked it on November 15, 2026, nothing had been added. The full official credits now name many more roles. Our [[characters/gta-6-voice-actors-and-cast|cast guide]] limits its table to these pre-release-profile characters, without newly revealed story roles.

## Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Rockstar's synopsis says Jason and Lucia "have always known the deck is stacked against them," and that an easy score gone wrong drops them "on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America." Rob Nelson of Rockstar North told Famitsu: "This game is the story of Jason and Lucia." Rupert Humphries, Rockstar's SVP of narrative, called it "a Bonnie and Clyde-type narrative" in an interview with Dazed. Dazed notes it is the first time a GTA game has put a playable couple in the lead.

- [[characters/jason-duval|Jason Duval]]: Army veteran working for drug runners in the [[world/leonida-keys|Leonida Keys]].
- [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia Caminos]]: fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary, with family roots in Liberty City.

You can [[gameplay/gta-6-character-switching|switch between them]], but Nelson says switching is restricted at points in the story.

## How many characters has Rockstar confirmed?

Eight bios. Real Dimez is one entry and two people, which makes nine. PCGamesN's guide has given both "six" and "eight" as Rockstar's count. The correct count is eight entries covering nine people.

| Lucia Caminos | Playable lead | Vice City, Liberty City roots |
| Cal Hampton | Jason's paranoid friend | Brian's crew |

## Are there four protagonists?

No. Nelson told Famitsu that Rockstar considered four protagonists and dropped the idea, because more leads would mean less time with each and weaker emotional investment. The leaked names "Billy and Kai" tied to that talk are covered in [[leaks/gta-6-four-protagonists-rumour|the four-protagonists rumour]].
