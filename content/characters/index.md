GTA 6 is built around two playable leads, **Jason Duval** and **Lucia Caminos**, a criminal couple caught in a conspiracy that stretches across the state of Leonida. Around them, Rockstar has officially profiled a small supporting cast: eight bio entries on its "Only in Leonida" page, covering nine people.

That official list has not grown since [[media/gta-6-trailer-2|Trailer 2]] in May 2025. When we checked the page on November 15, 2026, it still showed only the original eight profiles. Every other name you have seen comes from the [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]], trailer dialogue, or edition and pre-order copy.

## Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Rockstar's synopsis says Jason and Lucia "have always known the deck is stacked against them," and that an easy score gone wrong drops them "on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America." Rockstar North's Rob Nelson put it more simply to Famitsu: "This game is the story of Jason and Lucia."

- [[characters/jason-duval|Jason Duval]]: an Army veteran running jobs for drug smugglers in the [[world/leonida-keys|Leonida Keys]].
- [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia Caminos]]: fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary, with family roots in Liberty City.

Both are playable, and you can [[gameplay/gta-6-character-switching|switch between them]] when the story allows it.

## How many characters has Rockstar confirmed?

Rockstar has eight official bios. Real Dimez counts as one entry but is two people, which makes nine. PCGamesN's guide has given both "six" and "eight" as Rockstar's count; eight entries and nine people is correct.

| Lucia Caminos | Playable lead | Vice City, Liberty City roots |

## Are there four protagonists?

No. Nelson told Famitsu that Rockstar considered four protagonists and dropped the idea, because more leads would mean less time with each and weaker emotional investment. The leaked names tied to that talk are covered in [[leaks/gta-6-four-protagonists-rumour|the four-protagonists rumour]].
