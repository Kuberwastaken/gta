Raul Bautista is the career criminal in GTA 6's cast, a bank robber who recruits talent for big jobs. Of the eight characters with official Rockstar bios, he is the one whose page most openly predicts trouble for [[characters/jason-duval|Jason]] and [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia]].

## What Rockstar says about Raul

The Only in Leonida bio opens: "Confidence, charm, and cunning - Raul's a seasoned bank robber always on the hunt for talent ready to take the risks that bring the biggest rewards."

The second half is the warning: "Raul's recklessness raises the stakes with every score." Sooner or later, Rockstar writes, his crew "will have to double down or pull their chips from the table."

His pull quotes are a professional's maxims:

- "Experience counts."
- "Life is full of surprises, my friend. I think we'd all be wise to remember that."
- "A professional adapts."

## What does Raul do in the Extended Look?

In the [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] (August 27, 2026), Jason and Lucia drive the getaway for Raul's robbery, and he then invites them into his crew. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> That comes from Wikipedia's summary of the video. Brian Heder gives the pair a job in the same video, so Raul is one of two bio characters shown hiring them. The rest of that footage's cast is on [[characters/extended-look-characters|Extended Look characters]].

## Is Raul behind the "easy score" that goes wrong?

Rockstar hasn't said. The official synopsis says the story begins when "an easy score goes wrong." Wikipedia, citing VGC and Eurogamer, calls the inciting job a failed bank heist. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Rob Nelson told Famitsu only that "a job that was supposed to be simple ends in complete disaster."

A bank robber recruiting the leads makes Raul the obvious suspect, but nothing official links him to that job. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> Treat "bank heist" as a press summary until the game is out.

## How do robberies work in GTA 6?

Red Bull's preview coverage describes the systems Raul's line of work would use. None of this is on Rockstar's own pages. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

- Almost any shop can be robbed, armored vans included.
- Safe codes can be found or bought. Blowing the safe is faster but can damage the loot.
- Loot comes in duffel bags that must be fenced. Fences specialize and unlock through the story.
- Some story beats require a minimum cash amount.

What happens after the alarm is covered in [[gameplay/gta-6-money-banks-and-fences|money, banks and fences]] and [[gameplay/gta-6-wanted-system-and-heat|the wanted and heat system]]. His bio gives no home base, so Raul hasn't been placed anywhere in [[world/vice-city|Vice City]] or the rest of Leonida.

## Who voices Raul Bautista?

timesaver.gg names Manny Perez. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Rockstar has published no cast list, and we have seen no statement from the actor.

Don't confuse him with Manni L. Perez, the actor fans link to Lucia. The names are a letter apart and get mixed up in search results. See [[characters/gta-6-voice-actors-and-cast|voice actors and cast]].
