Raul Bautista is the professional in GTA 6's cast: a career bank robber who recruits talent for big jobs. Rockstar pitches him as the man who can make [[characters/jason-duval|Jason]] and [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia]] serious money, and as the man most likely to get them killed doing it.

## Who is Raul Bautista?

Rockstar's Only in Leonida bio opens: "Confidence, charm, and cunning - Raul's a seasoned bank robber always on the hunt for talent ready to take the risks that bring the biggest rewards."

The catch is in the second half of his bio: "Raul's recklessness raises the stakes with every score." Rockstar adds that sooner or later his crew will have to double down or walk away.

His pull quotes on Rockstar's page are short professional maxims about experience, surprises and adapting under pressure.

## What does Raul do in the Extended Look?

In the [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] (August 27, 2026), Jason and Lucia drive the getaway for Raul's robbery. Afterward, he invites them into his crew. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (Wikipedia's Extended Look summary)

That makes Raul one of the few bio characters shown actively recruiting the pair. Other Extended Look scenes are on [[characters/extended-look-characters|Extended Look characters]].

## Is Raul connected to the "easy score" that goes wrong?

Rockstar has not said. The official synopsis says the story begins when "an easy score goes wrong." Wikipedia, citing VGC and Eurogamer, calls it a failed bank heist. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

A bank robber recruiting the leads makes Raul an obvious suspect, but nothing official links him to the inciting job. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> Treat "bank heist" as a press gloss until the game is out.

## How do robberies work in GTA 6?

Raul's line of work is backed by systems press have described from previews. All of this is <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (Red Bull), not from Rockstar's own pages:

- Almost any shop can be robbed, armored vans included.
- Safe codes can be found or bought, or the safe can be blown, which is faster but may damage the loot.
- Loot comes as duffel bags that must be fenced. Fences specialize and unlock through the story.
- Some story beats require a minimum cash amount.

See [[gameplay/gta-6-money-banks-and-fences|money, banks and fences]] and [[gameplay/gta-6-wanted-system-and-heat|the wanted and heat system]] for what happens after the alarm goes off.

## Where is Raul based?

Rockstar does not say. His bio has no location. Fan wikis say [[media/gta-6-trailer-2|Trailer 2]] shows a robbery at a "City National Bank"-style branch, which would fit a [[world/vice-city|Vice City]] crew. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> No source ties that scene to Raul.

## Who voices Raul Bautista?

Unconfirmed. timesaver.gg names **Manny Perez** as Raul's actor. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Rockstar has published no cast list.

Do not confuse Manny Perez with **Manni L. Perez**, the actor fans link to Lucia. The names are close and often mixed up in search results. See [[characters/gta-6-voice-actors-and-cast|voice actors and cast]].
