**Yes, Jason and Lucia can drift apart.** Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told Edge that the couple start out "wanting to be together... but as the game progresses, it's yours to keep or yours to lose." The romance is optional. If it fades, the two stay partners in crime, because the story still needs both of them.

What Rockstar has **not** said: whether the relationship changes the ending, and whether they can get married.

## Is GTA 6 a Bonnie and Clyde story?

Yes, by Rockstar's own description. Rupert Humphries, Rockstar's SVP of narrative, told Dazed the game is "a Bonnie and Clyde-type narrative."

The comparison is older than the game's reveal. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier reported a Bonnie and Clyde-inspired duo in 2022, and Wikipedia, citing that reporting, says the narrative outline was set by late 2019. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

Rockstar's synopsis frames them as a pair who must rely "on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive." Dazed calls GTA 6 "the first time that a GTA game has featured a playable couple in the lead role."

## How do Jason and Lucia meet?

Rockstar has not shown how they met. The official bios treat the relationship as something already underway but uncertain:

- [[characters/jason-duval|Jason]]'s bio: meeting Lucia "could be the best or worst thing to ever happen to him."
- [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia]]'s bio: "A life with Jason could be her way out."

The bios are framed from opposite angles: Jason is unsure, Lucia sees him as a route out. Both lines are verbatim from Rockstar's Only in Leonida page.

### Can they get married?

Unknown. Nelson declined to confirm or deny marriage in the Edge preview. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (GTAVice.net's summary)

## How does the relationship system work?

Rockstar has described it only in broad strokes. The details below are a mix of grades.

- **Closeness scale.** Edge describes a four-point closeness scale built through partner activities. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> A separate X thread from GameVerse claims a "4-level relationship meter." <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> The two line up, but neither is Rockstar's own description.
- **Dates.** The GameVerse thread lists dates such as pool, mini-golf and kayaking. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> Those activities themselves are reported to be in the game (Red Bull, Edge). See [[gameplay/gta-6-activities-and-side-content|activities and side content]].
- **Together or apart.** Nelson told IGN: "You can do that as Jason and Lucia together, or you can do it on your own." Dazed says players "can choose to either play as a couple, or go off and cause chaos independently."
- **Messaging.** On Snapmatic, Jason and Lucia cannot post publicly because they are fugitives, but they can DM photos to each other. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> See [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]].

## Does the relationship affect the ending?

**Rockstar has not said.** This is one of the clearest contradictions in GTA 6 coverage:

- Red Bull reported that the relationship "will shape the story's course and ending." <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>
- Other guides, including Neonsect, point out that Rockstar has not said how the ending is affected.

We side with caution. Nelson's on-record quotes say the romance is yours to keep or lose and that the pair stay partners regardless. None of them mention the ending. Treat any ending impact as unconfirmed.

Separately, each character has a [[gameplay/gta-6-criminal-profile|Criminal Profile]] ranging from Professional to Irredeemable that reportedly affects the story. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (Edge, Red Bull)

### How many endings does GTA 6 have?

Rockstar has not said. No ending count has been published.

### Do Jason and Lucia die at the end?

Nobody outside Rockstar knows. Theories that they die in a Bonnie and Clyde-style shootout, or kill each other, are fan speculation covered by Sportskeeda. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> The same goes for "Jason betrays Lucia." No Rockstar material points to any specific ending.

## Can you switch between Jason and Lucia at any time?

**No, not at any time.** Switching exists, but the story controls it.

- Dazed describes driving as Jason and swapping to Lucia in the passenger seat, or swapping mid-chase between driving and shooting.
- Nelson to Famitsu: "There are times when it's restricted depending on the progression of the story." Some missions allow free switching; others lock you to one character.
- Red Bull reported you can switch "at any time" after the [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] showed a "Character Wheel" swap mid-drive. That **contradicts** Nelson, and we prefer Nelson's version.

Full details in [[gameplay/gta-6-character-switching|character switching]].

Splitting up has a practical use too. Separating Jason and Lucia is one way to shake a police description in the [[gameplay/gta-6-wanted-system-and-heat|wanted and heat system]]. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## Do their bodies change?

Yes. Dazed reports that diet, exercise and sleep visibly change both characters' bodies. Gyms affect physique, stamina and focus. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (Red Bull)
