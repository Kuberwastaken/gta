Real Dimez are Bae-Luxe and Roxy, a [[world/vice-city|Vice City]] rap duo and the newest signing at Only Raw Records. They share one entry on Rockstar's Only in Leonida page, which is why the eight official bios cover nine people.

## What Rockstar says about Real Dimez

The bio says the pair "have been friends since high school" and had the savvy to turn "shaking down local dealers into cold, hard cash via spicy rap tracks and a relentless social media presence." The hustle came first and the music second.

The career so far, per the same bio: an early single with local rapper DWNPLY "took Real Dimez to new heights." Then came "five years and a whole lot of trouble." They are now signed to Only Raw, "hoping lightning can strike twice."

Their pull quotes read like hooks:

- "Viral videos. Viral hooks."
- "All my dimes in this club. Meet my twin, make it a dub."
- "One hit away from fame."

Rockstar's copy never says which member is Bae-Luxe and which is Roxy.

DWNPLY gets one mention and nothing else: no profile, no image. Whether DWNPLY appears in the game is unknown.

## Are Real Dimez based on real rappers?

Rockstar hasn't named a model. Rupert Humphries, Rockstar's SVP of narrative, told Dazed that Miami bass, Kodak Black and Sexyy Red were influences. Those are influences on the duo's world and sound, not a one-to-one casting.

## How does their story play out in-game?

Dazed reports that the duo's "journey can be traced via in-game viral social media posts." Red Bull reports that the phone's Snapmatic app lets you follow accounts and watch NPC events and livestreams in real time. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Nobody has said you can follow Real Dimez on Snapmatic specifically. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> More on the apps at [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]].

Rockstar also hasn't said whether any Real Dimez song plays on in-game radio. The confirmed music is the six stations revealed on October 8 and *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*, 34 original tracks out November 19.
