Real Dimez are **Bae-Luxe and Roxy**, a [[world/vice-city|Vice City]] rap duo and the newest signing at Only Raw Records. They are one entry on Rockstar's character page but two people, which is why Rockstar's eight official bios cover nine characters.

They are also GTA 6's clearest window into its satire of influencer culture.

## Who are Real Dimez?

Rockstar's Only in Leonida bio says Bae-Luxe and Roxy "have been friends since high school." They are, per Rockstar, girls with the savvy to turn shaking down local dealers into cash through rap tracks and social media.

The rest of the bio, summarized:

- **Origin:** money from shaking down dealers, turned into "spicy rap tracks and a relentless social media presence."
- **Breakthrough:** an early hit single with local rapper **DWNPLY** took them to new heights.
- **Since then:** five years and a lot of trouble.
- **Now:** signed to Only Raw Records, hoping lightning strikes twice.

Their pull quotes on Rockstar's page are short and hook-like, built around going viral and being one hit away from fame.

## Who is DWNPLY?

DWNPLY is a local rapper named in the Real Dimez bio. Rockstar gives no profile, no image and no further detail. Whether DWNPLY appears in the game is unknown.

## Are Real Dimez based on real rappers?

Rockstar has not named a direct model. Rupert Humphries, Rockstar's SVP of narrative, told Dazed that Miami bass, Kodak Black and Sexyy Red were influences. Those are influences on the characters' world and sound, not one-to-one inspirations.

## How does their story play out in-game?

Dazed reports that the Real Dimez's "journey can be traced via in-game viral social media posts." That fits what press have reported about the phone: the **Snapmatic** app lets you follow accounts and watch NPC events and livestreams in real time. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (Red Bull) See [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone and social media]].

Whether you can follow Real Dimez on Snapmatic specifically has not been stated. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

Rockstar has not said. The confirmed music so far is the six revealed radio stations and *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*, 34 original tracks out November 19. See [[music/gta-6-radio-stations|radio stations]] and [[music/gta-6-soundtrack-album|the GTA 6 album]].
