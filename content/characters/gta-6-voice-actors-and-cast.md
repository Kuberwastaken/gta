Rockstar has published the GTA VI cast in its [official credits](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/credits). **November 20 update:** our pre-launch labels for the leads and several supporting actors are now superseded by first-party credits. This is new evidence, not proof that every earlier fan claim or social post was genuine. The historical trail stays on [[leaks/gta-6-actor-identifications|actor identifications]].

The credits write Cal and Dre’Quan without the surnames used in their official profiles. Real Dimez is a duo, so Bae-Luxe and Roxy Rashad have separate performer credits. These are cast credits: they do not separately itemize voice work, motion capture, facial capture or stunt doubles for each lead. We therefore do not infer that one credited actor performed every part of a character's production.

## Who plays Jason and Lucia?

**Dylan Rourke is Jason Duval; Manni L. Perez is Lucia Caminos.** Both pairings appear directly in Rockstar's Main Cast section. That replaces voice comparisons, likeness theories and user-edited IMDb listings as the evidence. The actors' identities do not establish anything about [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|the characters' relationship]] or the story's outcome.

## Is Stephen Root Brian Heder?

Yes. Rockstar explicitly pairs Brian Heder with Stephen Root. Our older distinction between Root saying he was in the game and press identifying his character was appropriate to that earlier evidence, but it is no longer the current answer. The role now has a first-party credit.

## Are Manny Perez and Manni L. Perez the same person?

No. Rockstar lists **Manny Perez** for Raul Bautista and **Manni L. Perez** for Lucia Caminos. Keep the exact credited names when looking up the performers; similar spellings are not interchangeable.

## What happened to the old rumours?

The confirmed matches are regraded on the [[leaks/index|leaks ledger]]. Earlier claims involving an alleged social-media confirmation remain a separate provenance question.
