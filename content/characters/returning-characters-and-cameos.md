**No returning GTA character is confirmed to appear in GTA 6.** What Rockstar has confirmed is a steady stream of references, mostly to Tommy Vercetti and the original *Vice City*, worked into pre-order items, landmarks and radio.

Rob Nelson set expectations to Famitsu: "The universes are separate, but we like looking back at previous titles in slightly clever ways."

## Is Tommy Vercetti in GTA 6?

**Not confirmed.** Tommy's name is all over the marketing, but as references, not as a character.

What Rockstar's own pages include:

- A tropical weapon pattern "inspired by Tommy Vercetti's iconic palm tree button-up."
- The **Hawk & Little Morgan** revolvers, "sourced from the Vercetti Estate."
- The Vintage Vice City Pack, with a '55 Vapid Stanier and a pastel linen suit.
- The **Ocean View Hotel**, Tommy's hotel in *Vice City*, which is shown in [[media/gta-6-trailer-1|Trailer 1]] (Den of Geek) and named on Rockstar's pages.

Famitsu also notes Tommy's name appears in the pack description. The references are <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. **Tommy appearing in the game is not.** A cameo or return is <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>, as whengta6.com and others note. Full detail is in [[leaks/tommy-vercetti-return-rumour|the Tommy Vercetti return rumour]], and the pack itself is on [[release/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses|pre-order bonuses]].

## Is Phil Cassidy back?

**Unconfirmed.** Den of Geek's [[media/gta-6-trailer-2|Trailer 2]] breakdown spotted an Ammu-Nation ad featuring a Phil Cassidy lookalike, possibly his son. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

Fans have also nicknamed an Ammu-Nation owner "Phil." GTA Intel flags the claim that this is an older Phil Cassidy as unsourced to Rockstar. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>

## Are Michael, Franklin or Trevor in GTA 6?

There is no confirmation. Cameo claims for GTA V's Michael, Franklin and Trevor, and for Lamar, circulate in guides such as timesaver.gg, but none rests on Rockstar material. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>

The [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] did reference Los Santos and Blaine County, GTA V's setting, per Engadget. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> A place name is not a character, but it does show GTA V's world exists somewhere in GTA 6's universe.

## Is Val really Valentina from GTA Online?

A character called "Val" appears in the Extended Look, tied to the Megamundo security job. Fans claim she is Valentina from GTA Online. GTA Intel lists it as a fan identification; timesaver.gg calls it "confirmed," which overstates it. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> See [[characters/extended-look-characters|Extended Look characters]].

A related thread: fans link Lucia's rumored actor, Manni L. Perez, to an earlier GTA Online role (SVG). That is part of the basis for the fan ID, not evidence of a returning character. See [[characters/gta-6-voice-actors-and-cast|voice actors and cast]].

## Why the references but no cameos?

Rockstar has not explained its plans. Nelson's "universes are separate" line suggests Rockstar treats the 3D-era games and the current era as different continuities, with nods rather than crossovers. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> The original *Vice City* was set in the '80s, and Rockstar's own Vice City tagline today says "we're a long way from the '80s."
