No character from an earlier GTA is confirmed to appear in GTA 6. What Rockstar has confirmed is a run of references, mostly to Tommy Vercetti and the original *Vice City*, built into pre-order items, landmarks and radio. Rob Nelson set the terms in his Famitsu interview: "The universes are separate, but we like looking back at previous titles in slightly clever ways."

## Is Tommy Vercetti in GTA 6?

Not confirmed. His name is all over the marketing, but as set dressing. Rockstar's own pages include:

- a tropical weapon pattern "inspired by Tommy Vercetti's iconic palm tree button-up";
- the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, "sourced from the Vercetti Estate";
- the Vintage Vice City Pack, with a '55 Vapid Stanier and a pastel linen suit;
- the Ocean View Hotel, Tommy's hotel in *Vice City*, shown in [[media/gta-6-trailer-1|Trailer 1]] per Den of Geek and named on Rockstar's pages.

Famitsu also notes Tommy's name appears in the pack description. The references are <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. A cameo or return is <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>, as whengta6.com and others point out. The full case is on [[leaks/tommy-vercetti-return-rumour|the Tommy Vercetti return rumour]], and the pack itself on [[release/gta-6-pre-order-bonuses|pre-order bonuses]].

## Is Phil Cassidy back?

Unconfirmed. Den of Geek's [[media/gta-6-trailer-2|Trailer 2]] breakdown spotted an Ammu-Nation ad with a Phil Cassidy lookalike, possibly his son. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> Fans have separately nicknamed an Ammu-Nation owner "Phil," and GTA Intel flags the idea that he is an older Phil Cassidy as unsourced to Rockstar. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>

## Are Michael, Franklin or Trevor in GTA 6?

No. Cameo claims for GTA V's three leads and Lamar circulate in guides such as timesaver.gg, and none rests on Rockstar material. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>

The [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] did mention Los Santos and Blaine County, GTA V's setting, per Engadget. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> That puts GTA V's state on the map of GTA 6's world as a place name, nothing more.

## Is Val really Valentina from GTA Online?

"Val" appears in the Extended Look, tied to the Megamundo security job. Fans say Val is Valentina from GTA Online. GTA Intel lists that as a fan identification; timesaver.gg calls it "confirmed," which overstates it. <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span> See [[characters/extended-look-characters|Extended Look characters]].

A related thread: SVG notes fans link Manni L. Perez, Lucia's rumored actor, to an earlier GTA Online role. That is part of the basis for the fan casting, not evidence of a returning character. See [[characters/gta-6-voice-actors-and-cast|voice actors and cast]].

## Why references but no cameos?

Rockstar hasn't explained. Nelson's "universes are separate" line reads like nods, not crossovers. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> The original *Vice City* was set in the '80s, and Rockstar's current [[world/vice-city|Vice City]] tagline says "we're a long way from the '80s."
