Macca the Gator is a cartoon, not a person you meet on the street. He stars in Macca & Friends, an animated series that plays on TV inside GTA 6. Rockstar's VI homepage and Only in Leonida page list *Macca the Gator* next to Chunkee the Manatee as part of Leonida's in-universe pop culture. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> Everything else about the show comes from a teaser and press coverage of it.

## What the teaser shows

A 30-second trailer for Macca & Friends surfaced in mid-November 2026, posted first by the X account DiscussingFilm. Insider Gaming, writing on November 13, says the show "follows the adventures of Macca the Gator and his wetland friends across the state of Leonida." The teaser ends on "Coming November 19 on WEAZEL," the game's fictional TV network. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

Rockstar Report says Rockstar reposted the teaser. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> We haven't seen it on Rockstar's own site. The November 19 date is in-universe flavor matched to the real launch. For when the game itself unlocks, see [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|unlock times by country]].

Insider Gaming names the crab "Pinchy." Rockstar Report writes "Pinche." No official spelling has been published, and in-game listings should settle it after launch.

A landlord crab lands in a state where Rockstar's bios run on rent deals. [[characters/jason-duval|Jason]] lives rent-free at one of [[characters/brian-heder|Brian Heder]]'s properties in exchange for shakedowns. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

## Where else Macca turns up

Insider Gaming says Macca and his friends also appear on physical Leonida maps and in the collector's box. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> The box is covered on [[release/gta-6-collectors-box|the collector's box page]], and the map leak that came from it on [[world/gta-6-leaked-map-and-counties|the leaked map page]].

## Why a gator?

Alligators are Leonida's signature animal. Rockstar's blurb for [[world/grassrivers|Grassrivers]], its Everglades stand-in, says "the gators may be the most famous attraction." Game Informer counts alligators among the game's more than 170 animal species. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> [[media/gta-6-trailer-1|Trailer 1]] restaged a real Florida viral moment, an alligator in a convenience store, per Den of Geek. More on the animals at [[world/gta-6-wildlife-and-animals|wildlife and animals]].

## Open questions

Rockstar hasn't explained how TV works in GTA 6, which channels exist besides WEAZEL, or whether shows run on a schedule. Until it does, nobody knows:

- whether Macca & Friends is a full series or a short loop;
- whether you can catch the "premiere" on day one;
- whether Macca merchandise shows up around Vice City;
- what the crab is called.
