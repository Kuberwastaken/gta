Macca the Gator is GTA 6's cartoon mascot: the star of **Macca & Friends**, an animated series you can watch on TV inside the game. He is not a story character you meet on the street. He is part of Leonida's fictional pop culture, the same way the series has always filled its TV channels with parody shows.

## Who is Macca the Gator?

Rockstar's own pages name *Macca the Gator* as an in-universe TV show, listed alongside Chunkee the Manatee as part of Leonida pop culture. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (Rockstar VI homepage and Only in Leonida)

The show itself got a trailer in November 2026. Insider Gaming reports that Macca & Friends "follows the adventures of Macca the Gator and his wetland friends across the state of Leonida." <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## When does Macca & Friends premiere?

On launch day. A 30-second teaser, revealed exclusively by the X account DiscussingFilm, said: "Coming November 19 on WEAZEL." WEAZEL is the game's fictional TV network. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (Insider Gaming, November 13, 2026)

Rockstar Report says Rockstar reposted the teaser. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> We have not seen Rockstar post about it on its own site.

That date is in-universe flavor timed to the real launch. It is not a real-world TV series. For when the game itself unlocks, see [[release/gta-6-release-time-by-country|unlock times by country]].

**Contradiction:** Insider Gaming names the crab "Pinchy." Rockstar Report writes "Pinche." We have not seen an official spelling. Expect the in-game credits or TV listings to settle it after launch.

A crab landlord is a pointed joke for a state where Rockstar's bios are full of people scraping by on rent deals, like [[characters/jason-duval|Jason]] living rent-free at [[characters/brian-heder|Brian Heder]]'s place in exchange for shakedowns. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

## Where else does Macca appear?

Insider Gaming says Macca and his friends also appear on physical Leonida maps and in the Collector's Box. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> The collector's box is covered on [[release/gta-6-collectors-box|the collector's box page]], and the map leak that came from it on [[world/gta-6-leaked-map-and-counties|the leaked map page]].

## Why a gator?

Alligators are Leonida's signature animal. Rockstar's description of [[world/grassrivers|Grassrivers]], its Everglades stand-in, says "the gators may be the most famous attraction," and Game Informer reports alligators among the game's 170-plus animal species. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> See [[world/gta-6-wildlife-and-animals|wildlife and animals]].

[[media/gta-6-trailer-1|Trailer 1]] even recreated a real Florida viral moment, an alligator in a convenience store, per Den of Geek. Macca is the cuddly, merchandised version of the same Florida mascot.

## How do you watch Macca & Friends in GTA 6?

Rockstar has not explained how in-game TV works in GTA 6, which channels exist beyond WEAZEL, or whether Macca & Friends runs on a schedule.
