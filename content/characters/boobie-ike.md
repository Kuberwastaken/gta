Boobie Ike is the established money in [[world/vice-city|Vice City]]'s music scene. Rockstar's bio calls him "a local Vice City legend - and acts like it," and makes him co-owner of Only Raw Records with [[characters/dre-quan-priest|Dre'Quan Priest]]. The label's signing is the rap duo [[characters/real-dimez|Real Dimez]].

## What Rockstar says about Boobie

Per the Only in Leonida bio, he is one of the few to turn his time in the streets into "a legitimate empire" of real estate, a strip club and a recording studio, and he's "all smiles until it's time to talk business." Of everything he owns, the partnership with Dre'Quan is the one he is "most invested in." The bio closes on the problem: "now they just need a hit."

His three pull quotes undercut the word "legitimate":

- "It's all about heart - the Jack of Hearts."
- "The club money pay for the studio, and the drug money pay for it all."
- "Top quality cuts."

The second line is the character in one sentence. The strip club funds the label and drug money funds everything, which puts him in the same trade as [[characters/jason-duval|Jason]] and [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia]]. Rockstar has not explained what the Jack of Hearts is.

Read the three music bios together and the money chain is complete. Dre'Quan used to book acts into Boobie's strip club. He then signed Real Dimez, who are now "signed to Only Raw Records, hoping lightning can strike twice."

## What does Boobie do in the Extended Look?

He is the client for Jason and Lucia's drug pickup in the [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] (August 27, 2026). <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> That comes from Wikipedia's summary of the video. The same video shows a pickup at Raymond's drug house broken up by a raid led by Méndez, a dirty VCPD cop. The summary doesn't say whether that is Boobie's job. The other names from that footage are on [[characters/extended-look-characters|Extended Look characters]].

## Who is Boobie Ike based on?

Rockstar hasn't named a model, and comparisons to real Miami figures are fan guesses. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span> The only real names Rockstar has attached to the Only Raw storyline are musicians. Narrative SVP Rupert Humphries told Dazed that Miami bass, Kodak Black and Sexyy Red fed into Real Dimez. He was talking about the duo, not Boobie.

His club and studio haven't been placed on a map either. The Vice City districts Rockstar has named, such as Ocean Beach and Little Cuba, are on [[world/gta-6-landmarks-and-neighborhoods|landmarks and neighborhoods]].
