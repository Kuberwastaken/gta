Boobie Ike is the established money in GTA 6's [[world/vice-city|Vice City]] music scene. Rockstar casts him as a former street figure who went legitimate, at least on paper, and now owns a slice of everything from property to a strip club to a recording studio.

## Who is Boobie Ike?

Rockstar's Only in Leonida bio opens: "Boobie is a local Vice City legend - and acts like it." He is, Rockstar says, one of the few to turn his time in the streets into a legitimate empire.

From the bio:

- **Empire:** real estate, a strip club and a recording studio.
- **Personality:** all smiles until it is time to talk business.
- **Priority:** of all his ventures, his partnership with young music mogul [[characters/dre-quan-priest|Dre'Quan Priest]] at **Only Raw Records** is the one he is most invested in.
- **Problem:** Rockstar's bio says simply, "now they just need a hit."

His pull quotes on Rockstar's page are blunt about where the money really comes from: the club funds the studio, and drug money funds all of it. Another line talks about heart and the Jack of Hearts, which Rockstar has not explained.

## How legitimate is Boobie's empire?

Not very, by his own pull quotes. Rockstar's copy describes a legitimate business portfolio, then has Boobie admit the drug money pays for everything. That gap between front and reality is the whole joke of the character, and it ties him directly to [[characters/jason-duval|Jason]] and [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia]]'s line of work.

## What does Boobie do in the Extended Look?

In the [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] (August 27, 2026), Boobie is the client for Jason and Lucia's drug pickup. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (Wikipedia's Extended Look summary)

The same video shows a pickup at Raymond's drug house going wrong when a dirty cop named Méndez leads a raid. Our sources do not say outright that these are the same job. See [[characters/extended-look-characters|Extended Look characters]].

## Who is Boobie Ike based on?

Rockstar has not said Boobie is modeled on anyone. Comparisons to real Miami figures circulate among fans, but they are guesses. <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>

For context, Rockstar's narrative lead Rupert Humphries cited Miami bass, Kodak Black and Sexyy Red as influences when discussing the Real Dimez with Dazed. That was about the duo, not Boobie.

## Where is Boobie based?

Vice City. Rockstar calls him a local legend there. His specific club and studio locations have not been placed on a map. See [[world/gta-6-landmarks-and-neighborhoods|landmarks and neighborhoods]] for the districts Rockstar has named.
