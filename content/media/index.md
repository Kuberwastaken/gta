Rockstar has released **four major GTA 6 videos** in three years: **Trailer 1** on December 4, 2023, **Trailer 2** on May 6, 2025, the **Extended Look** gameplay special on August 27, 2026, and the **Launch Trailer** on October 15, 2026. The runtimes below come from Rockstar's own video page, which we checked on November 15, 2026.

Some sites list Trailer 2 as 2:46 and the Launch Trailer as "about 90 seconds". Rockstar's page says 2:47 and 1:29. Rockstar also teased an in-game animated show, *Macca & Friends*, in mid-November <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>; see [[characters/macca-the-gator|Macca the Gator]].

## Which GTA 6 trailer should I watch first?

If you have two minutes, the [[media/gta-6-launch-trailer|Launch Trailer]] is the most current pitch for the story. If you have half an hour, the [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] shows how the game plays, from the wanted system to character switching. Trailer 1 is still the cultural moment: it set Guinness records for views and likes in 24 hours.

## Is there a GTA 6 Trailer 3?

Yes, in practice. Rockstar named it the "Launch Trailer", and press called it Trailer 3. Forbes and others spent summer 2026 predicting a third trailer. What came first was the Extended Look, a 26:48 special that premiered on Netflix, then the shorter Launch Trailer in October.
