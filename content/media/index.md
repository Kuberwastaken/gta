Rockstar has put out four major GTA 6 videos in three years. Trailer 1 (December 4, 2023, 1:31) and Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025, 2:47) were cinematic trailers. An Extended Look (August 27, 2026, 26:48) was a gameplay special that premiered on Netflix. The Launch Trailer (October 15, 2026, 1:29) closed the campaign. Runtimes and dates are from Rockstar's own video list, checked November 15, 2026.

## Which GTA 6 trailer got the most views?

Depends on who counts. Trailer 1 holds the Guinness record for a game trailer's first 24 hours on YouTube: 90,421,491 views and 8.9M likes. Rockstar claimed 475M views for Trailer 2 across all platforms in 24 hours without saying how it counted; its YouTube count of about 65M to 85M stayed below Trailer 1. Netflix reported 31.1M views of the Extended Look in four days, using hours watched divided by runtime. There is no verified figure for the Launch Trailer.

## Is there a GTA 6 Trailer 3?

Not under that name. Forbes and others spent June and July 2026 predicting a summer "Trailer 3". Rockstar shipped the 26:48 Extended Look in August instead, then the 1:29 Launch Trailer in October, which Game Rant headlined as Trailer 3. If you watch one thing, the Extended Look shows the most systems; the Launch Trailer is the current two-minute story pitch.
