GTA 6 has **more than 170 animal species** "across land, air, and sea", Rockstar told Game Informer in a September 29, 2026 exclusive, including some never seen in a Rockstar game <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Animals are tiered as **Rare, Discoverable and Legendary**, can be tracked, and **hunting and fishing** are both in.

We grade this page "reported" because the details reached us through Game Informer and preview coverage rather than a Rockstar post, even though Rockstar was the source.

## What animals are in GTA 6?

Rockstar named a handful to Game Informer, split between Florida natives and invasive species <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

| Native | Invasive |
| --- | --- |
| Alligators | Green iguanas |
| Flamingos | Cane toads |
| Manatees | Feral cats |
| Pelicans | Rhesus macaques |

The invasive list is a very Florida detail: the game is modeling the state's real ecological problems as well as its postcard animals.

Other sightings <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

- **Dolphins and a shark** in Trailer 1 <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>, plus an **alligator inside a convenience store**, a nod to a real Florida viral moment.
- **Boars and deer** in an in-game TV ad shown in the Extended Look, per Engadget.
- **Dogs**, which can be petted or scolded.

A breakdown of 60-plus bird species and 30-plus mammals circulates in search snippets from Sportskeeda and others, but we couldn't verify it on the page <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>.

## Are there legendary animals in GTA 6?

Yes, as a tier. Game Informer reports three tiers, **Rare, Discoverable and Legendary**, and says animals can be tracked <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar hasn't said which species are Legendary or what you get for finding them. RDR2 players will recognize the idea.

Animals can also be examined and logged in a checklist that VICE compares to RDR2's compendium <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## Can you hunt and fish in GTA 6?

Both are confirmed activities in Red Bull's preview roundup <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>, and Rockstar's own description of [[world/mount-kalaga|Mount Kalaga]] promises "prime hunting, fishing, and off-road trails" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Rockstar's edition copy also names **Gambit Bay** as a fishing spot <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. How hunting rewards work (pelts, meat, money) hasn't been explained. See [[gameplay/gta-6-activities-and-side-content|activities and side content]].

## Where do the animals live?

Rockstar hasn't mapped species to regions, but its region copy points the way <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>:

- **[[world/grassrivers|Grassrivers]]**: "The gators may be the most famous attraction, but there are far deadlier predators."
- **[[world/leonida-keys|Leonida Keys]]**: "some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in all of America."
- **Mount Kalaga**: hunting country.

Zoo visits are also listed among activities <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## How do the animals look?

Game Informer reports new fur and feather systems with iridescence, bioluminescence and translucency <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar hasn't shown how animals react to the confirmed [[world/gta-6-weather-and-hurricanes|storms and hurricanes]].

## Can you pet dogs in GTA 6?

Yes, per VICE, and you can scold them too <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Red Bull adds a very GTA detail: owners can refuse to let you pet their dog.

## Gators in Leonida pop culture

The in-game animated series *Macca the Gator* and the mascot Chunkee the Manatee are part of Leonida's TV and branding <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Meet him at [[characters/macca-the-gator|Macca the Gator]]. More on the state at the [[world/index|world hub]].
