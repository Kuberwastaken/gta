GTA 6 has more than 170 animal species "across land, air, and sea", Rockstar told Game Informer's Marcus Stewart in a September 29, 2026 exclusive, including some never seen in a Rockstar game <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Animals come in three tiers, Rare, Discoverable and Legendary, and can be tracked. The page is graded "reported" because Rockstar spoke through Game Informer and previews, not its own site.

## What animals are in GTA 6?

Rockstar named eight species to Game Informer, split between Florida natives and invasives <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

| Native | Invasive |
| --- | --- |
| Alligators | Green iguanas |
| Flamingos | Cane toads |
| Manatees | Feral cats |
| Pelicans | Rhesus macaques |

Rockstar volunteering invasive species at all is the tell: the game models Florida's ecological problems, not just its postcard animals <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>.

Other confirmed sightings: dolphins and a shark in [[media/gta-6-trailer-1|Trailer 1]], plus an alligator wandering a convenience store, a recreation of a real Florida viral clip (Den of Geek) <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Engadget spotted boars and deer in an in-game TV ad during the Extended Look <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. A breakdown of 60-plus bird species and 30-plus mammals circulates in Sportskeeda search snippets but isn't verifiable on the page <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>.

## How do Rare, Discoverable and Legendary animals work?

Game Informer reports the three tiers and tracking but no rewards <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. VICE says animals can be examined and logged in a checklist it compares to RDR2's compendium <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar hasn't named a Legendary species or said what logging one earns you.

## Can you hunt and fish in GTA 6?

Yes. Red Bull lists both as activities <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar's own copy sells [[world/mount-kalaga|Mount Kalaga]] on "prime hunting, fishing, and off-road trails" and names Gambit Bay as a fishing spot <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. What a kill or catch is worth has not been explained. Zoo visits are on the same activity list; the rest is on [[gameplay/gta-6-activities-and-side-content|activities and side content]].

## Where do the animals live?

Rockstar hasn't mapped species to regions, but its copy for [[world/grassrivers|Grassrivers]], the [[world/leonida-keys|Leonida Keys]] and Mount Kalaga points to three habitats <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>:

| Region | Rockstar's line |
| --- | --- |
| Grassrivers | "The gators may be the most famous attraction, but there are far deadlier predators" |
| Leonida Keys | "Some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in all of America" |
| Mount Kalaga | "Prime hunting, fishing, and off-road trails" |

## What's new in how animals look and behave?

Game Informer reports new fur and feather systems capable of iridescence, bioluminescence and translucency <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Dogs can be petted or scolded (VICE), and Red Bull adds that an owner can refuse to let you pet theirs <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar hasn't said how wildlife reacts to the confirmed [[world/gta-6-weather-and-hurricanes|storms and hurricanes]].

Leonida's TV and branding lean on animals too: the cartoon *Macca the Gator* and the mascot Chunkee the Manatee appear in Rockstar's material <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. See [[characters/macca-the-gator|Macca the Gator]] and the [[world/index|world hub]].
