The **Leonida Keys** are GTA 6's version of the **Florida Keys**: a chain of tropical islands and bridges off the southern tip of Leonida. In the story it's **Jason Duval's home turf**, where he works for local drug runners, and the base of veteran smuggler **Brian Heder**.

Rockstar sells it as the slow lane of Leonida: "Life in this tropical archipelago isn't flashy but it's easy." The catch, in the same breath, is that you are "right on the doorstep of some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in all of America."

## What is the real-life version of the Leonida Keys?

The Florida Keys, per GTABase and GTA BOOM <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The clearest clue is a long bridge in Trailer 1 that Den of Geek identifies as a Seven Mile Bridge analogue, part of the Overseas Highway (US-1) in real life. Rockstar hasn't named the in-game bridge or the highway.

Both outlets stress that Rockstar compresses its geography, so don't expect every real key to appear. The full comparison table is on [[world/gta-6-real-life-locations|GTA 6 real-life locations]].

## Who lives in the Leonida Keys?

The Keys are tied to two official characters <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>:

- **[[characters/jason-duval|Jason Duval]]**. After the Army, he "found himself in the Keys doing what he knows best, working for local drug runners." He lives rent-free at one of Brian's properties in exchange for help with local shakedowns.
- **[[characters/brian-heder|Brian Heder]]**, "a classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys", still moves product through his boat yard with his third wife, **Lori**. Jason is expected to stop by "for Lori's sangria once in a while."

[[characters/cal-hampton|Cal Hampton]], Jason's friend and another of Brian's associates, spends his time "snooping on Coast Guard comms", which fits a Keys setting, though Rockstar doesn't say where he lives <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>.

In the [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]], Brian hands Jason and Lucia a job over dinner and warns Jason not to sink deeper into the underworld <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## What can you do in the Keys?

The official copy says "Get your buzz on and pull up a deck chair." The gameplay previews give it substance <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

- **Scuba diving.** Diving is shown in the Extended Look, and Rockstar's Keys artwork shows divers on reefs. Oxygen, loot and depth limits haven't been explained.
- **Boating.** Rockstar names the Shitzu Squalo boat and the Crest Kayak on its edition pages <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Jet skis are also in.
- **Fishing.** Confirmed as an activity by Red Bull's preview roundup. Rockstar's edition copy names Gambit Bay for fishing, though not which region it's in.

See [[gameplay/gta-6-activities-and-side-content|activities and side content]] and [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|vehicles]] for more.

## What wildlife is in the Keys?

Trailer 1 shows dolphins and a shark offshore <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Rockstar told Game Informer the game has over 170 species "across land, air, and sea", and names manatees and pelicans among the natives <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar's warning about "dangerous waters" is the closest thing to an official hint about sharks. Full list: [[world/gta-6-wildlife-and-animals|GTA 6 wildlife]].

## What hasn't been shown yet

Rockstar hasn't shown a full map of the island chain, said how far south it runs, or confirmed whether hurricanes hit the Keys harder than the mainland.
