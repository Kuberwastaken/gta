The Leonida Keys are GTA 6's Florida Keys, an island chain off the southern tip of Leonida and the home base of [[characters/jason-duval|Jason Duval]]. Rockstar sells it as the slow lane of the state: "Life in this tropical archipelago isn't flashy but it's easy." Then the warning: you are "right on the doorstep of some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in all of America" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

It is one of [[world/leonida-regions|Leonida's six official regions]], first profiled with Trailer 2 in May 2025, and Rockstar's October 15, 2026 launch trailer shows the Keys again as part of Jason's backstory <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

## What is the real-life version of the Leonida Keys?

The Florida Keys, per GTABase and GTA BOOM <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The clearest tell is a long bridge in Trailer 1 that Den of Geek identifies as a Seven Mile Bridge analogue. The real bridge is part of US-1, the Overseas Highway. Rockstar has named neither the in-game bridge nor the highway.

Both outlets say Rockstar compresses and blends geography, so don't expect every real key to appear. The full table of matches is on [[world/gta-6-real-life-locations|GTA 6 real-life locations]].

## Who lives in the Keys?

Two official bios are set here <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Jason left the Army and "found himself in the Keys doing what he knows best, working for local drug runners." [[characters/brian-heder|Brian Heder]], "a classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys," still moves product through his boat yard with his third wife, Lori. Brian lets Jason live rent-free at one of his properties "so long as he helps with local shakedowns, and stops by for Lori's sangria once in a while."

[[characters/cal-hampton|Cal Hampton]], Jason's friend and another of Brian's associates, spends his evenings "snooping on Coast Guard comms with a few beers," though his bio doesn't say where he lives. In the [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]], Brian hands Jason and Lucia a job over dinner and warns Jason against sinking deeper into the underworld <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## What can you do in the Keys?

Rockstar's copy says "Get your buzz on and pull up a deck chair." The previews say what that means in practice <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Scuba diving is shown in the Extended Look, and Rockstar's Keys art shows divers on reefs; oxygen and depth rules are unexplained. Kayaking, jet skiing and fishing are listed activities in Red Bull's preview roundup, and Rockstar's edition copy names Gambit Bay as a fishing spot without placing it in a region.

For boats, Rockstar's edition pages name the Crest Kayak and the Shitzu Squalo, the latter docked at Washington Beach in Vice City <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Fan trailer IDs add the Shitzu Tropic <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. The full lists are on [[gameplay/gta-6-activities-and-side-content|activities and side content]] and [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|GTA 6 vehicles]].

## What lives in the water?

Trailer 1 shows dolphins and a shark offshore <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Rockstar told Game Informer the game has over 170 species "across land, air, and sea," naming manatees and pelicans among the natives <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The "dangerous waters" line is the nearest Rockstar has come to promising shark encounters. Species lists are on [[world/gta-6-wildlife-and-animals|GTA 6 wildlife]].

Rockstar has not said how far south the chain runs or whether seasonal hurricanes, confirmed for the game since September 29, 2026, hit the islands harder than the mainland; see [[world/gta-6-weather-and-hurricanes|weather and hurricanes]] and the [[world/index|world hub]].
