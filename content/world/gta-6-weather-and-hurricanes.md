Yes. Rockstar told Game Informer (Marcus Stewart, Sep 29, 2026) that GTA 6 has tropical storms and seasonal hurricanes <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Hurricanes had been a rumour for years; this was the first time Rockstar put its name to them. The grade is "reported" because the details came through Game Informer's exclusive rather than a Rockstar post.

## What weather is confirmed in GTA 6?

Everything below is from Game Informer's exclusive, except the last row, which Aaron Garbut gave to Edge for its Oct 29, 2026 cover feature <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

| Feature | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Tropical storms | Yes |
| Seasonal hurricanes | Yes; frequency not disclosed |
| Localized storms | You can stand in sunshine while rain falls in the distance |
| Wet surfaces | Puddles and wet surfaces persist after rain |
| Moving clouds | Cloud formations travel across the map |
| Rainbows | Appear in the right light |
| Day-night cycle | Longer than before, with extended sunsets (Garbut, Edge) |

Localized rain was a preview claim; Kotaku's later flight observation below supplies a concrete example of entering and leaving a rainstorm. It does not establish an exact weather schedule for every part of the map. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

## Why might it not be raining in my game?

**November 25 source check:** [Rockstar's November 23 launch-update notes](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/sN5pEdFYZdlvijKXd6xpJ/grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-updates-ps5-xbox-series-x-or-s) say the update fixed weather staying unchanged for extended periods. That is a specific acknowledged issue, not a promise of constant rain, a fixed storm timer or a cure for every weather complaint. The [[release/gta-6-day-one-patch|patch tracker]] separates these official notes from reported console build numbers. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (official patch notes)

In [Kotaku's November 24 report](https://kotaku.com/yes-despite-what-some-say-online-there-are-thunderstorms-in-gta-6-2001000112), **Zack Zwiezen says he saw rainstorms and fog during his own pre-release play**. He describes flying from sunshine into dark clouds and rain, then leaving the rain behind. That is a hands-on observation of **localized weather**, beyond the earlier promotional screenshots, but not our own test of a save or console version. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (journalist's play experience)

| Evidence | What it supports | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official stuck-weather fix | Rockstar addressed weather remaining unchanged too long | Every dry spell was a bug, or every save is now fixed |
| Kotaku's flight observation | Rain can occur in one area while another is sunny | A fixed route, storm frequency or map-wide forecast |
| Other players' dry-weather complaints relayed in the article | Some people still report little rain | A reproduced post-patch defect, failure rate or diagnosed cause |

The article also discusses a possible early-story weather restriction, but does not establish it. We are **not** treating a chapter threshold as an unlock rule or recommending story progress as a guaranteed weather fix. Rain and fog observations also do not verify hurricane destruction, flooding or every storm type in the previews below. Check the installed update, but do not infer a broken save merely from clear skies.

## How do lighting, fog and clouds interact?

**November 25 developer account:** in [Final Weapon's pre-launch Q&A, published by Noah Hunter](https://finalweapon.net/2026/11/25/gta-6-interview-rockstar-games-world-design-music-combat/), **Owen Shepherd, vice president of art for lighting and rendering**, describes building lighting systems that could respond to the world rather than a single fixed look. He says the direction evolved during development. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (developer interview)

| Example Shepherd describes | What the example supports |
| --- | --- |
| Players can destroy more lights; opening a door changes a dark motel room | Interior lighting can respond to player actions and the global illumination system |
| Streetlights warm up, apartment lights come on, and businesses later turn lights off | Night lighting has timed and animated variation beyond the sun moving |
| Clouds shadow the atmosphere, small lights illuminate fog, and city lights illuminate clouds | Local light and the volumetric atmosphere affect one another |

Shepherd links the atmospheric work to the volumetric system built for Red Dead Redemption 2, then expanded for GTA VI. This is a developer's explanation of the design, **not our own comparison of rendered frames**, a numerical graphics benchmark or a new lighting patch. His examples do not mean every light is destructible, every building is enterable, every switch is interactive or every light follows the same schedule.

The interview also does not provide a storm timer, a hurricane-unlock rule, flood behavior or a claim that all console rendering modes are identical. Keep those questions separate from the [[release/gta-6-ps5-pro-performance|console performance guide]] and the separately sourced weather observations above. The [[music/gta-6-justice-score#how-does-gta-vi-adapt-the-score-during-play|adaptive-score explanation]] covers a different system discussed in the same interview.
