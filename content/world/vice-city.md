Vice City is GTA 6's present-day Miami and the city in the game's tagline, "Vice City, USA." Rockstar calls it "the glamour, hustle, and greed of America captured in a single city," and adds, for anyone expecting 1986: "We're a long way from the '80s, but Vice City is still the sun and fun capital of America" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

Wikipedia, citing Dazed, reports that Rockstar's field team researched Miami from 2015 and that more than 50 street artists painted murals for the city's neighborhoods <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. It is one of [[world/leonida-regions|Leonida's six official regions]].

## What neighborhoods are in Vice City?

Rockstar's "Only in Leonida" tour runs beach to docks: the "pastel art deco hotels and bright white sands of Ocean Beach," the "bustling panaderías of Little Cuba," the "bootleg brands of the Tisha-Wocka flea market," and the VC Port, "the cruise ship capital of the world" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

Rockstar's edition and pre-order pages name more spots in and around the city <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>:

| Place | What Rockstar puts there |
| --- | --- |
| Shore Drive | Named in edition copy, a callback to the 2002 game |
| Shore Court garage | "A stone's throw from... Ocean Beach" |
| Washington Beach | Where the Shitzu Squalo boat is docked |
| Southside Vice City | The PTT Youngin$ gang compound |
| Ocean View Hotel | Tommy Vercetti's hotel in *Vice City* |
| Watson Bay | Paradise Garage |
| Stockyard | Stock 305 streetwear and Electric Fang Tattoo, with tattoos by the art collective FAILE |

Insider Gaming's list compiled from promotional material adds Vice Beach, Downtown, SW 9th Ave, Port Vice City, VCI Airport, Starfish Island, the Rialto Islands, Hamlet and Peacock Bay <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar has not said which of these, Watson Bay and Stockyard included, sit inside city limits. The sorted master list is on [[world/gta-6-landmarks-and-neighborhoods|landmarks and neighborhoods]].

The district pairings are GTABase's analysis, not Rockstar's <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

Trailer 1 adds building-level parodies, per Den of Geek <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>: a Boardwalk Hotel standing in for Miami's Avalon, the Atlantic Hotel beside the three-round-window "Scarface building," and a seaplane towing a "Nine1Nine" banner, a parody of Miami's E11EVEN club. ## Who runs Vice City?

Three official bios put the city's music and nightlife economy here <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. [[characters/boobie-ike|Boobie Ike]] is "a local Vice City legend - and acts like it," with real estate, a strip club and a recording studio. His partner [[characters/dre-quan-priest|Dre'Quan Priest]] "sets his sights on the Vice City scene" after signing the [[characters/real-dimez|Real Dimez]] to their label, Only Raw Records.

The [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] adds the law and the money <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Méndez, a dirty VCPD cop, raids Raymond's drug house while [[characters/jason-duval|Jason]] and [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia]] are there for a pickup. Val, Andrés de León and Petra Navarro are tied to a security job at the opening of Megamundo's first US headquarters, a Spanish-language network; the job ends in an armed robbery.

## What has Rockstar shown of the city?

Trailer 1 (December 2023) showed the Ocean View Hotel, a "Vice Vinyl" sticker shop and recreations of real Florida viral clips <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Fan wikis place Pißwasser beer, the Post OP delivery brand and a "City National Bank"-style robbery in Trailer 2 <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>.

The August 27, 2026 Extended Look added Effluvia, a rooftop bar downtown, and a seedy stock market that may be BAWSAQ, per Engadget, whose writers also spotted new 3D buildings on the HUD map <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Shot-by-shot notes are on [[media/gta-6-trailer-1|Trailer 1]] and [[media/gta-6-trailer-2|Trailer 2]].

## How big is Vice City compared to Los Santos?

Rob Nelson reportedly told YouTuber TGG during the August 2026 Rockstar North preview that Vice City is about twice the size of Los Santos. TGG also claimed the city plus suburbs is 11 times Los Santos and its surroundings, which GTA Intel rates the least corroborated of the size claims <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Rockstar's whole-map comparisons (about 2x GTA V, 3x RDR2) are on [[world/gta-6-map-size|GTA 6 map size]].

## What Tommy Vercetti references are in Vice City?

Rockstar's store copy leans on the 2002 game <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>: the Ocean View Hotel and Shore Drive, the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers "sourced from the Vercetti Estate," a weapon pattern "inspired by Tommy Vercetti's iconic palm tree button-up," and Flash FM, "the Vice City institution," back on the dial with hosts Robyn and Alex. None of it puts Tommy in the game. That stays a <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>, covered on [[characters/returning-characters-and-cameos|returning characters and cameos]].

## Is Rockstar promoting Vice City in real Miami?

Yes. Wikipedia, citing IGN, NBC Miami and others, lists "Welcome to Vice City" signage, a Kaseya Center roof deal worth about $975,000, E11EVEN rebranded as "Nine1Nine" from November 6, 2026, and a Miami Heat "A Night in Vice City" game on November 18 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Miami-Dade's sheriff and a county commissioner objected; NBC Miami reports commissioners approved the plan on September 16, 2026. The rest of the state is on the [[world/index|world hub]].
