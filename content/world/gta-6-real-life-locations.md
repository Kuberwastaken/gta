GTA 6's state of **Leonida is Florida**, and every region has a recognizable real-world model: **Vice City is Miami, the Leonida Keys are the Florida Keys, Grassrivers is the Everglades, Port Gellhorn is Panama City, and Ambrosia is Clewiston on Lake Okeechobee**. The odd one out is Mount Kalaga, which looks more like the southern Appalachians than anything in Florida.

One caveat before the tables. Rockstar has not published any of these pairings. They come from press and community analysis, mainly GTABase and GTA BOOM, and both outlets say Rockstar compresses and blends places, so the matches are not one-to-one <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## Is the Seven Mile Bridge in GTA 6?

An analogue is. Trailer 1 shows a long bridge across the Keys that Den of Geek compares to the Seven Mile Bridge, and outlets tie the Leonida Keys to the Overseas Highway (US-1) <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar hasn't named the in-game bridge. More on [[world/leonida-keys|the Leonida Keys]].

## What is Lake Leonida in real life?

Lake Okeechobee, per press analysis <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar names Lake Leonida as the home of One-Eyed Willie's off-road mod shop <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. That places it in Ambrosia's sugar country, which GTABase and GTA BOOM match to Clewiston, "America's Sweetest Town". The Allied Crystal refinery is Rockstar's stand-in for the area's sugar industry.

Neither outlet cites Lakeland, despite some fan lists that do.

## Is Port Gellhorn Panama City?

Mostly. GTABase points to Panama City and Panama City Beach on the Panhandle Gulf Coast. GTA BOOM calls it a Gulf Coast composite rather than a single town <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar's description of "cheap motels, shut-down attractions, and empty strip malls" in a "once-popular vacation spot" fits either reading. Its raceway is compared to Sebring.

## Why is Mount Kalaga not in Florida?

Because it doesn't look like Florida. Rockstar describes Mount Kalaga as "a national landmark up against the state's northern border" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>, and outlets match it to southern Appalachia and the Blue Ridge: Providence Canyon in Georgia and Table Rock in North Carolina come up most <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Ocala National Forest, a common fan guess, isn't named by the outlets we checked.

## What real places do the leaked maps point to?

The map leaked in August 2026 names a **Gloriana Key**, an industrial island some have compared to Virginia Key <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Its five counties (Lummox, Kelly, Leonard, Vice-Dale and Mariana) don't map cleanly onto real Florida counties. See [[world/gta-6-leaked-map-and-counties|the leaked map and counties]].

## What about places outside Leonida?

- **Glorianna**: Trailer 2 shows license plates from this Georgia-style neighboring state, per Den of Geek <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.
- **Liberty City**: named in [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia's]] official bio as where her family came from, and seen on plates in Trailer 2 <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

## Are the car brands based on real makers?

By fan convention, yes: Grotti for Ferrari, Pegassi for Lamborghini or Pagani, Vapid for Ford, Declasse for Chevrolet and Bravado for Dodge. Rockstar has never said so <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Model-by-model details are on [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|GTA 6 vehicles]].

## How did Rockstar research Florida?

Wikipedia, citing Dazed, says Rockstar's field team researched Miami from 2015, and more than 50 street artists painted murals for Vice City neighborhoods <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar has not described its research for the rest of the state.

## What isn't officially named

A "Little Haiti"-style district has not been named; only Little Cuba is. No official business or place called "Kelly's" exists either; the nearest name is the leaked Kelly County. The [[world/index|world hub]] collects everything else we know about Leonida.
