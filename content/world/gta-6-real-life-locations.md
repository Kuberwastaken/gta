Leonida is Florida, and press analysis has a real-world model for every region: Vice City is Miami, the Leonida Keys are the Florida Keys, Grassrivers is the Everglades, Port Gellhorn is Panama City and Ambrosia is Clewiston on Lake Okeechobee. Mount Kalaga is the exception; it looks like the southern Appalachians.

Rockstar has published none of these pairings. They come mainly from GTABase (Oct 3, 2026) and GTA BOOM (Sep 18, 2026), and both say Rockstar compresses and blends places, so the matches are not one-to-one <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## How do the other regions line up?

The Keys: Trailer 1 shows a long bridge across open water that Den of Geek compares to the Seven Mile Bridge, and outlets tie the region to the Overseas Highway (US-1) <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar has not named the in-game bridge.

Ambrosia: Rockstar puts One-Eyed Willie's off-road mod shop at Lake Leonida <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>, which outlets read as Lake Okeechobee. That places Ambrosia in the sugar belt around Clewiston, "America's Sweetest Town," with the Allied Crystal refinery standing in for the real industry <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Neither GTABase nor GTA BOOM cites Lakeland, despite fan lists that do.

Port Gellhorn: GTABase says Panama City and Panama City Beach; GTA BOOM says a Gulf Coast composite. Rockstar's "cheap motels, shut-down attractions, and empty strip malls" in a "once-popular vacation spot" fits either <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

Mount Kalaga: Rockstar calls it "a national landmark up against the state's northern border" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Outlets look outside Florida, to the Blue Ridge, naming Providence Canyon in Georgia and Table Rock in North Carolina. Ocala National Forest, a common fan guess, is not named by the outlets we checked <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## Which real Florida moments are recreated in the trailers?

Den of Geek lists four recreations of viral Florida clips in Trailer 1 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>: an alligator wandering into a convenience store, the "Florida Joker," a man watering his lawn in a thong, and a woman twerking on a car. The same satire runs through the in-game social feed; see [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|the phone and social media]] and [[media/gta-6-easter-eggs-in-trailers|trailer easter eggs]].

## Has real Miami played along?

Yes. Per Wikipedia's citations of IGN, NBC Miami and GameSpot, Miami got "Welcome to Vice City" signage, a branded Kaseya Center roof (about $975,000), E11EVEN rebranded as "Nine1Nine" from Nov 6, 2026, and a Miami Heat "A Night in Vice City" game on Nov 18 <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. NBC Miami reports commissioners approved the plan on Sep 16, 2026, over pushback from Miami-Dade's sheriff and one commissioner.

## What about places that don't map to Florida?

The August 2026 leaked map has a Gloriana Key, an industrial island possibly modeled on Virginia Key, and five counties (Lummox, Kelly, Leonard, Vice-Dale, Mariana) that match no real Florida county names <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>; see [[world/gta-6-leaked-map-and-counties|the leaked map and counties]]. Trailer 2 shows plates from Glorianna, a Georgia-style neighbor, and Liberty City, where [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia's]] family came from <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

Car brands follow fan convention rather than anything Rockstar has said: Grotti for Ferrari, Pegassi for Lamborghini or Pagani, Vapid for Ford, Declasse for Chevrolet, Bravado for Dodge <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Models are on [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|GTA 6 vehicles]].

No "Little Haiti"-style district has been named; only Little Cuba is. No official business called "Kelly's" exists either. The rest of the state is on the [[world/index|world hub]].
