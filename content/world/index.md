Leonida is Rockstar's Florida, "home to the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and beyond." Rockstar profiles six regions on its "Only in Leonida" page: [[world/vice-city|Vice City]], the [[world/leonida-keys|Leonida Keys]], [[world/grassrivers|Grassrivers]], [[world/port-gellhorn|Port Gellhorn]], [[world/ambrosia|Ambrosia]] and [[world/mount-kalaga|Mount Kalaga]] <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. They went up with Trailer 2 on May 6, 2025, and Rockstar calls them "a few of the must-see destinations," not the whole state.

## How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar staff say about 2x GTA 5 and 3x RDR2's playable area, and Edge reports a landmass "nearly as large as" GTA 4, GTA 5 and RDR2 combined <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. There is no official km² figure. Fan measurements land between 83 km² and 125 km² of land, against 49.3 km² for GTA 5 <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. The math and the disagreements are on [[world/gta-6-map-size|GTA 6 map size]].

## What real places is Leonida based on?

Press analysis pairs Vice City with Miami, the Keys with the Florida Keys, Grassrivers with the Everglades, Port Gellhorn with Panama City and Ambrosia with Clewiston on Lake Okeechobee. Mount Kalaga is the outlier: it reads as southern Appalachia, not Florida <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Landmark-level matches, from the Avalon to the Scarface building, are on [[world/gta-6-real-life-locations|real-life locations]].

## Where do Jason and Lucia live?

[[characters/jason-duval|Jason]] works for drug runners in "the Keys" and lives rent-free at one of Brian Heder's properties. [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia]] is out of Leonida Penitentiary, and her family came from Liberty City <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. The rest of the cast and their home turf is on [[characters/index|the characters hub]].
