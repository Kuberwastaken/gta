GTA 6 is set in **Leonida**, Rockstar's version of Florida, "home to the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and beyond." Rockstar profiles **six regions**: [[world/vice-city|Vice City]], the [[world/leonida-keys|Leonida Keys]], [[world/grassrivers|Grassrivers]], [[world/port-gellhorn|Port Gellhorn]], [[world/ambrosia|Ambrosia]] and [[world/mount-kalaga|Mount Kalaga]] <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

This section covers the map, the places and the systems that make the world feel alive. Every claim is graded; leaks and fan estimates are kept apart from what Rockstar has said.

## How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar developers put it at **about twice GTA 5's map and three times RDR2's playable area**, and Edge reports the landmass is nearly GTA 4, GTA 5 and RDR2 combined <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar has published no square-km or square-mile figure. Fan estimates range from about 83 km² to 125 km² of land <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. The full breakdown is on [[world/gta-6-map-size|GTA 6 map size]].

## What real places is Leonida based on?

Outlets match Vice City to Miami, the Keys to the Florida Keys, Grassrivers to the Everglades, Port Gellhorn to Panama City and Ambrosia to Clewiston. Mount Kalaga looks like southern Appalachia <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[world/gta-6-real-life-locations|real-life locations]].

## Where do Jason and Lucia live?

[[characters/jason-duval|Jason]] works for drug runners in the Keys and lives at one of Brian Heder's properties. [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia]] is fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary, and her family came from Liberty City <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Their story stretches "across the state of Leonida." The rest of the cast is on [[characters/index|the characters hub]].
