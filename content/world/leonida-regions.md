Rockstar profiles six regions of Leonida on its "Only in Leonida" page, launched with Trailer 2 on May 6, 2025. It bills them as "a few of the must-see destinations across the sunshine state." That wording leaves room for more: the leaked map adds counties, keys and towns Rockstar has never named.

## What are the six regions in GTA 6?

The excerpts are Rockstar's <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. The real-world column comes from GTABase and GTA BOOM, which both say Rockstar compresses and blends places <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Each has its own page: [[world/vice-city|Vice City]], [[world/leonida-keys|Leonida Keys]], [[world/grassrivers|Grassrivers]], [[world/port-gellhorn|Port Gellhorn]], [[world/ambrosia|Ambrosia]] and [[world/mount-kalaga|Mount Kalaga]]. Landmark-level matching is on [[world/gta-6-real-life-locations|GTA 6 real-life locations]].

| Region | What Rockstar says (excerpt) | Real-world match (press) | Named in official copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vice City | "The glamour, hustle, and greed of America captured in a single city." | Miami | Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Tisha-Wocka flea market, VC Port |
| Leonida Keys | "Life in this tropical archipelago isn't flashy but it's easy." | Florida Keys | "Some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in all of America" |
| Grassrivers | "You never know what lies beneath the surface of this primordial expanse." | Everglades | Gators, mangroves, "far deadlier predators" |
| Port Gellhorn | "Cheap motels, shut-down attractions, and empty strip malls won't bring the tourists back..." | Panama City / Panama City Beach | Malt liquor, painkillers, dirt bikes |
| Ambrosia | "In the heart of Leonida, American industry and old school values still reign supreme." | Clewiston and Lake Okeechobee | Allied Crystal sugar refinery, "the local biker gang" |
| Mount Kalaga | "A national landmark up against the state's northern border..." | Southern Appalachia (not Florida) | Hunting, fishing, off-road trails, "paranoid radicals" |

Rockstar has published no map with the regions drawn on it. If the Florida matches hold, the Keys and Grassrivers sit at the southern tip, Vice City on the Atlantic side, Port Gellhorn on the Gulf, Ambrosia inland around Lake Leonida, and Mount Kalaga on the northern state line <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>.

## Are the regions the same as counties?

No. Rockstar calls the six "destinations." The CyberLeek map from August 2026 divides Leonida into five counties: Lummox (north), Kelly (west), Leonard (east), Vice-Dale (around Vice City) and Mariana (south and the wetlands) <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Earlier coverage mentioned an "Ambrosia County" that is not on that list. Both map leaks are graded on [[world/gta-6-leaked-map-and-counties|leaked map and counties]].

Rockstar has given no size for any single region. The only region-level number is Vice City at about 2x Los Santos, via YouTuber TGG <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Whole-map figures are on [[world/gta-6-map-size|GTA 6 map size]].

## What other places has Rockstar named?

Outside the six region blurbs, Rockstar's pages name Leonida Penitentiary, Lake Leonida (home of One-Eyed Willie's off-road mod shop, which outlets read as Lake Okeechobee), Ocean Beach, Shore Drive, Washington Beach, Gambit Bay, Watson Bay, Stockyard and Southside Vice City <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

Insider Gaming's list from promotional material adds Starfish Island, Rialto Islands, Draper Island, Tequesta Retreat, Waning Sands, Capri, Hamlet, Peacock Bay, Key Lento and VCI Airport <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Most are not pinned to a region. [[world/gta-6-landmarks-and-neighborhoods|Landmarks and neighborhoods]] sorts them.

## Is there anything outside Leonida?

Two other states are named, and neither is shown as playable. Liberty City is in Lucia's bio and on license plates in Trailer 2. Den of Geek also spotted plates from Glorianna, a Georgia-style neighbor, in the same trailer <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Mount Kalaga's "northern border" line implies a state line on the map. Engadget caught passing references to Blaine County and Los Santos in the [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

Rob Nelson told Famitsu: "The universes are separate, but we like looking back at previous titles in slightly clever ways." Rockstar has not said whether more destinations will be added to the official list after Nov 19. The rest of the state is on the [[world/index|world hub]].
