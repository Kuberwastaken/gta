The reported activity list for GTA 6 runs to 18 entries: fishing, hunting, scuba diving, skydiving, base-jumping, jet skis, kayaking, basketball, mini-golf, pool, backyard wrestling, shooting ranges, gyms, zoo visits, barbers, salons, tattoo parlors and circuit racing <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The list comes from Rockstar's August 2026 previews and Edge's October cover feature, as written up by Red Bull, VICE and GTAVice.net. Rockstar has not published its own list.

## Ambient activity is not a minigame count

In [TechRadar's November 20 direct interview](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/there-are-close-to-a-thousand-animation-sets-for-ambient-activities-how-rockstars-gameplay-animation-makes-gta-6-a-fully-living-and-breathing-world-from-breathtaking-foot-chases-to-details-like-how-characters-open-a-door), Rockstar animation VP **Mark Tennant** describes close to a thousand ambient animation sets, plus hundreds of bespoke one-off events and custom sequences. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (developer statement) These support the background world, including people at work and spending time together. They are **not a claim of a thousand playable side activities**, a quest total or a completion-time estimate. The [[gameplay/gta-6-gameplay-features-confirmed#movement-and-ambient-animation|movement and animation section]] separates those production details from the activity list below.

## Why might a place change when you return?

[Game8's November 23 direct interview](https://game8.co/articles/latest/gta-6-has-enough-secrets-and-content-for-years-to-come-rockstar-says) adds a different kind of side content to the activity list. **Ghyan Koehne, Rockstar's Senior Design Director of Exploration & Ambient Content**, describes discoveries with different conditions: some only need an observant first visit, while others need a return visit or a particular time of day. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (developer interview)

| Discovery pattern | Koehne's explanation | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First visit | Some content is present at one location for the player to notice | Not every visible detail starts a quest |
| Return or night visit | Some content appears on a later visit or at night | No universal timer or reset interval supplied |
| Evolving location | A place can tell its story over visits separated by days | Not a promise that every location changes daily |
| Linked discoveries | Investigating one place can lead to other places and encounters | No total count or complete route list supplied |

Koehne also distinguishes interactions with particular NPCs from discoveries that involve the environment. That is a useful reason not to treat **one quiet visit as a complete survey of a place**. It does not establish that waiting a fixed number of hours will trigger something, or that every closed door, background person or unusual object hides playable content.

The interview describes the team's aim of keeping discoveries available for months and potentially years. That is **an exploration-design goal**, not a measured completion time, a promise of years of unique missions, a live-service schedule or an announcement of future DLC. These discoveries are also separate from the animation-set production count above and the list of playable sports below.

This section leaves out the article's named secrets and encounter solutions. It covers how Rockstar describes discovery, without giving routes, rewards or story examples. ## What a released-game review and developer interview add

[GAMINGbible's November 18 review and Rob Nelson interview](https://www.gamingbible.com/reviews/gta-6-review-389961-20261117) add evidence beyond the preview activity list. Reviewer **Sam Cawley**, writing after more than 70 hours on a base PS5 with publisher-provided code, describes spending free time at gyms, the beach and a nightclub. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> That corroborates selected activities; it is not an independent check of every entry in the table or a 100-percent-completion run.

Nelson's explanation is about **activities and interiors having a purpose**. The team wanted interaction that adds to playing the characters, rather than extra buildings that are only scenery or routines that become chores. In the direct interview he gives physically putting on clothes and pouring engine coolant into a car as examples of interactions left out. <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> (developer statement in an interview)

This does **not** supply an exact enterable-building count, a percentage of the map with interiors, or proof that every visible business is usable. Nor does a reviewer's 70-hour playthrough settle the [[guides/gta-6-how-long-to-beat|campaign-length question]]. See the [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|vehicle guide]] for the separate firsthand driving observations.

## Can you play darts, bowling or full basketball matches?

**Darts and bowling are not playable launch activities**, according to [VGC's direct interview with Rob Nelson](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/rockstar-explains-why-you-cant-play-darts-in-gta-6-we-dont-have-unlimited-time/), published November 19. VGC identifies both omissions; Nelson explains that even Rockstar has finite time and resources, and that darts did not reach the team's standard. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> This does not mean they are announced for a later update or an online mode.

Nelson describes activities as extensions of the wider controls and physical simulation. He gives pool's physics and weightlifting's physical interaction as examples. Basketball has a specific limit: players can move around and shoot, but **cannot play full matches**. A basketball entry in the preview list above should not be read as a complete competitive basketball game.

This interview explains selected inclusions and omissions, not an exhaustive list of everything removed during development. It supplies no release schedule for additions. For other unannounced systems, keep the [[leaks/index|leaks ledger]] separate from the playable-activity list.

## Why were tennis and other planned interactions left out?

**November 25 interview:** [PC Games' Michael Grünwald spoke with Rob Nelson and Scott Mitchell](https://www.pcgames.de/GTA-6-Spiel-55239/News/Rob-Nelson-Interview-Features-Aktivitaeten-Rockstar-1561016/) during the outlet's review period. The German-language report says **tennis was planned**, but the implementation did not meet Rockstar's standard, while activities such as mini-golf made the finished game. This adds to the separate VGC explanation of omitted darts and bowling above. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> (direct interview account)

| Interaction discussed in PC Games | Reported production decision |
| --- | --- |
| Tennis minigame | Planned, but its implementation did not satisfy the team |
| Crawling, furnishing the protagonists' home and crafting items | Considered, then left out; the account says some ideas did not fit the story or gameplay |
| Cinema and theatre visits | Omitted because Rockstar did not consider them interactive enough |

These are our English paraphrases of the outlet's account, not verbatim English developer quotations. The report does not assign the same reason to every cut, give a complete removed-feature inventory, or say how far each idea reached in development. It also does not announce their return through a patch, DLC or an online mode. A considered home-furnishing system is not evidence of a working property-purchase system; see [[world/gta-6-interiors|interiors and property claims]].

Nelson also points to the protagonists' relationship as a feature the team values, and the article connects the quieter character moments to lessons from Red Dead Redemption 2. That explains the stated design approach, not a measured playtime or a promise that every activity changes the relationship. Our [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|relationship guide]] keeps the supported systems separate.

## Is casino gambling a playable activity?

Not according to [Kotaku's November 18 direct interview and released-game observations](https://kotaku.com/gta-6s-map-contains-areas-that-could-be-used-in-the-future-says-rockstar-2000756338). The reviewer says the large casinos he checked could not be entered, while Rockstar's Stephen Donkin says gambling did not fit this story or its economic balance. A visible casino is not evidence of a usable gambling minigame. <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>

Donkin's separate comment about leaving future map options open is not a gambling-update, expansion or online-mode announcement. The [[world/gta-6-interiors#can-you-gamble-in-the-casinos-and-does-spare-map-space-mean-dlc|interiors guide]] separates the developer's statement from the article's speculation. No claim here covers every building, a future release date or a tested money-making method.

## Can you fish, hunt and scuba dive?

Yes, all three. Rockstar told Game Informer there are over 170 animal species, tiered Rare, Discoverable and Legendary, which you can track and log in a checklist like RDR2's compendium <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rockstar's own copy names Gambit Bay as a fishing spot and sells [[world/mount-kalaga|Mount Kalaga]] on "prime hunting, fishing, and off-road trails" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Diving appears in the Extended Look, and Rockstar's Keys artwork shows divers on reefs; oxygen limits and underwater loot are unconfirmed. Species detail is on [[world/gta-6-wildlife-and-animals|wildlife and animals]], reef country on [[world/leonida-keys|Leonida Keys]].

## Do gyms and food change your character?

Gyms change physique, stamina and focus <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>, and Dazed reports that diet, exercise and sleep visibly change Jason's and Lucia's bodies <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. One system went further and was cut: a fridge you had to restock, dropped as too realistic, per Wikipedia citing Eurogamer and GamesRadar+ <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## Where can you change your look?

Rockstar's edition pages name three shops <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>: Electric Fang Tattoo in Stockyard, with designs by the artist collective FAILE; Stock 305 streetwear, also in Stockyard; and Sara's Unisex Salon. Appearance is also a stealth tool. A haircut or a change of clothes is one of the reported ways to shake a police description; see [[gameplay/gta-6-wanted-system-and-heat|the wanted system and heat]].

## Do activities affect Jason and Lucia's relationship?

Yes. Edge describes partner activities feeding a four-point closeness scale <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Rob Nelson told Edge the couple start out "wanting to be together... but as the game progresses, it's yours to keep or yours to lose" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Specific date lists (pool, mini-golf, kayaking) trace back to an X thread <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. More on [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|Jason and Lucia's relationship]].

## Are there side jobs?

RydeMe, an Uber parody in which passengers chat, is the one job-style app previews mention <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>; Rockstar hasn't said how it pays. Robbery is the other income: almost any shop can be hit. See [[gameplay/gta-6-phone-and-social-media|phone apps]] and [[gameplay/gta-6-money-banks-and-fences|money and fences]]. Dogs can be petted or scolded, per VICE, and owners can refuse you, per Red Bull <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## How long is GTA 6 with side content?

Rob Nelson reportedly said his own playthrough took about 80 hours; Red Bull frames that as a full run with optional content <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Other outlets read 80 hours as the story alone, with side content pushing past 100. Rockstar has given no official runtime, and the map opens gradually as you explore. See [[guides/gta-6-how-long-to-beat|how long to beat GTA 6]].

Surfing, an old rumour, and any business-ownership system remain unannounced <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>. Every graded system sits on the [[gameplay/gta-6-gameplay-features-confirmed|confirmed features list]] and the [[gameplay/index|gameplay hub]].
