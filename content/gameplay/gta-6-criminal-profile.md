GTA 6 keeps a Criminal Profile for each lead: a meter running from Professional at one end to Irredeemable at the other, marked with angel and devil icons. Edge's October 29 cover feature and Red Bull's gameplay roundup both report that it affects the story <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. That is the whole public record. Rockstar has not said what raises or lowers it.

Because it is per character, [[characters/jason-duval|Jason]] and [[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia]] can end up with different reputations depending on how you play each. You switch between them when the story allows; see [[gameplay/gta-6-character-switching|character switching]].

## Is it the same as the wanted level?

No. The wanted level is the police in the moment: six stars, witnesses, CCTV, your clothes and your car <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The Criminal Profile is the long-run record of what kind of criminal each lead is. Whether one feeds the other is not on the record. Police rules are on [[gameplay/gta-6-wanted-system-and-heat|the wanted system and heat]].

## What might move the meter?

Rockstar has not said, so this is our reading <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>. Combat reportedly supports non-lethal disarms through vitals targeting, and robberies reward care: blowing a safe is faster than finding or buying its code but may damage the valuables inside. "Professional" points to clean, low-collateral jobs and "Irredeemable" to the opposite. Bank robber Raul Bautista's official pull quote is "A professional adapts", which fits the label without being a stated link. Combat details: [[gameplay/gta-6-weapons|weapons]]. Robbery rules: [[gameplay/gta-6-money-banks-and-fences|money and fences]].

## Does it change the ending?

Not confirmed. Reports tie the profile to the story, not to the ending <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. Red Bull separately says Jason and Lucia's relationship "will shape the story's course and ending"; other outlets say Rockstar never said so, and we treat it as unconfirmed. Nelson's framing in Edge is that the plot follows what the leads want: "But what if these characters want something else?" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. More at [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|Jason and Lucia's relationship]].

## How does it fit the characters?

Rockstar's bios set up both ends of the meter. Lucia has "learned her lesson - only smart moves from here"; Jason "wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. A meter measuring how far each drifts from those intentions fits the writing, though Rockstar has not drawn the connection <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>.

Still unknown: how the profile is displayed, what moves it, and whether it opens or closes missions.
