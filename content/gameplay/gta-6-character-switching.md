**Yes, GTA 6 lets you switch between Jason and Lucia**, and you can do it in the middle of the action, such as swapping from the driver's seat to the passenger with a gun <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. But you **can't switch at any time**. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told Famitsu that "there are times when it's restricted depending on the progression of the story" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

So it works more like a story tool than a free-for-all: some missions let you swap freely, others lock you to one character.

## How does switching work in GTA 6?

Dazed, which spent time with Rockstar for its autumn 2026 issue, describes it like this <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>:

- Drive as **[[characters/jason-duval|Jason]]**, then swap to **[[characters/lucia-caminos|Lucia]]** in the passenger seat.
- Swap **mid-chase** between driving and shooting.

The [[media/gta-6-extended-look|Extended Look]] showed a **Character Wheel** swap mid-drive <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## Can you switch characters at any time?

This is where reports conflict.

| Source | Claim | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rob Nelson to Famitsu (via GTAVice.net) | Switching is "restricted depending on the progression of the story"; some missions allow free switching, some lock control | <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span> |
| Red Bull Extended Look recap | You can switch "at any time" | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |

We go with Nelson. He's a named Rockstar lead speaking on the record, and his answer is more specific. Red Bull's line most likely describes free-roam or the moments when switching is allowed <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>.

## Can Jason and Lucia play separately?

Yes. Nelson told IGN: "You can do that as Jason and Lucia together, or you can do it on your own" <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Dazed says players "can choose to either play as a couple, or go off and cause chaos independently."

That has a practical side. Splitting the pair up is one of the reported ways to shake a police description, since cops may be looking for two suspects <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[gameplay/gta-6-wanted-system-and-heat|the wanted system]].

## Does each character have separate stats?

Partly, per previews <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

- The **[[gameplay/gta-6-criminal-profile|Criminal Profile]]** is tracked per character.
- Each character has **multiple personal cars**.
- Gyms affect physique, stamina and focus <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>, and Dazed confirms diet, exercise and sleep visibly change both bodies <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

**Focus**, a slow-motion aiming state that marks weak points, was shown with Jason. Whether Lucia has it too is unclear <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[gameplay/gta-6-weapons|weapons and combat]].

## Why only two protagonists?

Rockstar considered four and rejected it. Nelson told Famitsu that more leads would mean less time with each, making emotional investment harder <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Leaked names "Billy and Kai" are linked to the four-protagonist talk but neither is confirmed <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[leaks/gta-6-four-protagonists-rumour|the four protagonists rumour]].

## Does switching affect the relationship?

Indirectly. The romance is optional and "yours to keep or yours to lose", Nelson told Edge, and partner activities build closeness <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Even if the romance fades, they stay partners in crime. Full detail at [[characters/jason-and-lucia-relationship|Jason and Lucia's relationship]].
