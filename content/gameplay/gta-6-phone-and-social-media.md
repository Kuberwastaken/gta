GTA 6's phone is built around parodies of real apps. Previews name five: **Snapmatic** for social media, **BuckMe** for banking, **Waink** for scanning cars, **RydeMe** for ride-hailing and **Scooter Bros** for vehicle delivery <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The twist on social media: **Jason and Lucia can't post publicly, because they're fugitives**.

Satire of social-media and influencer culture is one of the game's core themes, and Rockstar's own character bios lean on it <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>.

## What apps are on the GTA 6 phone?

| App | Parody of | What it does | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Snapmatic | Instagram / TikTok | Follow accounts, watch NPC events and livestreams in real time | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |
| BuckMe | Banking apps | Holds your bank balance | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |
| Waink | Car-value apps | Scans a car's security, tracker, registration cost and resale value | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |
| RydeMe | Uber | Ride-hailing; passengers chat | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |
| Scooter Bros | Delivery apps | Delivers a vehicle to you for a fee | <span class="tag-reported">reported</span> |

The "parody of" column for BuckMe, Waink and Scooter Bros is our gloss <span class="tag-speculation">speculation</span>; previews explicitly call Snapmatic an Instagram/TikTok parody and RydeMe an Uber parody.

**LifeInvader and Bleeter**, social networks from earlier games, are unconfirmed <span class="tag-rumour">rumour</span>.

## Can you post on social media in GTA 6?

Not publicly. Per Red Bull, Engadget and VICE <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>:

- Jason and Lucia **can't post publicly** on Snapmatic because they're fugitives.
- They **can DM photos to each other**.
- You can **follow accounts** and **watch NPC events and livestreams** as they happen in the world.

An optional livestream feed opens with R3, which outlets read as a nod to LifeInvader <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>.

## How does social media show up in the story?

Rockstar's character bios put it front and center <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>:

- The **[[characters/real-dimez|Real Dimez]]** built their career on "spicy rap tracks and a relentless social media presence." Dazed reports their journey "can be traced via in-game viral social media posts."
- **[[characters/cal-hampton|Cal Hampton]]** spends his time "snooping on Coast Guard comms with a few beers and some private browser tabs open."

Trailer 1 also recreated real Florida viral clips, like the alligator in a convenience store <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[media/gta-6-easter-eggs-in-trailers|trailer easter eggs]].

## What do the money and car apps do?

**BuckMe** holds your bank balance, which is safe when cash in hand is lost on Wasted or Busted <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[gameplay/gta-6-money-banks-and-fences|money, banks and fences]].

**Waink** tells you whether a car is worth stealing: its security tier, whether it has a tracker that could alert police, what it costs to register, and what it would sell for <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. See [[gameplay/gta-6-vehicles|vehicles]] and [[gameplay/gta-6-wanted-system-and-heat|the wanted system]].

## Can you listen to the radio on your phone?

Yes. Rockstar says radio and podcasts can be heard on foot via the phone, a series first <span class="tag-confirmed">confirmed</span>. Station details are on [[music/gta-6-radio-stations|radio stations]].

## What ads and products appear on screens?

Fictional products seen in ads include **Angstipan**, the **Prairie Sandwich** and **Pißwasser** beer, per VICE and Engadget <span class="tag-reported">reported</span>. The in-game TV show *Macca & Friends* premieres "exclusively on WEAZEL", per Insider Gaming; see [[characters/macca-the-gator|Macca the Gator]].

## What isn't known yet

Whether there's a phone camera mode beyond DMs, whether Snapmatic followers react to your crimes, and whether more apps unlock through the story. Rockstar hasn't said. The [[gameplay/index|gameplay hub]] has the rest.
