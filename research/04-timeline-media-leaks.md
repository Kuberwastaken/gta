# GTA VI: Development & Marketing Timeline, Trailers, Media, Leaks

Research date: 2026-11-15 (launch reported for 2026-11-19). Compiled for gta.kuber.studio.

## How to read this file

- **Status tags:** `[confirmed]` means an official Rockstar, Take-Two or platform-holder statement, or a court or record body, as relayed by the cited page. `[reported]` means mainstream press reporting that hasn't been officially confirmed. `[rumour]` means an unverified claim. `[debunked]` means a claim later shown false.
- **Verification marks:** **(F)** means I fetched the page and read it. **(S)** means I saw the page only as a search result, through the search tool's summary of that page. Several major outlets (VGC, IGN, GameSpot, Forbes, Variety, Bloomberg, BBC) blocked direct fetching. Their claims are cited **(S)** and should get a final spot-check before publishing.
- **Wikipedia:** The English Wikipedia article on GTA VI was read in full **(F)**. It is used as a cross-check, and the press source is cited wherever one was seen.

## 6. 2022 leak & the Arion Kurtaj case

1. **18 Sep 2022.** "teapotuberhacker" posts 90+ clips (about 50 min) to GTAForums and claims the source code too. The same actor claimed the Uber breach. [confirmed] The Record (S); Wikipedia (F)
2. **19 Sep 2022.** Rockstar confirms the "network intrusion". [confirmed] The Record (S)
3. **22 Sep 2022.** A 17-year-old is arrested in Oxfordshire. [confirmed] Wikipedia (F)
4. **Aug 2023.** A Southwark Crown Court jury finds Kurtaj did the acts behind 12 offences (Lapsus$ hacks including Rockstar, Uber, Nvidia, BT/EE). He is unfit to stand trial, so there is no conviction. [confirmed] Game Rant 2026-10-09 (F); IGN/Yahoo 2026-07-16 (F)
5. **21 Dec 2023.** Indefinite hospital order. The judge cited his continued intent to commit cybercrime. A 17-year-old accomplice gets an 18-month youth rehabilitation order. Rockstar put its cost at about $5M. [confirmed] TBS News (S); TechRadar (S); Wikipedia (F)
6. **Later.** The Court of Appeal rules that evidence of six earlier offences shouldn't have been admitted, but leaves the findings intact. [reported] Game Rant (F)
7. **Jul 2026.** He is judged fit to plead and moved to prison to await retrial (BBC's Joe Tidy). [reported] IGN/Yahoo (F)
8. **9 Nov 2026.** Retrial listed at Woolwich Crown Court. **No outcome had been reported as of 15 Nov 2026.** [reported] Game Rant (F)

## 7. Rockstar staff, labour & studio info

- **Dan Houser** (co-founder and lead writer of GTA III through RDR2) took an extended break in 2019, had his resignation announced in Feb 2020, and left on 11 Mar 2020. He founded Absurd Ventures (registered Feb 2021, announced Jun 2023). He says he wrote none of GTA VI. GTA VI is the first mainline GTA story since 1997 without him. [confirmed] Wikipedia (Dan Houser) (F); PC Gamer (F, headline)
- **Sam Houser** remains president and is described by a GTA VI lead (GamesRadar+, 2 Nov 2026, per Wikipedia citations) as involved in every aspect of creative development. He signed the 8 Nov 2023 trailer announcement. [confirmed] Gematsu (S); Wikipedia (F)
- **Studios:** Rockstar North (Edinburgh, lead), San Diego, Los Angeles (NPC-focused, founded 2023), Toronto and others. Headcount grew from 2,441 to 4,711 across 12 offices during development. [reported] Wikipedia (F)
- **Public-facing leads in 2026:** Rob Nelson and Aaron Garbut (Rockstar North co-studio heads), Rupert Humphries (narrative; co-writer of the Extended Look), Jennifer Kolbe (head of publishing). [confirmed] Wikipedia Extended Look (F); GameSpot (S); The Register (F)
- **Return to office:** five days a week from April 2024. [reported] Wikipedia (F)
- **Oct 2025 firings and IWGB:**
  - 31 UK staff fired on 30 Oct 2025 (about 34 per Wikipedia, 31 North + 3 Toronto). Rockstar alleges gross misconduct and leaks in a Discord. The IWGB says the server was a private union channel and files claims for victimisation and collective dismissal. [confirmed / disputed]
  - Interim pay relief was denied on 12 Jan 2026 (Bloomberg, (S)).
  - The Glasgow tribunal started about 10 Sep 2026. Rockstar failed to strike the blacklisting allegations. Closing submissions are 17–18 Nov 2026.
  - A recognition ballot of about 1,600 staff runs 12–26 Nov 2026. Kolbe: "if they want a union, they will have a union." If it passes, it would be the UK games industry's largest recognised union.
  - Sources: The Register 2026-11-12 (F); GamesRadar+ (S); VGC (S)
- **2026 workplace items** (per Wikipedia citations, not independently fetched): Game Developer (1 Jul 2026) reports pay-inequity and crunch claims. IGN (11 Sep 2026) reports privacy film installed after drones photographed office windows. [reported]
