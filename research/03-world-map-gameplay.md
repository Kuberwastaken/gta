Research date: 2026-11-15 (launch reported for 2026-11-19, PS5 / Xbox Series X|S).

## 1. The state of Leonida: official regions

- [confirmed] Leonida is the game's Florida-style state, "Home to the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and beyond". The places section is titled "Tour a few of the must-see destinations across the sunshine state." Rockstar's own merch calls it "the Goodtime State". (Rockstar Games, "Only in Leonida" and VI homepage, read 2026-11-15, https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida)
- [confirmed] Six destinations are profiled officially: **Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Mount Kalaga (National Park)**. They first appeared with Trailer 2 on 6 May 2025. (Rockstar "Only in Leonida". Den of Geek, Matthew Byrd, "GTA 6 Trailer 2 Breakdown", 2025-05-06, https://www.denofgeek.com/?p=975280)

## 3. Map size

- [reported, Rockstar via press] The map is about **3× the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2**. Rockstar told the *New York Times* (Christopher Byrd, 27 Aug 2026), as cited by Wikipedia and by GTA Intel ("How Big Is GTA 6's Map?", 2026-08-27, updated 2026-09-29, https://gtaintel.com/news/gta-6-map-size-explained).
- [reported, Rockstar via press] About **2× GTA V**. Rob Nelson gave the figure to YouTuber TGG during the Aug 2026 Rockstar North preview (relayed by Kotaku, IGN and PC Gamer), and Aaron Garbut repeated it in Game Informer's Sept 2026 cover story. (GTA Intel, as above. TimeSaver, "GTA 6 Map Size: 2x GTA 5, 3x RDR2...", read 2026-11-15, https://timesaver.gg/blog/gta-6-map-size-vs-gta-5-confirmed-2026)
- [reported, Rockstar via Edge] "Its total landmass, including Vice City and the surrounding areas, is nearly as large as the playable maps" of GTA IV, GTA V and RDR2 combined. Edge cover feature by Simon Parkin, 29 Oct 2026, summarised by GTAVice.net ("GTA VI Edge Preview Reveals Huge New Details", 2026-10-29, https://www.gtavice.net/news/gta-vi-edge-preview-reveals-huge-new-details-on-gameplay-police-relationships-and-more).
- [rumour] Vice City is **2× Los Santos** (Nelson to TGG), and Vice City plus suburbs is **11× Los Santos and surroundings** (TGG). GTA Intel calls the 11× figure the least corroborated. A VICE roundup instead says "populated space nearly 11 times GTA 5's". The wording differs between outlets.
- [confirmed] Rockstar has published **no square-km or square-mile figure**. IGN's 27 Aug 2026 piece quotes Rockstar calling it Rockstar's most complex world and a "very big" map. (VICE, Brent Koepp, "150 New GTA 6 Details Revealed...", 2026-08-27, https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-new-details-netflix-extended-look-previews/)
- [rumour] **GTA 6 Mapping Project**, the community reconstruction now co-run with GTA Wiki, estimates about **125 km² of land (about 2.55–2.6× GTA V land)**. GTA Wiki said a "final version" would release in November, ahead of launch. Critics note that its outline leans on the unverified leaked map. (TimeSaver, as above. Sportskeeda, "GTA 6 mapping project hints at a 2.55x bigger map than GTA 5", https://sportskeeda.com/gta/news-gta-6-mapping-project-hints-2-55x-bigger-map-gta-5-report, seen in search results only)
- [rumour] A Reddit measurement of the leaked map gives about **83.2 km² explorable land**, which would be about 1.69× GTA V (49.3 km² land) and 2.32× RDR2 (35.9 km²). It excludes water and possibly comes from an old build. (VICE, Brent Koepp, "GTA 6 Map Size Leak Reveals How It Compares to RDR2 and GTA 5", 2026-08-25, https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-map-leak-size-comparison-rdr2-gta-5/)
- [rumour] GTABase's map hub estimates "1.7x bigger than GTA V's map". (https://gtabase.com/gta-6/map/, undated)
- **Contradiction:** "3× RDR2" and "2× GTA V" only agree if RDR2's map is measured small (about 36 km² land). With the high RDR2 figure (75 km²) they are inconsistent. Rockstar has not said whether water counts. (TimeSaver analysis)
- [reported] The map unlocks gradually as you explore, and a full playthrough with optional content runs about 80 hours (Rob Nelson). (Red Bull, Phil Briel, "GTA 6 gameplay: Key details that you might have missed", updated 2026-10-20, https://www.redbull.com/us-en/gta-6-gameplay-details)

### Interiors, density, underwater

- [reported] Aaron Garbut told Edge the game has "an order of magnitude more interiors" than its predecessors, furnished to reflect their occupants. Roads were narrowed versus GTA V, and day/night cycles are longer, with extended sunsets. (GTAVice.net summarising Edge, 2026-10-29. TimeSaver)
- [reported] Many buildings have multiple exits for escapes. (Red Bull, 2026-10-20)
- [confirmed/reported] **Scuba diving** is an activity: diving is shown in the Extended Look and Rockstar's Keys art shows divers on reefs. Kayaking and jet skiing are also listed. How diving works in detail (oxygen, loot) is unconfirmed. (Wikipedia citing IGN, Weber, 27 Aug 2026. Red Bull, 2026-10-20)

## 4. Wildlife

- [reported, Rockstar to Game Informer] **Over 170 animal species** "across land, air, and sea", including some never seen in a Rockstar game. Named natives are flamingos, manatees, pelicans and alligators. Named invasives are green iguanas, cane toads, feral cats and rhesus macaques. Animals are tiered as Rare, Discoverable and Legendary and can be tracked. New fur and feather systems add iridescence, bioluminescence and translucency. (Game Informer, Marcus Stewart, "Exclusive: Grand Theft Auto VI Includes Over 170 Animal Species", 2026-09-29, https://gameinformer.com/exclusive/2026/09/29/exclusive-grand-theft-auto-vi-includes-over-170-animal-species)
- [rumour] Breakdown of 60+ bird species and 30+ mammals (Sportskeeda and others via search snippets, not verified on the page).
- [confirmed] Trailer 1 shows dolphins and a shark, and an alligator in a convenience store (a real Florida viral moment). (Den of Geek, 2023-12-05)
- [reported] Hunting and fishing are both confirmed activities (Red Bull). Boars and deer appear in an in-game TV ad (Engadget). Animals can be examined and logged in a checklist like RDR2's compendium, and dogs can be petted or scolded (VICE, 2026-08-27). Owners can refuse to let you pet their dog (Red Bull).

## 5. Weather

- [reported, Rockstar to Game Informer] **Tropical storms and seasonal hurricanes** are confirmed. Storms are localised (you can be in sunshine while rain falls in the distance), puddles and wet surfaces persist after rain, cloud formations move across the map, and rainbows appear in the right light. Frequency and gameplay effects were not disclosed. (Game Informer, Marcus Stewart, "Exclusive: Grand Theft Auto VI Features Tropical Storms And Hurricanes", 2026-09-29, https://gameinformer.com/exclusive/2026/09/29/exclusive-grand-theft-auto-vi-features-tropical-storms-and-hurricanes)
- Status change: hurricanes were a long-running **[rumour]**. Since 29 Sep 2026 they are confirmed by Rockstar via Game Informer. Older claims about floods and surfing physics remain unconfirmed.

## 6. Vehicles

- [confirmed] Named on Rockstar's edition pages: **'95 Grotti Cheetah** (Ultimate), **'55 Vapid Stanier** (Vintage Vice City pre-order pack), **Vapid Ganado** (Jason's low-riding pickup), **Dinka Enduro** motorcycle, **Crest Kayak**, **Shitzu Squalo** boat (docked at Washington Beach), **'67 Vapid Dominator Buggy** ("Mud Club monster"), plus a gold donk/"hi-riser" from Rideout Customs. The cover art shows a helicopter, a speedboat and a yellow scissor-door supercar. A Pegassi dealership is seen behind the Cheetah. (Rockstar VI homepage, read 2026-11-15)
- [reported] Six cars Rockstar showcased in November 2026 screenshots: Grotti Cheetah, Albany Buccaneer, Progen Emerus, Invetero Coquette, Albany Manana, Pegassi Tempesta. Trailer IDs from fans include the Pegassi Zorrusso, Pfister Astron, Buckingham Maverick, Frogger, Supervolito and Volatus helicopters, an Aeromotive blimp, and the Shitzu Tropic. No official count exists, and fan databases disagree (one lists 176). (Search snippets from GTABase, GTA Online Wiki, TimeSaver and Daily Game Score. Treat individual model IDs as **[rumour]** unless Rockstar named them)
- [reported] Trailer 1 vehicles per Den of Geek: Cheetah, Vapid Stanier, Bravado Buffalo, Imponte Ruiner/Runner, Karin Futo, Bravado Banshee, Pegassi Speeder, Dinka Verus, Gauntlet Hellfire, Sabre Turbo, Comet, Maverick, Dodo, Sea Sparrow, Obey Tailgater and others. These are fan IDs. (Den of Geek, 2023-12-05)
- [reported] Vehicle systems: multiple personal cars per character and garages. Trunks store weapons, outfits and even people. Vehicles have security tiers and theft needs tools (hot-wiring, key cloners/duplicators, lock picks, window smashing that triggers alarms, or quick-time-event hijacks). Trackers can alert police. Stolen cars can be registered for a fee. The **Waink** app scans a car's security, tracker, registration cost and resale value. Pay 'n' Spray repairs cars and clears your wanted level. Fuel gauges and refuelling (hold the button for about 10 seconds) return, as does engine health. E-scooters are in. **Scooter Bros** delivers a vehicle for a fee. Circuit racing is new to the series. Rockstar says handling blends GTA IV's weight with GTA V's accessibility. (Red Bull, 2026-10-20. GTAVice.net/Edge, 2026-10-29. VICE, 2026-08-27)
- Brand-to-real-maker pairings (Grotti = Ferrari, Pegassi = Lamborghini/Pagani, Vapid = Ford, Declasse = Chevrolet, Bravado = Dodge) are fan convention and not Rockstar statements. **[rumour]**

## 7. Weapons and combat

- [confirmed] Named on Rockstar's edition pages: Jason's **Girardi ES9** pistol, Lucia's **Klose K17** pistol, and the **Hawk & Little Morgan** revolvers ("sourced from the Vercetti Estate"). Rockstar also offers a tropical weapon pattern "inspired by Tommy Vercetti's iconic palm tree button-up". (Rockstar VI homepage)
- [reported] **Duke 556** assault rifle shown in the Extended Look. (Engadget, 2026-08-28)
- [reported] Loadout replaces the weapon wheel: **two handguns + two long guns**, only one long gun on the back, the rest stored in the vehicle (like RDR2's horse storage). A visibly carried weapon changes NPC reactions without automatically drawing police. Auto-aim is off by default. A "Focus" slow-motion mode highlights weak points. Vitals targeting allows non-lethal disarms. Recoil and weight are heavier. Police cars can be looted. (Red Bull, 2026-10-20. Engadget, 2026-08-28. GTAVice.net/Edge. Wikipedia)

## 8. Police / wanted system

- [reported] **Six wanted stars** shown in the Extended Look, up from GTA V's five. (Engadget, 2026-08-28. VICE, 2026-08-27)
- [reported] Crimes only draw police when there is a witness, an alarm, or a stolen-car tracker. A "heat" system tracks what police know: CCTV, appearance and clothing, number of suspects, weapons and vehicle. Star styles (solid, flashing, hollow/outlined, red) signal visual contact, lost contact, an unidentified suspect, or being inside the search zone. You can shake a description by splitting up Jason and Lucia, changing clothes, getting a barber haircut, wearing a mask against CCTV, or swapping or torching the car. Police may keep a perimeter at a robbed shop. Police helicopters use searchlights and police dogs appear. Phil Hooker (Rockstar) says police won't search a bus unless they saw you board it. NOOSE (from GTA IV) is referenced. (Red Bull, 2026-10-20. GTAVice.net/Edge, 2026-10-29. Engadget, 2026-08-28)
- [reported] **Criminal Profile**, tracked per character, ranges from Professional to Irredeemable (angel and devil icons) and affects the story. (GTAVice.net/Edge. Red Bull)
- [debunked] Claims that GTA VI "eliminates the wanted star system" (fan site leonidaexplorer). Contradicted by the official Extended Look footage, which shows stars.

## 9. Activities, economy, phone and social media

- [reported] Activities: basketball, kayaking, mini-golf, pool/billiards, scuba diving, skydiving/parachuting, base-jumping, jet ski, fishing, hunting, backyard wrestling, shooting ranges, gyms (which change physique, stamina and focus), zoo visits, barbers, salons, tattoo parlours and circuit racing. Partner activities build the relationship (Edge describes a four-point closeness scale). (Red Bull. VICE, 2026-08-27. GTAVice.net/Edge)
- [reported] Weight and appearance change with diet, exercise and sleep. A fridge-restocking system was **cut** as too realistic. (Wikipedia citing Eurogamer, 27 Aug 2026, and GamesRadar+, George Young, 30 Aug 2026)
- [reported] Economy: three money types, **bank (BuckMe app), cash, and duffel-bag loot**. Cash is lost on Wasted/Busted, so use ATMs. Loot must be fenced, and fences specialise and unlock through the story. Almost any shop can be robbed, armored vans included. Safe codes can be found or bought, or the safe can be blown (faster, but may damage valuables). Some story beats require a minimum cash amount. (Red Bull. VICE)
- [reported] Phone apps: **Snapmatic** (Instagram/TikTok parody; follow accounts, watch NPC events and livestreams in real time; Jason and Lucia cannot post publicly because they are fugitives, but can DM photos to each other), **BuckMe** (banking), **Waink** (cars), **RydeMe** (Uber parody; passengers chat), **Scooter Bros** (vehicle delivery). An optional livestream feed opens with R3, read as a Lifeinvader nod. LifeInvader and Bleeter are unconfirmed. (Red Bull. Engadget. VICE. GTAVice.net)
- [confirmed] Satire of social-media and influencer culture is a core theme. Character bios: the Real Dimez use "a relentless social media presence", and Cal browses "private browser tabs" while snooping on Coast Guard comms. (Rockstar "Only in Leonida")
- [reported] Fictional products in ads: Angstipan, the Prairie Sandwich, and Pißwasser beer. (VICE, 2026-08-27. Engadget)

## Contradictions summary
1. Map size: Rockstar's "2× GTA V" vs the Mapping Project's 2.55–2.6× vs leak measurements of 1.69× (land only) vs "nearly GTA IV + V + RDR2 combined". The ratios conflict depending on whether water counts and how RDR2 is measured.
2. "Vice City 2× Los Santos" vs "11× Los Santos and surroundings", both via TGG and not confirmed.
3. Counties: the leaked Lummox / Kelly / Leonard / Vice-Dale / Mariana list vs earlier "Ambrosia County" mentions. Collector's poster readings (Moreau, Manzana) may be misreads.
4. Radio count: six revealed vs Edge's "23 stations". The list of returning stations (V-Rock, Emotion 98.3, VCFM) is not on Rockstar's page.
5. Collector's Box price: £350 vs $399.99, which may be regional.
6. Port Gellhorn counterpart: Panama City (GTABase) vs "Gulf Coast composite" (GTA BOOM). Mount Kalaga is outside Florida in most analyses.
