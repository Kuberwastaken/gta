# 01 — Launch Logistics & Buying Guide (GTA VI)

Research snapshot: **2026-11-15** (four days before launch). Compiled from web searches and fetched pages.

**Confidence tags**
- **confirmed**: first-party source (Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony, Microsoft, or a ratings board).
- **reported**: a credible outlet.
- **rumour**: leakers or unverified claims.

**Method notes**
- Rockstar Newswire and Support pages render with JavaScript, and fetches either timed out or returned only the title. Rockstar's exact wording therefore usually comes through outlets that quote it, and each bullet says when that is the case.
- Several outlets returned 403 when fetched: Push Square, Forbes, TweakTown, VideoCardz and Technology.org. Bullets that rely on those outlets are marked "(search-result snippet)", meaning the fact was seen in a search result and not on the fetched page.

## 2. Unlock times, pre-load, file size, day-one patch

### Unlock / release time
- **Rockstar's rule:** GTA VI "will release midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions", on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. [confidence: confirmed (Rockstar Support wording, quoted by third parties; the Rockstar page timed out when fetched)]
  - Rockstar page: "Grand Theft Auto VI regional pre-load and release times on PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S", Rockstar Support (updated Nov 3, 2026 per citing pages), https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s
  - Quoted by:
    - "GTA 6 Release Time Confirmed", Timesaver.gg, updated Nov 5, 2026, https://timesaver.gg/blog/gta-6-release-time-unlock-countdown-2026
    - "GTA 6 Release Time: Midnight Local, Rockstar Confirms", gtasix.org, verified Nov 13, 2026, https://gtasix.org/en/guide/launch-time/
- **"Local" means the store region, not your own time zone.** PS Store data gives one timestamp per country or store region (about 15 distinct windows), all in UTC: [confidence: reported (store data compiled by fan sites)]
  - New Zealand: 11:00 on Nov 18 (first in the world)
  - Australia (Sydney): 13:00 on Nov 18

  - Mexico and Central America: 06:00 on Nov 19

- **US time zones:** The whole US store unlocks at midnight ET, so the rest of the country starts on the evening of Wednesday, Nov 18:
  - Central: 11 PM
  - Mountain: 10 PM
  - Pacific: 9 PM
  - Hawaii: about 7 PM (if the single ET timestamp applies there)

## 4. Platforms and performance

- **Launch platforms:** PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PS4 or Xbox One native version, though Xbox One can play via cloud (below). Rockstar's site says "Plays Best On PS5". [confidence: confirmed]
  - Sources: rockstargames.com/VI (fetched); Xbox Wire
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. There is no 60 fps or 40 fps mode and no performance toggle at launch. [confidence: confirmed (Rockstar Pre-Load Newswire, Nov 10, 2026, as reported)]
  - Sources:
    - GTA BOOM, Nov 10, 2026 (fetched)
    - "GTA 6 PS5 Pro enhancements revealed", The Shortcut (Adam Vjestica), Nov 10, 2026, https://www.theshortcut.com/p/gta-6-ps5-pro-enhancements-revealed
    - "Rockstar reveals GTA 6 console performance targets and PS5 Pro upgrades", Notebookcheck, Nov 10, 2026, https://www.notebookcheck.net/Rockstar-reveals-GTA-6-console-performance-targets-and-PS5-Pro-upgrades.1447121.0.html
  - **Contradiction:** gta6-life.com shows a 60 fps "Performance" mode table. An older Rock and Borys podcast rumour claimed 30 and 60 fps modes. Both conflict with Rockstar's statement.
- **PS5 Pro extras:** PSSR upscaling and **ray-traced shadows**, which Rockstar describes as "only available on PlayStation 5 Pro". [confidence: confirmed (as reported)]
  - Rockstar has not given internal resolutions for any console.
  - Sources: The Shortcut; GTA BOOM; "Rockstar confirms GTA 6 targets 30 FPS on PS5 Pro with PSSR and ray-traced shadows", TweakTown, https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114598/rockstar-confirms-gta-6-targets-30-fps-on-ps5-pro-with-pssr-and-ray-traced-shadows/index.html (search snippet)
  - **Contradiction:** an earlier ComicBook.com "PlayStation leak" claimed ray-traced reflections, shadows and lighting. The official list names shadows only.
- **Base PS5 extras:** DualSense haptics, Tempest 3D Audio and faster loading. All marketing footage was captured on a base PS5, not a Pro. [confidence: confirmed (as reported)]
  - Sources: GTA BOOM; VICE, Oct 15
- **Datamined hints (unconfirmed):** 120 Hz output and PSSR v13.60 hint at a possible future 40 fps mode. [confidence: rumour]
  - Source: Notebookcheck
- **Xbox Series S:** confirmed to run the game natively. Xbox Wire calls it "the most affordable way to play natively". The 30 fps target applies, but resolution has not been disclosed. Figures such as "720p–900p" are guesses. [confidence: confirmed (native support, 30 fps); rumour (resolution)]
  - Sources: Xbox Wire; Notebookcheck
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming at launch:** [confidence: confirmed (Microsoft)]
  - Day one (Nov 19, from 1 AM ET), on **Xbox One** and Xbox Series X|S.
  - The cloud version streams at Series S settings.
  - You must buy the game; it is not in Game Pass.
